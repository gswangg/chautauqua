# Multi-portal: build decisions

Every place the build corrects the mock, fills a gap it left open, or makes a call it didn't. The mock is `Chautauqua Portals.dc.html` in this folder; the brief is `docs/design/briefs/multi-portal-brief.md`. The brief's participation model is fixed and is followed as written.

## Model and engine

| # | Area | Decision | Why |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Design files | The Portals frame lives in `docs/design/multi-portal/`, not beside the v12 frames. | The v12 campaign's pack-audit scans enumerate `docs/design/*.dc.html` and pin exact populations. This pack is verified by its own browser probe instead. |
| 2 | Participations | Three kinds, keyed `p:<participantId>` (a role on a session), `c:<sponsorContactId>` (membership of a sponsor company), `r:<eventRoleId>` (an event role with no session or company: Press, Volunteer, Host, Staff). | The mock lists "Press · no session" and a Volunteers portal, which neither a session nor a company can express. |
| 3 | Lifecycle | A declined participation leaves every portal. An invited one is matched but inactive: it assigns nothing until accepted, and its home lists what acceptance brings, with each item's deadline. | The brief's model; "what acceptance brings" is the invitation page's "If you accept, we'll ask for". |
| 4 | Rules | An OR of AND groups. Ops: is, is not, is any of, is none of, is empty, is not empty. A field a participation lacks (a press role has no session format) counts as empty, so "is not Workshop" matches it. An empty rule matches nobody. | The negative-conditions gap; "missing counts as empty" is the only reading where "is not" and "is empty" agree. |
| 5 | Coverage | Coverage and "not in any portal" are measured over people on accepted sessions (invited ones included), sponsor contacts and event roles. Sessions in review and declined sessions are not gaps. | The mock's not-in-portal list includes "Session still in review" and "Session declined". Nobody should get a portal for those, and counting them makes coverage permanently short. |
| 6 | Items | Portal items materialize as `task_assignment` rows with `source = portal` and a `scope_key` (empty for person items, the participation key otherwise). Existing tasks and assignments stay `source = direct`, with today's semantics. | The Speakers grid, onboarding defaults and reminders keep working unchanged. The unique key becomes (task, contact, scope). |
| 7 | Merging | The same item from two portals for the same scope shows once, with the earliest deadline, required if either requires it, the longer grace, view-only if either says so, and editable after submitting if either allows it. The person record names both portals and which one set the deadline. | The brief's merge rules. The last two settings are gap items with no stated merge, so the strictest reading is used for locks and the most generous for allowances. |
| 8 | Lost portals | Losing a portal detaches its rows (`detached_at`) and never deletes them: the item leaves the home, and finished work stays on the person's record. Regaining the portal re-attaches the same row. | "Finished work kept when someone loses a portal". |
| 9 | Field permissions | A portal that names a field decides it outright; among portals naming it, the strictest level wins. Fields no portal names keep today's defaults. Phone and email default to hidden, and email is never editable. The lock line names the portal and participation that caused it ("View only, from Invited speakers"). | Keeps today's profile page exactly as it is (no phone or email fields there today) while a portal can still open a field up. |
| 10 | Capabilities | Additive: an action is allowed if any covering portal allows it. A participation no portal covers keeps today's abilities (edit the submission, add co-presenters). | A new portal must never silently take today's abilities away from someone it doesn't cover. |
| 11 | System items | Accept your invitation, Design your sessions, Bio and Headshot are built-in library items, derived on read: Bio and Headshot are done when the profile field is filled. | The mock lists Bio and Headshot as items. Storing a second copy of "is the bio filled" would drift from the profile. |
| 12 | Reconcile | Memberships and assignments are reconciled after every write that can change a match, in the background, every 15 minutes from the cron, and on the home itself when it notices an item with no row yet. | Counts are live ("without anyone touching a portal"), and a missed background run must not leave someone without an item. |
| 13 | Migration | Every event gets a Profile basics portal covering accepted sessions and active sponsorships, keeping today's field permissions and abilities, with no items. Its note is the event's welcome note (see 30). Resources stay visible to everyone with a portal by default; a resource can be limited to the portals that list it. | "Existing speakers see exactly what they see today after the migration." |
| 14 | Submission origin | `submission.origin` (cfp, organizer, sponsor, import) is backfilled: import where an external reference is set, organizer where there is no form, cfp otherwise. | The "How the session arrived" condition needs it for sessions that predate it. |
| 15 | Audit | `audit_event` is append-only, reusable app-wide (actor, action, target, subject, scalar details). Written by preview-as-participant, portal create, update, duplicate, delete and copy, tier and sponsorship changes, and sponsor-session submit, request changes, approve and decline. Deleting a contact nulls their id on audit rows and keeps the rows. | Who looked at whose data, and who changed what, has to survive. A deleted person's id must not. |

## Gaps the brief named

| # | Area | Decision | Why |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 16 | Notifications | Saving a portal change whose preview shows people gaining items offers "Email the N people who get new items" (defaulting to the portal's own setting). People are emailed once per save with every item they gained, through a notification queue. Reminders cover portal items, including relative deadlines. | The brief: the preview says who'll be emailed and lets the organizer choose. One email per person per save, not one per item. |
| 17 | Copy from another event | Portals (with their items, recreating library tasks the target lacks) and sponsor tiers can be copied from another event in the org. A name that already exists is skipped and reported. Fixed-date deadlines are copied as "no deadline"; relative ones are kept. | Last year's dates are wrong this year, and silently keeping them would set deadlines in the past. |
| 18 | Duplicate and delete | A duplicate copies items, permissions and note with an empty rule (it matches nobody until edited). Delete detaches the portal's rows, and finished work stays on records. | A duplicate with the same rule would double every person's items the moment it saves. |
| 19 | Item settings | Per portal: display name, deadline (none, a fixed day, or N days after joining), required, grace days, allow edits after submitting, view-only once done. Help text per profile field. | The brief's list. Relative deadlines count from the day the participation joined the portal (or accepted its invitation). |
| 20 | Share kit | A library item type for speaker and sponsor promotion: the organizer writes the text with `{name}`, `{event}`, `{session}` and `{link}` placeholders, and the participant gets it filled in with LinkedIn, Bluesky and X share links and "Mark as shared". Portal-only: it can't be assigned directly from the Speakers page. | The brief's gap item. Filling the text per person and session is what makes it worth more than a shared doc. |
| 21 | Reopen | An organizer reopens a closed item for one person until a day they choose, from the person record. | Grace ends a deadline for everyone; real events need a per-person exception. |

## Corrections and calls on the mock

| # | Area | Decision | Why |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 22 | Invited sessions | An invited speaker's session is an accepted, organizer-created session with an invited participant; the invitation's token is stored hashed on the participant and is the credential for the invitation page only. Accepting with no account goes on to set a password. Declining offers an optional note to the organizers. | Reuses the existing participant and claim flows instead of a parallel invitation object. |
| 23 | Sponsor teammates | A teammate added by a sponsor contact is "invited" until they first open their portal, then "joined". Only joined contacts reach the sponsor's pages. | The team page shows invited vs joined; access must not be granted by an email the invitee hasn't acted on. |
| 24 | Sponsor speakers | Speakers on a sponsor session are invited only when the organizers approve it ("new — invited on approval"). Approve creates the session in Submissions as accepted (origin sponsor) with the speakers as participants, which is what puts them in the CFP or Sponsors portals. Decline needs a reason and returns the slot. | The mock's approve and decline notes. |
| 25 | Approval queue | Adds a "With the sponsor" group (changes requested, waiting on them) between Waiting and Decided. | The mock's queue has nowhere to show a session sent back for changes. |
| 26 | Sponsor URLs | Sponsor pages use the sponsorship id (`/portal/sponsors/<id>`), not a company slug. | Ids are unguessable and never collide across events; every route resolves the id through the contact's own joined membership. |
| 27 | Participant home | Session sections are shown for every accepted session, even one no portal adds items to, and keep today's session-card content (format, placement, the deliverable line, Upload again, Read notes). The home keeps "Your submissions". Direct tasks keep showing in the profile area. | "Existing speakers see exactly what they see today." |
| 28 | Done items | Done items fold behind a "N done" line at phone; desktop shows them inline, muted. | The 390 frame folds them; desktop has the room. |
| 29 | Notes | Portal notes render in the house muted style, not italic. | The design system's type vocabulary has no italic. |
| 30 | Welcome note | Profile basics' note is the event's welcome note: editing it there edits the stored welcome note, which still shows where it always has, at the top of every participant's home. Settings · Portals keeps showing it and its edit drill can still change it. | The mock says the Speaker portal section's welcome note "becomes the Profile basics portal". Moving it without moving where it renders keeps today's home unchanged. |
| 31 | Hero | "Hi Priya — 5 things to do" at every width. | The 390 frame's "5 to do" would hide the noun at phone, and the v12 phone frame says "things to do". |
| 32 | Settings | The Settings rail keeps the repo's order (Your data before People and roles), with Portals in Speaker portal's place (same `?section=portal` key) and Sponsorships after it. Portals' rows are the mock's (Portals, Coverage, Libraries, Access), plus the welcome note and a Branding row whose Change opens the existing branding, welcome note and resource-files form. The fixed "Speakers can edit" pills are gone: what participants may edit is set per portal. | The repo's rail order is newer than the Portals frame's. Branding and resource files have no other home. |
| 33 | Sub-pages | Settings sub-pages (Portals, a portal, Libraries, Sponsorships) keep the app's normal header and use the section rail with a back link above the H1, like a section's edit view. | One header across the app. The frame's breadcrumb header would be a second navigation pattern. |
| 34 | Person record | The person record is a page under Contacts (`/contacts/<id>`). Preview as participant is the participant's own server-rendered home under a fixed ink bar (`/portal/preview/people/<event>/<contact>`), and every open is audited. | The preview has to be the real page, not a re-implementation; it can only be that if it renders where the portal renders. |
| 35 | Not in any portal | `/speakers?portal=none` is its own list (person, participation, why, a link to the record), reached from the coverage count or the Speakers portal filter. | It lists participations, not speakers-by-tasks, so it can't be a filter of the grid's rows. |

## Organizer screens

| # | Area | Decision | Why |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 36 | Portal editor · name | The H1 shows the portal's name; "Rename" opens a name field, and opens on its own after New portal or Duplicate. | Keeps the frame's plain H1 while a new portal still gets named. |
| 37 | Portal editor · matches | Matches are counted a moment after the rule stops changing. "N saved" is the saved rule's live count, with +/− against it. An unfinished condition isn't counted (the panel says so) and blocks Review and save with a list naming the field. | Memberships lag the rule until a recount; the server refuses a condition with no value. |
| 38 | Portal editor · rule | "Add a condition" in every group, "Add an 'or' group" after the last. One select for is / is not; a checklist behind a select-shaped button for is any of / is none of; free text for fields with no list (custom fields). | The frame draws both actions once after group 1, which reads oddly with two groups. |
| 39 | Portal editor · items | Deadline is a select (No deadline / Fixed date / After joining) with a date or days box beside it; grace shows "—" when there's no deadline. Each row's "Options" holds the per-portal name, allow edits after submitting, view-only once done, and Remove. | The gap item settings, which the frame doesn't draw. Grace only extends a deadline. |
| 40 | Portal editor · permissions | All profile and session fields are listed; unset fields show their default; only fields the organizer decided are sent. Email offers Hidden or View only. Help text per field via "Add help text". The sponsor-only actions become switches once any group could match a sponsor contact. | Only decided fields should constrain anyone (decision 9). |
| 41 | Portal editor · note | Previewed in the house muted style under the first match's heading; person-only portals preview under "Your profile"; Profile basics' note previews as the top of every home. A checkbox exposes the portal's own "email new joiners" setting. | Mirrors where the note shows (decision 30); the setting had no other control. |
| 42 | Change preview | The title follows what changes ("gives N people new items", "applies to N participations" when nobody joins or leaves). Items added and removed are listed; gain lines say "N already have it from another portal". The email choice defaults to the portal's setting. | The brief's notification gap; the server's raw lines are terse. |
| 43 | Delete and leave | Deleting a portal asks for its name typed in (Profile basics adds a stronger warning). Leaving the editor with edits asks first, from the back link, the Settings rail, the top nav and browser close. | Deleting can't be undone; edits are one page, one save. |
| 44 | Library | Grouped by type, with a tab only for types that have items; tasks are editable in place; a resource is shown to everyone with a portal or only through the portals that list it. A new form opens the CFP form builder in a new tab so the editor keeps its edits. | No filter that can only empty the list. The form builder edits only the CFP form today. |
| 45 | Copy from another event | Pick the event, then portals and/or sponsor tiers; the result lists what was copied and skipped. | Gap item; the dialog says why something was skipped. |
| 46 | Sponsorships · tiers | Each entitlement row is count × format × length; a format that states its length shows it read-only. Editing a tier that has sponsors asks the server which sponsors would go over, 300ms after the last edit, and lists them before Save (Save stays enabled; approved sessions are never removed). | The frame's edit-tier note. |
| 47 | Sponsorships · sponsors | A sponsor opens a wide dialog: tier, status, package slots, sessions over the allowance, per-sponsor extras and contacts. The primary contact has no Remove. Pending sponsors are flagged. Add sponsor takes an optional first contact. | The frame shows sponsors as rows only; extras and contacts need a home. |
| 48 | Approval queue | Waiting, With the sponsor (changes requested or withdrawn to edit) and Decided, with a back link to Speakers; the Speakers head links to the queue while sessions wait. | Decision 25; the queue lives under Speakers but had no way in. |
| 49 | Review page | The last request is pinned while changes are requested and on resubmission. History is newest first, in the event's timezone. The package and the decisions sit beside the submission, and after the submission at phone. Approve has no confirm: its receipt reports what happened, including failed or skipped emails. | The frame's review page, with multiple request rounds. |
| 50 | Person record · layout | Group headings use the session reference and title, the company or the role, not the participant's "Your talk". Participations with no items and no pending invitation get no item group. Unanswered invitations show what acceptance brings as Waiting rows ("Arrives when Ruth accepts"). | The organizer reads references; the view's headings speak to the participant. |
| 51 | Person record · provenance | "How it arrived" combines the session's origin with its status ("Through the CFP · accepted", "Invited by organizers · no reply yet"); company rows read "Gold sponsor"; roles read "Added by organizers". A merged item shows one outlined tag whose portal names are separate links, and the line names which portal set the deadline and the required flag. "Also assigned directly" when both. | Answers "why does she have this?" without opening a portal (e3 note). |
| 52 | Person record · actions | Reopen sits in the Deadline cell of a closed item and defaults to a week out. Event roles (Volunteer, Press, Host, Staff) are added or removed after a confirm; portals re-match in the background. Invite to speak picks an accepted session the person isn't on, a role, an optional message and reply-by day. | Decision 21 and the gap roles; invitations reuse decision 22. |
| 53 | Person record · heal | Reading the record materializes any portal item that has no row yet, the way the participant's home does, so every item can be reopened. | Otherwise portal items stay unreopenable until the next recount. |
| 54 | Preview bar | The fixed ink bar sits above the portal's own header (the shell starts below it) with an outlined "Exit preview" button back to the person record. | The frame's bar; covering the header hid whose portal it was. |
| 55 | Settings sub-pages | The section rail on a sub-page is the same set of buttons Settings draws, and goes through the app's leave guard. | One rail, and the editor's unsaved-edits guard applies to it. |
| 56 | Status words | The participant home and the person record use one set of item status words from the domain module. | Both screens describe the same item. |
