NotesOpen questions and new components
Open questions — choices I made
  1. Losing a portal keeps finished work. The item leaves the home; the answer stays on the record. Alternative: keep showing completed items read-only.
  2. Grace period is per item, not per portal. If the same item comes from two portals with different grace, I took the longer — the participant-friendly reading of “earliest deadline”.
  3. Sponsor-added speakers are invited on approval, not on submit, so nobody is invited to a session that is declined.
  4. Invited items are named but not due before acceptance. Relative deadlines start at acceptance, not invitation.
  5. Settings sub-nav changes: “Speaker portal” is replaced by Portals, and Sponsorships is added. The Settings frames still show the old nav and need updating when this ships. Portal pages use a wider 1180 main column than the 820 Settings forms, because their tables need it.
  6. Portals live in Settings, sponsor approval in Speakers. Approval could equally sit in Submissions.
  7. Rules saved with zero matches are allowed and flagged, rather than blocked.
  8. No portal names shown to participants — only the note to participants. Anyone who needs the “why” is an organizer, and the record shows it.
New components
  • “Or” group in the rule builder — stacked “all of” blocks under a dashed “or” rule. Extends the Contacts filter-rules row.
  • Provenance tag — pill naming the portal(s) behind an item; ink-outlined when merged.
  • Lock line — “Locked” caps plus the reason, under a profile field.
  • Change-impact modal — gain/lose counts and item lists before any rule or item save.
  • Preview bar — fixed ink bar for logged previews.
  • Package panel — entitlements with used/available state. Everything else reuses existing rows, sections, tags and controls.
