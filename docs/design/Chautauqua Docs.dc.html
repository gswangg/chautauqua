Running an event · for organisers

Triaging submissions

Deciding which talks make the programme. Accepting or declining never emails anyone — that is a separate, deliberate act in Comms.

Every submission arrives as Pending. From the Submissions table you can move one to the accept queue, the decline queue, or the waitlist — and you can do it to a hundred at a time.

Worth knowing A status change is silent. Speakers learn their outcome when you send from Comms, which is why the two are separate screens.

Working through the queue

Open a submission to read the abstract, the form answers and every review it has collected. Accept and Decline sit in the right rail beside them, so the decision is next to the evidence.

  1. Filter to Needs triage from the saved views.
  2. Open the oldest — the table sorts that way by default.
  3. Decide, then use Next › rather than going back to the list.

The element library

Nine blocks. An article is a sequence of these and nothing else — a new kind of block is a change to this page first, not a one-off inside one article.

01 · Prose

Body copy is 16px on 1.7, at the 680 measure, in #3F4237. Bold marks a UI label the reader will look for on screen — Needs triage, Approve — and nothing else. Links inside prose take the olive and underline on hover.

Paragraphs are short. If one runs past six lines at this measure it is two paragraphs, or it is a list.

02 · Headings

H1 · 38 · one per page, the title

H2 · 24 · a step or a topic

H3 · 18 · rare, and never below this

Three levels. An article needing H4 is two articles.
03 · Steps and lists
  1. Numbered when order matters — a procedure the reader follows.
  2. One action per step, starting with the verb.
  3. Never more than seven; past that it is two procedures.
  • Bulleted when order does not matter.
  • Parallel grammar across items, always.
04 · The aside · one weight only
Worth knowing Something true that the screen does not say. Olive edge, one line of label, two sentences at most.
Cannot be undone Ink edge, reserved for irreversible acts and data loss. If everything is a warning, nothing is — expect at most one per article.
Two weights, no others. No tip / note / info / caution ladder — a reader cannot rank five colours, and translation breaks the words.
