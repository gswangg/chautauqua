# DEC-749: A person's name is entered as ONE field and split on the last whitespace

docs/design/Chautauqua Submissions.dc.html:470-474 shows the New submission modal asking for "Speaker name" and "Speaker email" — two fields, not three — and the Contacts quick-add does the same. The store keeps firstName/lastName (schema.contact), so the split belongs in one exported pure helper: everything before the final run of whitespace is the given name, the last token is the family name, a single token yields firstName only with an empty lastName, and nothing is inferred beyond that. One helper with its own unit test means the modal, the quick-add row and any future entry point cannot disagree, and the API contract (`contact: {email, firstName, lastName}`) is untouched. The email label reads "Speaker email" — the mock never marks a field "(OPTIONAL)" in its label; optionality is shown by the absence of the required marker.
