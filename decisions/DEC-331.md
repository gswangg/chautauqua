# DEC-331: Perf enforcement must cover all five public surfaces plus both whole-agenda .ics feeds; a new check over budget is a logged product finding, never a loosened budget or a dropped check

SPEC §7 is explicit that 'budgets that aren't enforced are decoration', and SPEC J10 names five public surfaces. scripts/perf-smoke.ts's timed set (checks[] at :278-373) covers only /e/:slug/sessions (:323) and /e/:slug/agenda (:328) plus schedule.ics with 150 ids (:335). Three gaps follow, all at the 2k-submission/800-contact perf seed where they matter: /e/:slug/speakers is the single most plausible remaining perf cliff in the product (speaker-rooted composite gate plus per-speaker session hydration over 800 contacts) and is unmeasured; /e/:slug/gallery and /e/:slug/schedule are likewise unmeasured public surfaces; and the whole-agenda .ics paths — /e/:slug/agenda.ics and, since DEC-323 made a bare request publish the entire agenda, /e/:slug/schedule.ics with no ?ids= — are newly reachable, O(entire agenda) by design, and timed nowhere. This is the exact class DEC-310 was written about, and the decomposition that just landed rewrote those very modules. All added checks take cls 'public' and are graded by DEC-309's public budget minus the /health overhead floor. The rule that keeps this honest: if an added check exceeds budget, it is a PRODUCT finding logged as an open item naming the owning module and function — the budget is never raised, the check is never dropped or marked optional, and the lane that discovered it does not optimise src/ (that is a separate cited fix).
