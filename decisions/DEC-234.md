# DEC-234: deleteField dangling visible-if references: verified fail-safe, fix DECLINED for stage 1

No SPEC clause, clarification, or eval-rubric ID covers builder field-deletion referential semantics. Declined as a stage-1 non-defect; recorded so future review lenses do not reopen it as unaddressed.
