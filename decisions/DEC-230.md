# DEC-230: zonedMinutesToUtc becomes DST-safe: two-pass offset correction with Temporal-'compatible' disambiguation (gap resolves forward, overlap resolves to the earlier instant)

Review-lens verified live: src/lib/timezone.ts:15-52 computes the zone offset at the naive guess instant and applies ONE correction without re-verifying, so wall-clock times in/near a DST transition export a silently wrong .ics instant — contradicting the module's own 'works across DST boundaries' comment and the calendar-invites pitch. Fix: after the first correction, read the candidate instant back through the same Intl.DateTimeFormat; if it does not reproduce the requested wall clock, apply the residual delta once and re-check. Disambiguation policy (matches Temporal's 'compatible' mode): nonexistent times in a spring-forward gap resolve FORWARD to the post-transition instant; ambiguous times in a fall-back overlap resolve to the EARLIER instant (first occurrence). Pure Web APIs only (DEC-002). Regression tests must cover spring-forward gap, fall-back overlap, and normal times on both sides of transitions in at least America/New_York, Europe/Berlin, and a southern-hemisphere zone (Australia/Sydney).
