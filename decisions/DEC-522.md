# DEC-522: A date-only field is a DAY LABEL, not an instant: displayed as its calendar day (read in UTC), expanded to an event-local instant at every HARD gate, compared raw only for soft signals

Two binding decisions currently contradict each other on the same values, which by the w24 lesson means one is wrong. DEC-153 (`app/src/lib/dates.ts:7-15`) rules that every date-only field is stored as UTC-midnight epoch-ms and displayed by reading it back in UTC; DEC-408/DEC-413 rule that the portal and public surfaces render dates in the owning event's timezone. Applied to the same stored number they disagree by a day, and both consequences are live on main. DISPLAY: `src/routes/portal/tasks.tsx:164,282`, `src/routes/portal/index.tsx:144` and `src/server/repo/tasks/reminders.ts:93` pass a task due date through `formatEventDate(ms, eventTz)`, so a due date the admin grid shows as Mar 1 renders as "Sun, 28 Feb" in the speaker's own portal and in the reminder email he receives -- for every event west of UTC. GATE: the formerly-separate `app/src/pages/forms/FormSettings.tsx:25` (since consolidated into the forms/settings surfaces) wrote a CFP close date as UTC midnight of the chosen day, and `src/lib/submit-core.ts:11-13` closes the form strictly after that instant, so a producer who sets "closes Mar 1" on a Los Angeles event actually closes submissions at 16:00 on Feb 28 -- and `src/routes/public/submit.tsx:249` then prints "closes Sun, 28 Feb 2027, 16:00 PST" on the public page, a date the producer never typed. The identical bug gates speaker editing (`src/domain/edit-lock.ts:11,18`) and reviewer evaluation (`src/domain/evaluation.ts:313-321` via `src/routes/review/reviewer.ts:73,189`, where it silently empties a reviewer's queue a day early). Decision: the stored UTC-midnight number is a LABEL for a calendar day in the owning event's timezone, and there are exactly two legal uses. (1) DISPLAY: render the day itself via a new `formatCalendarDate(ms)` reading in UTC -- never `formatEventDate`/`formatEventDateTime`, which re-interpret it. True instants (createdAt, sentAt, submittedAt, uploadedAt) are unaffected and keep event-tz rendering. (2) HARD GATE: expand the label to a real instant in the event's timezone before comparing -- start-of-day for an open date, end-of-day (next local midnight minus 1 ms) for a close date -- via two new helpers in `src/lib/timezone.ts` built on the existing DST-safe `zonedMinutesToUtc`, and derive the YYYY-MM-DD from the label with getUTC* accessors, never `toISOString` (standing ban). The gate predicates take the timezone as a parameter so no call site can forget it. Deliberately NOT expanded, so nobody re-litigates it: the SQL overdue predicates (`tasks/grid.ts:83,225`, `overview.ts:323`) and the 72h reminder window (`domain/reminders.ts:44`) compare the label directly. Those are soft signals -- a flag and an email nudge -- whose worst error is under a day, and expanding them would require IANA timezone math inside SQLite. A hard gate that rejects a submission, locks an edit, or empties a queue gets the expansion; a nudge does not. Rejected: storing event-local instants instead, which would push timezone knowledge into every SPA date input and reverse DEC-153 across forms, plans and tasks at once.
