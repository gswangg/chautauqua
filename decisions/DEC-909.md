# DEC-909: A form field states its own optionality and its own budget — one renderer, one grammar, no asterisks

src/views/form-render.tsx:110-123 is the ONE field renderer behind /submit/:slug, /portal/edit and /portal/tasks. It marks required with a trailing ' *' and says nothing at all about optional fields or length budgets. The frames say the opposite on both counts: docs/design/Chautauqua Public and Portal.dc.html:533 labels an optional field 'Accessibility needs · optional', and :364/:490/:602 put a live counter ('412 / 1,200') right-aligned on the label row of every long-text field. An asterisk is a legend the page never prints; '· optional' is the same information in words, and it is the OPTIONAL case that needs saying — a submitter's real question is what they may skip, not what they may not. The counter is not decoration either: a required abstract that silently truncates at a limit nobody displayed is the same class of defect as a required number that lands null. Rule: the label is the frame's 11px/800/0.1em uppercase micro-label; required fields carry NO marker; optional fields append ' · optional'; a long-text field with a maximum carries a live thousands-separated counter fed by the same inline script that already toggles conditional visibility (form-render.tsx:214) — no framework, no new bundle. The `required` attribute and the data-required visibility contract are untouched: this is grammar, not validation. Because there is one renderer, all three surfaces inherit it; forking the grammar per surface is how six grammars get installed.

## Amendment: the cap-disclosure duty extends to hand-written SSR controls, held by a scan over the portal surfaces (wave 2)

DEC-909 gave FormFieldDef-driven controls a live counter, and w1-a gave them maxlength. Every SSR control NOT built from a FormFieldDef was left out. Verified offender: src/routes/portal/edit.tsx's 'Add a co-presenter' form renders firstName/lastName/email with no maxlength while src/server/repo/portal-edit.ts's addCoPresenter refuses anything over MAX_NAME_LENGTH -- the speaker types 300 characters and learns on submit. src/routes/portal/profile.tsx and src/routes/portal/tasks/views.tsx already do it right, which is what makes this a drift rather than a missing feature. Ruling: every hand-written text-entry control in the SSR portal declares the maxlength of the constant its writer enforces, or is named in a scan exemption with the reason written beside it -- never an allowlist populated from the failure output. Population deliberately excludes src/routes/public/** and src/views/** (owned by w1-e and w1-a this wave) and type=password (owned by DEC-740's amendment).

## Amendment (wave 134): A field states its own grammar in the frame's words — and where the frame's prose asserts behaviour a source-scan forbids, the app's truthful line wins and the frame is superseded

Three field captions adopt the frame verbatim: Submissions.dc.html:296 (co-presenter placeholder 'Add a co-presenter by name or email…' vs the app's 'Search contacts by name or email...'), :681 ('What the submitter reads' under the builder's Label control — absent), :685 (help-text placeholder 'Beginner, intermediate or advanced' vs 'Shown beneath the field, e.g. 300 words max'). Two frame captions go the other way and are recorded as blessed deviations rather than built. (i) Submissions.dc.html:298 'A new co-presenter is emailed a portal link' — `src/routes/api/submissions.ts:6` states, and a source-scan test enforces, that the submissions routes NEVER import a mailer; adding a participant cannot send anything, and `SubmissionDetailPage.tsx:1855` already says so truthfully. (ii) Speakers.dc.html:697 'skips anyone emailed in the last hour' — the dedupe gate is `task_assignment.last_reminded_at` (`src/server/repo/tasks/reminders.ts:214`), not any email, so the shared `REMIND_SKIP_RULE` ('skips anyone reminded in the last hour', `remindSkipVocabulary.ts:8`, one vocabulary across 11 files and its own scan) is the accurate word. Wave 133 proved a ruling's prose can be the stale artifact; so can a frame's.
