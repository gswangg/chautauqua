# DEC-909: A form field states its own optionality and its own budget — one renderer, one grammar, no asterisks

src/views/form-render.tsx:110-123 is the ONE field renderer behind /submit/:slug, /portal/edit and /portal/tasks. It marks required with a trailing ' *' and says nothing at all about optional fields or length budgets. The frames say the opposite on both counts: docs/design/Chautauqua Public and Portal.dc.html:533 labels an optional field 'Accessibility needs · optional', and :364/:490/:602 put a live counter ('412 / 1,200') right-aligned on the label row of every long-text field. An asterisk is a legend the page never prints; '· optional' is the same information in words, and it is the OPTIONAL case that needs saying — a submitter's real question is what they may skip, not what they may not. The counter is not decoration either: a required abstract that silently truncates at a limit nobody displayed is the same class of defect as a required number that lands null. Rule: the label is the frame's 11px/800/0.1em uppercase micro-label; required fields carry NO marker; optional fields append ' · optional'; a long-text field with a maximum carries a live thousands-separated counter fed by the same inline script that already toggles conditional visibility (form-render.tsx:214) — no framework, no new bundle. The `required` attribute and the data-required visibility contract are untouched: this is grammar, not validation. Because there is one renderer, all three surfaces inherit it; forking the grammar per surface is how six grammars get installed.
