# DEC-467: user.email is subject to the DEC-454 rule too, and exactly one normalizeEmail survives in the product

DEC-454 closed contact.email at the CFP, the contacts API, the CSV import, and (DEC-462) manual session creation. `POST /api/v1/users` (src/routes/api/users.ts:66) still writes `user.email` from `record.email.trim().toLowerCase()` with only a non-empty and a MAX_NAME_LENGTH check, then immediately mails the account notice to it inside a DEC-238 try/catch that swallows the failure. An organizer inviting a reviewer at a typo'd address gets a 201, a one-time password, and no signal that the notice went nowhere — "nothing falls through the cracks" inverted. It is also the identity string DEC-456's findAccountUserId matches on, so a malformed one can never link to its contact. The route validates with isValidEmail and stores normalizeEmail's output, answering 400 with fields.email like every other write. Separately, src/domain/contacts.ts:50 carries a private `normalizedEmail` that is a byte-identical second copy of normalizeEmail; DEC-454 said one normalization function exists in the product, so it is deleted and its call sites repointed — no re-export, no shim (house rule).
