# DEC-466: The DEC-460 population was under-enumerated by three; the enumeration criterion is now mechanical, not a hand-built list

task-w17-f-pagination-audit-stage1.md enumerated 20 list endpoints and 14 unbounded ones, and DEC-460 froze that list as authoritative. It missed three handlers that emit the DEC-013 envelope with `total: <array>.length, page: 1, perPage: <array>.length || 1` — the exact cosmetic shape DEC-460 was written to outlaw: `GET /api/v1/review/plans/:id/queue` (src/routes/review/reviewer.ts:136), `GET /api/v1/plans/:id/progress` (src/routes/review/plans.ts:310), and `GET /api/v1/contacts/duplicates` (src/routes/api/contacts/crud.ts:114). The queue is the worst of them: it is the one list SPEC.md:193-195 names by function ("keyboard-fast review"), and for an unrestricted reviewer on the 2,000-submission perf event it returns all 2,000 shaped rows. All three are assembled in JS from an already-materialized array, so DEC-461(e)'s blessed exception applies verbatim — clampPage + listPerPage, slice the already-ordered array, report the full array length as `total`, never re-derive `total` from the slice. The durable half of this decision matters more than the fix: a hand-built inventory is a sample wearing an enumeration's clothes, which is precisely what DEC-459 forbids. The population criterion is now mechanical and re-derivable — every `c.json({ items` site under src/routes — and no ledger may grade SPEC.md:353 from DEC-460's list again.
