# DEC-819: The Review surface names the number it is showing

aggregateEvaluations (src/domain/evaluation.ts:127-133) already returns a WEIGHTED mean — computeWeightedScore per evaluation, normalized by total weight — and the sortable header now reads 'Weighted score', but the caption beneath it still says 'Mean of submitted reviews · recusals excluded' (app/src/pages/review/ResultsTable.tsx:323), which describes a plain average and contradicts the plan editor's own 'Scores average by weight'. Separately, ReviewerQueue.tsx:151-163 heads the plan-SCOPED route with the same 'Your queue' as the landing and passes showHeading={false}, so the one fact that distinguishes the two pages — which plan you are in — is the fact that is dropped. Decision: the caption states the weighting, the scoped page is headed by its plan's name (with '← Your plans' kept and 'Your queue' reserved for the landing), and the plan and progress rows carry the subtitle figures they already compute ('All tracks · 3 reviews each', 'X of Y'). One vocabulary, and every number on the page says what it is.
