# DEC-630: A phone-only block is display:none at desktop, enumerated across every stylesheet

The same defect has now shipped twice — a phone list with no desktop display:none renders a second time underneath the desktop layout (the "stray concatenated row", and the earlier pipeline phone-list leak) — and each time it was fixed with a spot check on that one class. The signature is statically decidable: a phone-named selector whose TOP-LEVEL rule (outside any @media) declares a display other than none paints on desktop. The test enumerates every phone-named selector in every stylesheet under app/src and src/routes; a phone-named class either has no top-level rule at all (it inherits its parent's hidden state) or its top-level rule is display:none. Anything that is genuinely not phone-only is NAMED in an allowlist with its reason, never absorbed by loosening the rule.
