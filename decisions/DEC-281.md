# DEC-281: Wave 8 is the exit-battery wave; only a P1 may displace it again

DEC-262's rule (battery only in a wave whose planner emits zero product tasks) has now deferred the battery three times, and each deferral was individually justified but collectively unbounded. Bound it: wave 8's planner emits the eight DEC-256 sections and nothing else, declares S per DEC-270 step 1 / DEC-280, and does NOT emit product tasks for defects it notices while reading the tree -- it records them in its waveSummary so the battery lanes confirm or refute them as OPEN ITEMS. The single exception, and it must be argued explicitly in the wave summary, is a P1: data loss, a cross-tenant or role authz leak, or a J1-J12 job that cannot be completed at all. Anything smaller is logged and, if still real after the battery, fixed in wave 9 with only the affected sections re-run. This makes 'when does stage 1 get declared' a decidable question rather than a function of how hard the next planner looks.
