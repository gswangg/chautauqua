# DEC-483: The clamp enumeration scans the clamp EXPRESSION, not the existence of a page-size constant

DEC-480 requires an executable enumeration, and DEC-477/487 simultaneously re-homes PUBLIC_PER_PAGE/MAX_PUBLIC_PAGE/MAX_PUBLIC_ROWS into src/server/repo/public/bounds.ts. A scan phrased as 'no *_PER_PAGE constant outside src/lib/pagination.ts' would red-flag a sibling lane's correct code, and the first worker to hit that red flag would weaken the assertion — which is exactly how DEC-460's and DEC-466's hand lists died. The scan therefore targets the clamp arithmetic that only src/lib/pagination.ts may own: a line matching /Math\.min\([^)]*\bPER_PAGE\b/ anywhere under src/ other than src/lib/pagination.ts, or a const declared exactly DEFAULT_PER_PAGE / MAX_PER_PAGE outside it. The public row ceiling's own Math.min binds MAX_PUBLIC_ROWS, not a PER_PAGE, so it is not a match and needs no allowlist entry — the criterion separates the two rules by shape rather than by a maintained list of exceptions. app/ is out of scope: the SPA's mirror of the 200 max is a client-side convenience, not the server contract.
