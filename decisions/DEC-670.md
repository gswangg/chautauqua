# DEC-670: A capped anonymous list reports the cap in the vocabulary of what it SHOWS, never a count of what it hides

src/routes/root.tsx:276 renders "Showing {shown} of {total} events" where `shown` is the number of CANDIDATE rows listHubEvents returned (up to LIMIT 100) and `total` is `select count(*) from event where org_id = ?` (src/server/repo/public/home.ts:69-73) -- neither number describes the page. groupHubEvents (src/lib/home-hub.ts:66) then DROPS every event that is neither an open CFP nor has a published session, so a hub rendering three rows can print "Showing 100 of 137 events" and, worse, disclose to an anonymous competitor exactly how many unannounced events the org holds. DEC-581 put the privacy predicate in home-hub.ts precisely so a stranger never learns more than the rows say; a total computed OUTSIDE that predicate is the same leak by another route. The rule: the cap note may only ever speak about rows that passed the predicate, and it appears only when the bounded candidate window was actually truncated. Second, the candidate window orders by start_date desc, so an open CFP on an older-dated event can be cut by the cap and vanish from the hub with nothing said -- the bound must be reported as a fact (`capped`) the renderer can state, not inferred from a mismatched count. Third, event.start_date/end_date are NOT NULL columns (the former src/db/schema.ts:116-117 (now split across src/db/schema/)), so HubEvent's `number | null` typing and root.tsx's `!` assertions are a modelled-nullable/asserted-non-null pair: tighten the type and delete the dead branches rather than keep both.
