# DEC-597: Contacts are creatable and exportable from the directory itself; contacts becomes a first-class export kind

POST /contacts exists but the directory offers only Import CSV — there is no way to add one person, and no way to get the list back out, on a screen whose whole purpose is the org's people. Adding a client-side CSV built from the loaded page would export whatever happened to be paginated in, which is precisely the cap-the-UI-cannot-see failure; so 'contacts' joins EXPORT_KINDS and inherits DEC-560's deterministic ordering and DEC-027's export route, and the New-contact modal writes through the endpoint that already exists.

## Amendment (wave 64): the new-contact modal states WHY email is required and ends by naming what it does not do

`docs/design/DESIGN-RULINGS.md:339` rules both facts for `NewContactModal` — email carries the reason it is required ("it is how contacts are matched and merged") and the modal closes by saying that adding someone to the directory does not put them on an event, since *Add to an event* on their row is the separate act. Read on main: `NewContactModal.tsx` has the Email FormRow (`:158-165`) with no reason line and no closing sentence; the only place the distinction exists is the separate `AddToEventModal`. This is the exact confusion the graded CSV-import cluster produced from the other direction (directory vs event coupling), so the copy is load-bearing, not decoration.
