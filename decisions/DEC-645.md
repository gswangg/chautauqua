# DEC-645: The aie profile scales every dimension the scale mandate names, not just the row counts the first pass reached

PerfProfile carries submissions/contacts/tracks/answers/statuses; rooms, days, reviewers, plans, tasks and deliberate conflicts are module-level constants in scripts/perf-seed.ts. So `--profile=aie` produces 2,500 submissions in 4 rooms with 12 reviewers and one plan — a "scale" event whose agenda, review and onboarding surfaces are still at demo scale, which is exactly where the mandate's design-at-scale bars bite (10 rooms × 4 days legibility, ≥5,000 assignments, 400 tasks ~15% overdue). Every volume the mandate names becomes a profile field; `default` keeps today's numbers bit-for-bit so existing budgets stay comparable run-to-run.
