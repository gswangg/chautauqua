# DEC-469: The CI perf smoke must be able to measure the two lists DEC-460 named as unbounded-over-an-org's-lifetime

SPEC.md:356-357 makes the perf smoke the enforcement mechanism ("hit the hot endpoints against the 2,000-row seed and fail the build over budget — budgets that aren't enforced are decoration"). task-w17-f had to report /api/v1/pipeline and /api/v1/users as static-analysis findings only, because scripts/perf-seed.ts seeds neither pipeline entries nor extra org users: it returned 3/3 and 19/19 rows live, so no measurement could ever have caught the unbounded read on the two endpoints DEC-460 ranked most at risk. A budget row that is unmeasurable by construction is exactly the class of evidence DEC-453 refuses to accept. The perf seed therefore grows pipeline entries and org users to scale-target counts, and the smoke gains read-budget checks for both endpoints; existing checks are additive-only so prior runs stay comparable.
