# DEC-661: Every public surface has a guessable root: /e/:slug and /embed/:slug redirect to the sessions surface, and an unknown slug 404s rather than redirecting into one

src/routes/public/index.tsx registers /e/:eventSlug/<surface> for each surface but nothing for the bare /e/:eventSlug or /embed/:eventSlug, so a judge or an embedder who types the obvious URL falls through to the app-wide 404. SPEC section 2 principle 1 makes routes guessable an explicit product rule and section 9 records that the eval agent probes URLs by hand. The redirect resolves the event first so an unknown slug answers 404 directly: redirecting into a 404 costs a round trip and briefly asserts the event exists.
