# DEC-627: The public edge cache is purged by publish-affecting writes only, classified by a closed two-list enumeration

bumpPublicVersionMiddleware is mounted globally (src/server/app.ts:37) and bumps chq:pubver after ANY non-GET response < 400 (src/server/pubcache.ts:116), so POST /login, /logout, reviewer evaluation PUTs, saved views, email sends and pipeline notes all invalidate every /e/* and /embed/* entry — the only two cached prefixes (src/routes/public/index.tsx:85-86) — collapsing the hit rate SPEC §7's sub-50ms cached-TTFB budget depends on. Classification is a pure predicate over (method, path) with an explicit never-public list and an explicit public-affecting list; the default for anything unclassified is to BUMP (a stale public page is worse than a cold cache), and a source-scanning test requires every mutating route to appear in exactly one list so the manifest cannot desync.

## Amendment (wave 70): a mutating route is classified by its MOST PUBLIC-REACHING WRITE — content-approval revocation is public-affecting wherever it happens

visibleSessionConditions() (src/server/repo/public/gates.ts:26) gates every /e/* and /embed/* read on content_status='approved', so reopenContentReview (src/server/repo/files-content-status.ts:88) can make an accepted session vanish from public. Two of its callers sit in NEVER_PUBLIC: POST /api/v1/submissions/:id/files (src/routes/files.ts:204, pattern pubcache.ts:190) and POST /portal/tasks/:assignmentId/upload (src/routes/portal/tasks.tsx:494, swept up by the /portal/tasks/* wildcard at :194). Their stated rationale — "no public route ever renders a submission's files" — was true when written and was falsified by the DEC-020 reopen amendment; the boundary outlived its reason. The already-established precedent is DEC-741 (/content-note is public-affecting because its note/mail half rides alongside a content_status write). Ruling: /api/v1/submissions/:id/files moves to PUBLIC_AFFECTING; the /portal/tasks/* wildcard is replaced by the three exact non-public mutating patterns (complete, form, comments) with upload promoted to PUBLIC_AFFECTING. Every rationale comment names the reaching WRITE, not the route's nominal subject. And because a hardcoded surface list goes stale, the guard pairs its named list of content-status-writing routes with a COUNT assertion over the writer call sites, so a fourth caller cannot land silently.
