# DEC-609: The content worklist is four columns: a row is a session, its deliverables, its status and its actions

The worklist renders five fixed columns plus one per deliverable kind, so adding a kind widens the table and every count sits under its own header at the far right, away from the session it belongs to. The design's four-column IA keeps a row readable at any kind count: identity (ref, title, speakers) stacks in one cell, per-kind counts become chips inside a single deliverables cell (a kind with nothing uploaded is shown as absent, not as a bare zero), status is one cell, and the approve/ask-for-changes pair is the last. The chips carry the kind's name, not a position in a header row, so meaning survives the phone reflow.
