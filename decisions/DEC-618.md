# DEC-618: AUDIT.md's claims are enumerated by a test, not maintained by hand

An audit that drifts from the code is worse than no audit. Every route path AUDIT.md names must resolve against the route manifest render-sweep already enumerates, and every 'not built' claim must be a check that finds nothing in the tree. Hand-listed manifests desync — enumerate them in a test. The audit is also where capped or degraded behaviour gets recorded honestly (row caps, recipient caps, stage-2 deferrals), so the same rule that governs on-screen totals governs this document: never print a number you did not count.

## Amendment (findings wave 7) — AUDIT.md's prose drifted from the tree in four places; the export-kind list joins routes and absence markers as a MECHANICALLY checked claim

DEC-618 exists because AUDIT.md's claims must be enumerated by a test rather than maintained by hand, and DEC-642 extended that to the "deliberately not built" list. Everything else in the document is honour-system prose, and four claims have gone stale on current main: (a) the `/logout` paragraph (docs/AUDIT.md:199-204) describes a GET confirmation card carrying a CSRF form and a "Stay signed in" link, while src/routes/auth-login.tsx:224-232 records DEC-154's wave-25 amendment — "/logout has no screen" — and the GET is a bare redirect to /login; (b) the J12 section names exports as "submissions, speakers, sessions, show-flow" while EXPORT_KINDS (src/server/repo/exports/kinds.ts:6) is submissions/speakers/evaluations/agenda/email-log/contacts — `sessions` is not a kind at all, and evaluations plus email log, both named explicitly by SPEC J12, go unmentioned; (c) the stage-2 section says production email delivery is unbuilt with "Resend as the stage-2 adapter behind it" while src/mail/email-binding.ts (the Cloudflare `send_email` binding adapter, DEC-996) ships on main and README's live-demo paragraph credits it with sending real mail; (d) the Airtable bullet omits the AIRTABLE_ORG_ID throw. README.md is deliberately excluded from this ruling — task-w6-d owns the same class there. RULING: correct all four, and convert the export list from prose into a checked claim — test/audit-claims.test.ts parses EXPORT_KINDS and asserts the J12 section names exactly those kinds, no more and no fewer. A self-audit whose honesty rests on memory is the document most likely to be wrong.

## Amendment (wave 30): every numeric cap AUDIT.md states is compared against the imported constant, two-directionally, with a population tripwire

docs/AUDIT.md:342-349 admits the gap in its own honesty rules — route paths and absence markers are mechanical (DEC-618/DEC-642), the caps are 'read live from its source constant' by a human. That human was wrong at least once: AUDIT.md:248-250 says /admin/comms 'caps at MAX_PER_PAGE=200 selected submissions' when src/routes/comms/compose-core.ts:107-109 caps submissionIds at MAX_COMPOSE_RECIPIENTS = 100 (src/domain/compose.ts:9) — a stated total that was never counted, against the document's own rule. Constants are importable; the claim is therefore mechanically checkable and must be checked.
