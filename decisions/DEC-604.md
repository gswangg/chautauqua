# DEC-604: A speaker may add a co-presenter to their own submission, and adding one never writes to somebody else's contact record

ABS-S1 is built around a speaker adding Marcus Okafor to Priya's proposal and ABS-11 scores the persisted role label; today the only participant control is organiser-side. The privilege question this raises is the whole design: a speaker must not be able to use the portal as a write path into the org's CRM. So the portal resolves the email to an existing contact and touches none of its fields, or creates a contact carrying name and email only; the insert is bounded by the edit lock, capped per submission, and the (submission_id, contact_id) uniqueIndex is treated as the contract it is — a duplicate is a validation error, not a 500. Nothing emails, per the house invariant, and the page says so rather than letting the speaker infer an invitation went out.

## Amendment (findings wave 12)

MAX_PARTICIPANTS_PER_SUBMISSION is enforced only in addCoPresenter (src/server/repo/portal-edit.ts:482-498), which counts EVERY participant row on the submission — including organiser-invited ones. inviteParticipant (src/server/repo/participants.ts:76-126) performs no count check at all. The cap therefore binds the speaker and not the organiser, while the organiser consumes the speaker's headroom. That asymmetry is not free: participant count is the multiplier on expandRecipients' 100-recipient compose batch (src/domain/compose.ts:66-86), on the DEC-932 acceptance task back-fill, and on every public speakers row.

RULING: the cap is a property of the submission. inviteParticipant gains the same pre-write count check, refusing with the shared cap grammar before any INSERT; the constant's doc comment in src/domain/participant-roles.ts:43-49 is corrected to say so instead of scoping itself to the portal path. insertActiveParticipants (the roster-push writer onto a submission it just created) is explicitly out of scope and unchanged.

## Amendment (findings wave 15): MAX_PARTICIPANTS_PER_SUBMISSION binds every writer door, and each door refuses in its own established shape

src/server/repo/participants.ts:37-40 states the cap is "a property of the SUBMISSION, enforced at BOTH doors that can add a participant row". There are four. Two enforce it (addParticipant, participants.ts:96-97; addCoPresenter, portal-edit.ts:487). Two do not: (1) insertActiveParticipants (participants.ts:~183 `db.insert(schema.participant).values(chunk)`, no count read), reached from pushContactsToEvent (contacts/push.ts:110) from POST /api/v1/contacts/import with an eventId -- a 200-row roster CSV mints ONE submission carrying 199 participants, 33x the cap of 6; (2) the Sessionboard participants importer (src/server/repo/import/sessionboard.ts:658), same bare chunked insert. RULING: the cap binds all four. The constant stays single-sourced at src/domain/participant-roles.ts:54; no door re-declares it. Refusal shape follows each door's existing grammar rather than inventing a third: the CSV roster import REFUSES BEFORE ANY WRITE with a 400 naming the real numbers (it is one indivisible batch, and a post-write refusal would leave contacts created with no roster) -- the headroom is computed exactly, from the file's distinct valid emails minus those whose contact is already on the roster, so a legitimate small batch is never falsely refused; the Sessionboard importer SKIPS the offending rows into its existing `skipped: {row, reason}[]` channel and keeps going, because that importer is already row-wise and per-row honest. insertActiveParticipants itself gains a pre-write assertion so no future caller can reopen this door silently.

## Amendment (wave 56): the V10 co-presenter frames are the build spec — three copy facts, list-then-form, a secondary submit, and the 1600 form grammar

docs/design/DESIGN-RULINGS.md:216-232 (vendored, authoritative) plus the V10 intake at docs/eval-findings.md:53-65 draw the portal edit view that DEC-604 shipped unframed. Measured against src/routes/portal/edit.tsx at this runtime: fact 1 is WRONG on main (':263-264' says 'They will not receive an email or invitation' where the ruling requires 'No email goes to them — tell them yourself' — the ruling's point is that a speaker who assumes an invitation went out will not follow up, and the passive voice loses the instruction); fact 3 is ABSENT entirely (no window line anywhere in edit.tsx; the ruling names it as REPLACING an 'Edits are live on the public pages straight away' sub-line that contradicted fact 2 — that sub-line is already gone, but its replacement never landed, so the screen states neither); fact 2 is present per-row at ':254-256' and stays. Structure (list first, form second, secondary submit) is already correct and must not be churned. The desktop half is unbuilt: the ruling grants this one portal page its own 1600 frame against B6's 560 column because it is the one portal page with a form, and imports three things from the B10 form spec (DESIGN-RULINGS.md:145-148) — first/last name two-up in a 1fr 1fr grid, email paired with a 190px role select, and a right-flushed footer row (Cancel then Save changes) instead of the phone's stacked action bar, with 'Add co-presenter' left-aligned at its natural width so it never competes with the page's own primary. RULING: build to the frames. The window line renders only when data.form.closeDate is non-null — an omitted line beats an invented date. Duplicate stays a server-only rejection (migrations/0019's unique index is the arbiter, the client cannot pre-empt it) rendered in the standard error shape: banner naming what did not happen, the email field marked with the API's own message, and 'Everything you typed is still below.'

## Amendment (wave 60): a labelled field inside a flex/grid column fills its column; max-width is not a width

Verified: src/routes/portal/portal.css.ts:527-541 builds the DEC-604 two-up co-presenter layout (flex-wrap, `flex: 1 1 220px` per pair member, a 190px role select), and src/routes/portal/edit.tsx:316/327/343 renders bare unclassed <input> elements inside those columns. src/views/theme.ts:179-181 gives text inputs `max-width: 100%` only, and portal.css.ts's `width: 100%` rule at :336-340 is keyed on `.chq-field .chq-input`, a class these inputs do not carry — so they sit at the browser's intrinsic ~188px inside a 220px-basis column, which is exactly what gate-10 measured. The bare-page fix ruled once already never reached the portal form. RULING: a text input that is the sole control of a labelled field inside a flex/grid column takes the shared field-fill contract (the existing .chq-field/.chq-input classes, not a new one-off rule), so the frame's two-up pairing renders at the drawn widths; a test asserts the co-presenter inputs resolve through a rule that sets width, not merely max-width.

## Amendment (wave 67): LEAD is a position, role is a vocabulary — one participant identity everywhere, and the ternary is reversed out

`app/src/pages/submissions/SubmissionDetailPage.tsx:1470-1472` renders `{isLead ? 'LEAD' : 'CO-PRESENTER'}`, a binary that collapses 'moderator' and 'panelist' — both real members of PARTICIPANT_ROLE_OPTIONS (`src/domain/participant-roles.ts:19-24`) — into "CO-PRESENTER", while the speaker portal renders `participantRoleLabel(r.role)` (`src/server/repo/portal-edit.ts:448`) and prints "Moderator"/"Panelist" for the same person. Two surfaces, two identities, one human: exactly the mandate's P3 #14. The comment at :1445-1449 that justifies the ternary (quoted as a wave-25 clause) is REVERSED by this amendment and must say so. RULING: LEAD is a POSITION computed from the resolved lead participant and keeps its own chip; every other row's text is `participantRoleLabel(p.role).toUpperCase()`, including the documented free-text fallback. Chip class derivation is unchanged — only the text is re-sourced. DEC-604 owns the vocabulary, so the ruling lands here, not on the decision the stale comment cited.

## Amendment (wave 70): the organizer's add-co-presenter door states its non-effect, exactly as the portal's does

The v10 portal frame (10--23) mandates the honest line "No email goes to them — tell them yourself". `POST /api/v1/submissions/:id/participants` does the same nothing — its own comment (routes/api/submissions.ts:673-678) says "Per product principle 4, this does NOT send an email" — but the organizer surface (SubmissionDetailPage.tsx:1554-1615) says nothing at all, which is why a judge read the two surfaces as asymmetric. The server behaviour is already symmetric (both doors write `inviteStatus: 'invited'`; portal-edit.ts:584 and `inviteParticipant` agree). RULING: this is copy, never a new send — the organizer door names both facts the portal names: no mail goes out here, and the portal invitation is a separate explicit action.
