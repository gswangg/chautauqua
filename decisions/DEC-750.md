# DEC-750: A dialog may not promise what the store cannot carry, nor drop a control the data model needs — and dialog actions align left

Two mock-versus-model conflicts, decided together so no worker resolves them twice. (1) The Save-this-view frame draws an unchecked "Share it with the other organisers" checkbox, but schema.savedView has no owner and no shared flag — every view is event-wide by construction. Rendering a checkbox that cannot vary is the disabled-control lie in another costume, so the modal states the fact as a caption ("Everyone organising this event sees it") and the rest of the frame's copy is adopted verbatim: subtitle naming the actual filter state, label "Name it", primary "Save the view", a text "Close" rather than a glyph. (2) The New submission frame shows four fields, which would remove the Track control DEC-598 added after track-less submissions silently fell out of track-scoped assignment, the public track filter and the agenda — chrome fidelity never removes a capability, so Track and Format stay below the four named fields. (3) Every modal frame in the pack puts its primary at the bottom LEFT; that is one declaration in components/modal-frame.css, not per-dialog markup.
