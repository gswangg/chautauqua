# DEC-574: An upload control never discards the unsaved fields around it

Two confirmed data-loss paths with one cause: a file upload navigates or unmounts, and everything typed but not yet saved is gone. src/routes/portal/profile.tsx renders the headshot as its own <form> posting to /portal/profile/headshot with a PRG redirect, so a speaker who writes a new bio and then uploads a photo loses the bio with no warning. app/src/pages/contacts/ContactDrawer.tsx calls onSaved() after uploadHeadshot(), and ContactsApp's onSaved closes the drawer and reloads — same loss, for bio, notes and custom-field rows. Resolution: the portal profile becomes ONE multipart form (details + optional headshot) posting to POST /portal/profile, which saves fields and photo in a single act and redirects with ?saved=1[&headshot=1]; the now-redundant POST /portal/profile/headshot route is deleted rather than kept as a shim (house rule: no back-compat). The admin drawer keeps the drawer mounted after an upload, refreshes only the headshot, and signals the list through a callback distinct from the close-on-save one. The DEC-084 dimension gate, DEC-059 downscale and the DEC-245 distinct success notice all survive the move unchanged.
