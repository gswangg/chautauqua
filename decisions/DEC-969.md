# DEC-969: One menu primitive: a popup menu dismisses on an outside press, takes focus, roves with arrows, and hands focus back

Two popup menus ship in the admin SPA and both are role="menu" panels with Escape and nothing else. app/src/components/EventSwitcher.tsx:183/:218 is on EVERY admin screen — click anywhere else on the page and the event menu stays open over the content — and app/src/pages/speakers/ParticipationMenu.tsx:57/:75, DEC-830's new participation control, has the same three gaps: no outside-pointerdown dismissal, no focus moved into the panel on open, no ArrowUp/ArrowDown roving between its menuitemradio rows, and no focus returned to the trigger when it closes, so a keyboard organizer opens the menu and is left at the trigger with an invisible panel below them. Escape alone is not a menu; it is a list of buttons that happens to be absolutely positioned. This is the field guide's own rule — one shared vocabulary plus a lock beats per-page fixes — applied to the second dialog-adjacent primitive after ModalFrame. Rule: app/src/lib/useMenu.ts owns the behaviour as `useMenu(open, close)` returning a container ref (wrapping trigger AND panel) and a panel key handler; it closes on a pointerdown outside the container, focuses the panel's first [role^="menuitem"] on open, roves ArrowDown/ArrowUp with wrap plus Home/End, and returns focus to the container's [aria-haspopup] trigger on Escape, outside press or selection, removing its listeners when closed or unmounted. EventSwitcher and ParticipationMenu both adopt it and neither keeps a private Escape wiring. The two phone sheets are dialogs, not menus, and are out of scope until the mobile round.
