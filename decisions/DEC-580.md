# DEC-580: A drill-in resolves its own subject instead of trusting whichever list happens to be loaded

ContentApp derives the open deliverable as `items.find(i => i.id === submissionId)`, where items is the current page of the WORKLIST filtered to changes_requested. From the Files library the chosen file's submission is almost never in that page, so `selected` is undefined, the detail never renders, and the "versions and comments" button appears to do nothing but change the URL — the CNT-D1 defect, which is why it reads as a wiring bug even though the panel works from the submission-detail page. A row's identity must be sufficient to open it: when the id is not present in the loaded list, fetch it (GET /api/v1/submissions/:id) rather than falling through to the list. Loading and not-found are rendered states, never a silent no-op.
