## Amendment (wave 9, sha 3323a7b7): deliverableCounts is RETIRED — a superseded computed field is deleted, not ledgered; its FILE_KINDS vocabulary guard moves to latestFileByKind

DEC-247 established `deliverableCounts` (per-kind chain-root counts) to answer "does this kind have a deliverable". DEC-708's amendment introduced `latestFileByKind`, which answers the same question PLUS the version ("Slides v3 · Recording v1"), and that is what `SessionList.tsx`'s Latest-file column reads. Verified on main: no component reads `deliverableCounts` — it survives only as a declaration (`app/src/pages/content/types.ts:66`), test fixtures, and a per-request `GROUP BY` hydration in `src/server/repo/submissions/list.ts` (~`:490-546`) paid on every submissions/content list read. `test/wire-field-reader.scan.test.ts`'s own ledger entry calls it "a follow-up deletion candidate rather than decided here". A ledger reason that defers a decision is a work item, and the house rule is delete-the-old-code, not keep-a-shim. Ruling: retire the field end to end. DEC-879's rule that the per-kind vocabulary is spoken from `FILE_KINDS` survives its subject — retarget `test/deliverable-counts-vocabulary.test.ts` onto `latestFileByKind` rather than deleting the guard. `EventFileChainItem.versionCount` is a DIFFERENT ledger entry with its own DEC-902 reasoning and is deliberately NOT in scope.
