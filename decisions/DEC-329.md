# DEC-329: A walkthrough probe whose premise contradicts a binding decision is a PROBE defect: rewrite it to assert BOTH directions (what must vanish AND what must remain); narrowing a FETCH is allowed, weakening an ASSERTION never is

task-w15-b-c3-walkthrough-fix.md landed on main at OPEN ITEMS: 1 / PARTIAL with the only unresolved item in the whole stage-1 exit predicate: scripts/walkthrough/public.ts's check 'J10 visibility gate: hidden participant is absent from every surface' (createThrowawaySubmission ~:558, assertAbsentEverywhere ~:568, the check itself ~:604-651) asserts that hiding a submission's ONLY participant removes the submission TITLE from all five /e and all five /embed surfaces. I re-verified the premise against the binding decision rather than inheriting the lane's conclusion: DEC-274.md and src/server/repo/public/gates.ts split the gate in two — visibleSessionConditions() is submission.status='accepted' AND submission.content_status='approved' with NO reference to participant, and the speaker sub-query carries visibleParticipantConditions() separately — precisely so 'a TBA keynote or a session whose speaker is not yet announced must still appear on the program with an empty speaker line'. docs/sessionboard-reference/00-how-sessionboard-works.md:89 agrees ('hidden speakers are excluded from embeds' — speakers, not their sessions), and SPEC §5's 'distinct gates, never collapsed' is the same rule. So the product is right and the probe is wrong. The repair rule, binding from here: a probe rewritten because its premise was wrong must assert the decision's behaviour in BOTH directions — the thing that must disappear (the participant's NAME, everywhere) and the thing that must remain (the session title on the three session-rooted surfaces and its VEVENT/UID in schedule.ics?ids=) — because a one-directional rewrite silently degrades into a no-op assertion, which is the failure mode DEC-326 forbids. Narrowing a FETCH so the probe actually reaches its fixture (scoping /sessions with ?q= against the PER_PAGE=12 cap, or /agenda with ?day=) is a legitimate repair and not a loosening; deleting or weakening an assertion is not.
