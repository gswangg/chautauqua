# DEC-906: Results are ranked: RANK leads the table, REF and the evaluation tally leave it, and a decision is one line

app/src/pages/review/ResultsTable.tsx:331-353 opens with REF and carries a '# evaluations' column, while the 03-review results frame leads with RANK and drops both; the producer reading results wants the order, not the identifier. Rule: a leading, non-sortable RANK column renders offset + index + 1 — the row's position in the ordering the table is CURRENTLY showing, whose default is the weighted score, so on arrival RANK is score rank and after a re-sort it is the rank by what the reader chose to rank by. The ref is not deleted from the payload or the page: it renders as a muted prefix inside the Title cell, so dropping the column costs no identification. The evaluation tally leaves the table (the progress panel already owns coverage), scores print to one decimal, and Accept/Decline sit side by side on one line in the Decision cell rather than stacking a row's height for two buttons.
