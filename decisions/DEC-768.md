# DEC-768: Public agenda rows grow to their content; a block never clips

VERIFIED: src/routes/public/agenda.tsx:84-86 sets `grid-template-rows: auto repeat(N, 22px)`, so a 15-minute session gets exactly 22px and its title and speakers are cut off -- the user-reported "overflowing text in calendar grid", confirmed by the EMB probe at 1280x800. The admin grid already solved this (app/src/pages/agenda/DayGrid.tsx:227, `minmax(24px, auto)`, DEC-742) and the field guide records the lesson ("a card you must scroll inside is not readable: rows grow"). Decision: the public grid uses the same rule -- explicit rows become minmax(<rowHeight>, auto) so a row expands to its tallest block, and .chq-pub-agenda-block gets no fixed height and no overflow:hidden. The public surface is the product's output (principle 5); content that is cut off is not published.

## Amendment (wave 67): the public agenda is one day at a time, and the day IS the h1

docs/design/README.md §"Public agenda — desktop": "The day appears once, as the `<h1>` ... The filter bar's day control is the switcher — one day at a time, since ten sessions is a page, not a scroll. That removes two of the three day repetitions on its own." The landed surface does the opposite: `AgendaContent` (`src/routes/public/agenda.tsx:526`) renders a literal `<h1>Agenda</h1>`, then a `DaySwitcher`, then a per-day `<h2>` for EVERY day, because `dispatch.tsx:174` only scopes to a day when `?day=` is present — so the default `/e/<slug>/agenda` stacks all three seeded days and the switcher navigates from a page that already shows its destination (also the exact shape EMB-07 grades: "at least one session differs between the two captured days"). RULING: `/e/:slug/agenda` (and its `/embed` twin) is single-day by default. The default day is the FIRST day that has scheduled sessions — deterministic and therefore edge-cacheable, never a "today" comparison, which would make the version-salted cached copy wrong for a visitor on another date. The `<h1>` names that day and its counts; the switcher sits right-aligned on the heading row; the per-day `<h2>` disappears because the day is now named once. `/schedule` is untouched: it is the personal picker and legitimately lists every day.

Repointing formatDay's BODY fixes all ten callers in one edit and leaves the switcher untouched; a second grammar surviving in the same page is the defect, not the locale. formatEventDay keeps no privileged status -- if the sweep orphans it, it goes.

## Amendment (wave 48)

**ONE public clock grammar: formatStartTime24 is the only time-of-day formatter on any /e/:slug surface.** DEC-768 already closed the public CALENDAR-DAY grammar to one function (formatDayLong). The CLOCK was never closed: src/routes/public/cards.tsx:133 still exports a 12-hour formatMinutes (`9:30 AM`) used by agenda-grid.tsx:147/152, agenda-list.tsx:80/91/97, agenda.tsx:215/272, agenda-rail.tsx:157 and programme.tsx:70/76, while the same session on the sessions list and on session detail renders `9:30` through formatStartTime24 (cards.tsx:145, detail.tsx:30). Two grammars for one value on one event; the vendored docs/design/README.md 'Public agenda — desktop' writes the day heading as '9:00 to 17:30' and the eval mandate's CLASS 6 records the app's 12-hour clock as the deviation. RULING: formatStartTime24 (unpadded 24-hour H:MM) is the single public clock; formatMinutes is deleted, not deprecated; a start-plus-end range renders as `9:00–9:30` through the same function. The admin agenda's own two forms (gridMath.formatMinutes zero-padded, formatGutterTime unpadded, both 24h) are a SEPARATE, already-reasoned surface and are untouched.
