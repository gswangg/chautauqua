# DEC-742: A clash is shown at full size, and placing a session returns focus to what was placed

eval-findings asks for a decision between a lane-split cell and the mock's full-width card. The Agenda pack answers it: the desktop clashing cell is ONE inverted (ink) card, and the phone frame stacks both sessions inside a single ink block led by "Two sessions in this slot". So the desktop cell keeps one card and stacks the clashing sessions inside it under the caption "Two sessions in one room", and the cell's row grows to fit its content — no inner scroll, because a scroller inside a 44px grid cell hides the very evidence the state exists to surface (README J9: conflicts are surfaced, never blocked). Lane-splitting is rejected: at four rooms a half-width lane cannot hold a title, and it invents a second geometry the phone view does not share. Placement also currently leaves focus on a button that no longer exists; after a successful place, focus moves to the placed session's cell, and after Cancel to the first cell of the armed row, so the keyboard path never lands on the document body.
