# DEC-719: A sandboxed embed child is identified by its source window and per-instance nonce; its origin is the literal string "null"

public/embed.js sandboxes the iframe with allow-scripts and deliberately no allow-same-origin (DEC-617), which gives the child document an opaque origin; its postMessage therefore arrives with event.origin === 'null', and the guard at :114 comparing against SCRIPT_ORIGIN can never pass. Every third-party <chq-embed> stays pinned at the 160px MIN_HEIGHT and clips the list it was embedded to show, while test/embed-element.test.ts passes because jsdom does not model opaque origins. The choice is to drop the sandbox or to stop treating the origin string as the identity. Keep the sandbox: under an opaque origin, identity is carried by axes that are not forgeable from another frame — event.source must be this instance's contentWindow and data.id must equal this instance's random nonce. The origin check narrows to 'our own origin, or the opaque "null" of our own sandboxed child', and every other origin is still refused.
