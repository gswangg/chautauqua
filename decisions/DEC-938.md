# DEC-938: The armed agenda grid quiets a clash card with INK, never with opacity; the armed lattice is a 1px inset ring

`.chq-day-grid-armed .chq-day-grid-clash-card` (agenda.css:264) fades the whole card with `opacity: .55`, which fades its TEXT along with its fill — the rule's own comment computes the blended result at rgb(127,128,122), and DEC-870's card text over that measures 3.56:1, the last AA failure on the desktop gate. Opacity is the wrong instrument: it cannot distinguish the surface being quieted from the words that must stay readable. Separately, `.chq-day-grid-armed .chq-day-grid-cell-btn` (:200) paints `outline: 2px solid var(--chq-ink)` on all ~144 cells at once; the offsets double at shared edges into a black lattice that outshouts the schedule it is drawn over. Rule: quieting is a COLOUR substitution, never an opacity — the armed clash card keeps opacity 1 and swaps to muted surface/ink tokens chosen so its text measures >=4.5:1 against its own painted fill and the armed cell ring measures >=3:1 against that same fill. The at-rest armed ring drops to 1px in a lighter token (inset, keeping z-index 6); the 2px/3px ink and brand rings stay, reserved for :hover and :focus-visible where exactly one cell wears them. The ratio is held by a computed-contrast test over the token values, not by a screenshot.
