# DEC-777: The speaker's own session page: one fact per row, event-timezone dates, no organiser-only sections

VERIFIED in src/routes/portal/index.tsx:356-413: the status badge is a bare <span> immediately after the back link (they share a line), the REF·format·track line and the placement line are sibling inline spans that run together, and the placement renders `{detail.day}, {minutesToClock(...)}` -- the raw ISO "2027-05-12, 09:00" the probe reported, even though the route already holds detail.timezone and the module already imports the event-time formatters. The mock (docs/design/Chautauqua Public and Portal.dc.html) shows the speaker's own session as identity rows plus a Slides card with version/size/"shared with the organisers", and no Participants or Answers dump -- both of which the speaker still reaches through /portal/submissions/:id/edit, so removing them from the READ view deletes no capability. Decision: block-level rows, dates through src/lib/event-time.ts in the OWNING EVENT's zone ("Tue 12 May, 10:00 · Room 2A"), a Slides card with real file meta, and the welcome tagline confined to the portal home where identity is not being displaced.

## Amendment (wave 4): the speaker's read view names who else is on the session

The portal read view deliberately dropped the Participants section in the w2-f rebuild, leaving co-presenters visible only inside the edit form -- so a speaker reading their own session page cannot see who they are presenting with, and the SPK graders record the absence. DEC-777's rule is 'one fact per row, no organiser-only sections'; a co-presenter is the speaker's own fact, not an organiser's. Ruling: one 'With <names>' line under the meta line, sourced from the SAME getPortalParticipants read the edit view uses (never a second query shape), naming people as the portal stored them (DEC-866: never echo a CRM identity resolved from an email), the viewer excluded, and the line absent entirely when there is nobody else.


## Amendment (wave 13) — one portal placement grammar; no portal surface prints a raw YYYY-MM-DD

src/routes/portal/index.tsx:180-185 interpolates `placed.day` RAW, so the portal home prints "DevFlow Conf 2027 · you speak 2027-05-12, 10:00, Room 2A", while the SAME FILE's formatPlacement (line 378) prints "Wed, May 12, 2027, 10:00 · Room 2A" on the submission detail. RULING: add formatDayMedium(day) to that family under the identical contract (day is a wall-clock 'YYYY-MM-DD' in the OWNING event's timezone per DEC-010 — no timeZone parameter, UTC calendar fields, malformed input returned unchanged), and make portal/index.tsx's formatPlacement the ONE portal placement grammar that both the home subline and the detail line compose. formatDayShort and formatDayLong are untouched — the agenda day switcher's "Tue 12" is pinned by frames.
