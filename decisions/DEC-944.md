# DEC-944: The auth family renders at the frames' own card metrics, and an expired claim link is a card, not a sentence

docs/design/screens/11-account.png is authoritative for /login, /account/password and the admin not-found, and src/routes/auth.css.ts misses it on four measurable counts: `.chq-auth-card` clamps to 660px where the login frame measures 640; `.chq-auth-card-narrow` keeps the login card's 44px padding where the password/not-found frames measure 35px padding around a 450px control column; `.chq-auth-title` is 36px where both frames render 28px; and inputs are 44px tall against the frame's 48px (the submit button already is 48). Two more are structural: `.chq-auth-back` carries a permanent text-decoration where the frames underline on hover only, and the frame puts '‹ Back' at the card's left padding edge with the title INDENTED ~36px from it, which the current stacked pair does not express. The demo-identity block renders four 44px `.chq-btn-secondary` buttons where the block's own signed mandate wording is three SMALL LINKS in card vocabulary — a prefill affordance must not outweigh Sign in, the one action on the page. Semantically each auth page is a card floating in a bare <body> with no <main> and no <h1>: 'Change your password' is a <div>. And GET /claim/:token with an unknown or expired token answers `c.text("This link is invalid or has expired.", 410)` — the one place in the product where a person hits raw text/plain, and the place where a speaker whose invite aged out most needs to be told what to do next. Rule: the three AUTH_CSS consumers (src/routes/auth.tsx, src/routes/account.tsx, src/server/not-found.tsx) share ONE card vocabulary at the frames' numbers — card 640 / narrow 520, narrow padding 35 with a 450 control column, title 28px, inputs and primaries 48px, links underlined on :hover only — each page wraps its card in <main> and names itself with exactly one <h1>; the demo block becomes small links keeping the `.chq-auth-demo-btn` hook the DEMO_PREFILL_SCRIPT delegates on; and the expired claim link renders the same card with an eyebrow, an <h1>, prose naming the recovery ('ask the organizer to send a new portal invite'), and a Log in link, still at status 410. AUTH_CSS stays a fixed value-free constant (DEC-374).
