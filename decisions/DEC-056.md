## Amendment (wave 68): /docs/api documents the PUBLIC read surfaces too, in their own group, with their own coverage assertion

DEC-056's hand-maintained page (src/routes/docs.tsx) documents /api/v1 only, and test/docs-route-coverage.test.ts diffs ROUTE_GROUPS against the mounted API sub-apps so that table cannot drift. But J12 and swyx's stated bonus ("an API", "embeds on ai.engineer", data flowing outward) are served just as much by the no-login read surfaces -- the .json/.xml feeds under /embed/:eventSlug, the two .ics endpoints, the chromeless /embed twins, /embed/e/:embedId, the printable programme and /submit/:eventSlug -- and NOTHING documents their URL shape anywhere. A technical teammate can only discover them by reading the source or copying a builder snippet. They go in a SECOND exported constant (PUBLIC_ROUTE_GROUPS) rendered by the same page, so the existing ROUTE_GROUPS-vs-/api/v1 diff stays exactly as it is and the new group gets its own assertion against publicRoutes' registered GETs. Paths only, no knob tables: the per-surface knob set is being changed in another lane this wave and a documented knob list would drift the day it landed.
