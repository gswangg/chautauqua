# DEC-476: Schedule-slot minutes are day-bounded at isValidSlotInput, the single slot write, using the same MINUTES_PER_DAY as AUTO_SCHEDULE_BOUNDS

the former src/server/repo/agenda.ts:367-368 (now split across src/server/repo/agenda/) validates only startMin >= 0 and endMin > startMin, with no upper bound, while src/routes/agenda.ts:129-134's AUTO_SCHEDULE_BOUNDS carefully caps the very same quantities at 1439/1440. One request shape is bounded and its twin is not. A PUT /api/v1/submissions/:id/slot with endMin: 1e9 therefore persists and flows unchecked into zonedMinutesToUtc for the .ics DTEND (src/routes/public/feeds.ts:60, src/routes/comms/compose-core.ts) and into src/routes/public/agenda.tsx:16-17's Math.min/Math.max day extent, where the grid row span becomes millions of rows -- and this happens on the same request where isDayWithinEventRange already guards the day. Two bounds for one concept is how they drift; MINUTES_PER_DAY becomes one exported pure constant that both sites read. Bounding at the write boundary, not defensively at every reader, is the fail-loudly rule: a 400 naming the bound, never a 500 and never a persisted absurdity.
