# DEC-600: The speaker-facing status vocabulary is deliberately not the organiser's, and no surface may reconcile them

The re-verification pass lists 'Pending (organiser) vs Under review (speaker)' as an open defect. It is not one: speakerStatusLabel in src/server/repo/portal.ts collapses pending/accept_queue/decline_queue to 'Under review' precisely so internal queue states never leak to a speaker, and app/src/pages/submissions/types.ts already holds the organiser vocabulary in one place. Recording this as a decision so no future wave 'fixes' the divergence and leaks the accept/decline queue. A fidelity or defect report is an observation, not spec.
