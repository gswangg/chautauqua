# DEC-054: Custom submission statuses are deferred: the DEC-003 five-status pipeline is the stage-1 contract

SPEC §5 notes '(+ custom)' on the status pipeline, but swyx's stated minimum ('unreviewed → approve/maybe/deny') is fully covered by pending → accept_queue/decline_queue → accepted/declined, and every consumer (filters, bulk actions, exports, public gates, acceptance idempotency) is built on the DEC-003 literals. Retrofitting user-defined statuses now is a cross-cutting change with high regression risk and no eval or clarification demand. Recorded so no future wave re-litigates it; it belongs on the post-competition roadmap with the Sessionboard importer.
