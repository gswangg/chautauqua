# DEC-080: Public surfaces at scale: chunked hydration, SQL-join track filter, and a 300-id cap on schedule.ics ?ids=

J10's always-current program pages die first at AIE scale: every repo/public.ts id list (page hydration, cumulative show-more, full agenda, user-controlled .ics ids) funnels into unchunked inArray. Fixes: hydrateSessions and getPublicSessionsByIds iterate DEC-078 chunks; the two-step track filter in getVisibleSubmissionIdsOrdered becomes an innerJoin on submission_track with eq(trackId) — one bound param, no id list at all; comms.ts loadIcsScheduleData chunks its submissionIds. The public, unauthenticated /e/:slug/schedule.ics?ids= endpoint additionally gets a hard cap: src/lib/itinerary.ts exports MAX_ITINERARY_IDS = 300 and the route returns 400 beyond it (fail loudly, per house style) — no real personal itinerary exceeds 300 sessions, and an uncapped list is a free amplification vector on a no-login route.
