# DEC-549: A task upload becomes a session deliverable only when its task declares deliverable_kind; a task with no declared kind produces a file private to its assignee

src/routes/portal/tasks.tsx:547-571 gives EVERY file_request completion `kind = scope.deliverableKind ?? 'handout'` and `submissionId = resolveDeliverableSubmissionId(...)`. Because getFileScope keys off a non-null submissionId, that makes a personal task upload indistinguishable from a session deliverable: files-authz.ts:121 grants it to every co-participant on the submission, and files-authz.ts:157 / routes/files.ts:380-384 grant it to any reviewer holding a non-anonymized plan assignment covering it. A producer's "upload your signed speaker agreement" or "send your ID for badging" task therefore hands that document to co-speakers and to volunteer reviewers — contradicting J7's "only their own content, ever". (The reviewer report attached this to the hotel-stay and flight-reimbursement must-haves; those are kind='form' tasks per DEC-009/DEC-111 and never produce files, so the concrete harm is the producer-authored file_request, not the defaults.) The fix is already latent in the design: files-authz.ts:243-258 documents two DISJOINT populations and getTaskFileScope deliberately requires submissionId === null, restricting access to organizer + assignment contact + uploader (canAccessTaskFile) — DEC-240 simply made that population unreachable. Decision: (a) deliverable_kind is the producer's explicit opt-in. When task.deliverable_kind is non-null the upload keeps DEC-240's behavior exactly (that kind, linked to resolveDeliverableSubmissionId's submission, chained on re-upload). When it is null the upload is PRIVATE: submissionId stays null, kind stays 'handout', previous_file_id chaining is unchanged, and /files/:fileId serves it through getTaskFileScope/canAccessTaskFile. (b) resolveDeliverableSubmissionId is therefore only called on the declared-deliverable branch — a private upload costs one query fewer. (c) The seed's file_request task keeps its explicit deliverable_kind='presentation' (scripts/seed.ts:987): that is now the demonstration of the opt-in, and the demo's content pipeline is unchanged. (d) The regression bar is negative and positive: a co-participant and an in-scope non-anonymized reviewer both get 403 on a null-deliverable-kind task file that the assignee and an organizer both get 200 on; with deliverable_kind set, all four keep today's answers.
