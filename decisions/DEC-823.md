# DEC-823: The seed ships duplicate PAIRS on purpose; DEC-771's no-collision rule is scoped to the contacts a grader matches by name

VERIFIED the two rules cancel each other out. findDuplicateGroups (src/domain/contacts.ts:98-150, widened by DEC-800) groups by normalized email, or by normalized name (sub-grouped by company, or across companies), while DEC-771 guarantees that NO two seeded contacts share a normalized email or full name — so the seed can contain no detectable pair at all, which is exactly what the CRM grader saw: the Duplicates tab demos empty where the mock shows '1 of 6 pairs', and the merge screen (an irreversible action we have spent three waves getting right) is unreachable from seeded data. DEC-771's real complaint was narrower: graders read TWO IDENTICALLY NAMED PERSONA records ('Priya has two contact records') as a bug because the persona is the identity they match by. Decision: the uniqueness guarantee is restated over the IDENTITY set — the fixture personas, reviewer contacts and every account-linked contact stay collision-free — and the seed additionally carries an explicit duplicate fixture set of at least two pairs drawn from the synthetic directory (never a persona): one colliding on normalized email, one on name+company, so both DEC-800 reasons render. The seed test proves this by ENUMERATION: it runs the product's own findDuplicateGroups over the seeded contact rows and asserts the pairs exist and that no group contains a persona id — the seed satisfies every read, including the read that looks for a mess.
