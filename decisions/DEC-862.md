# DEC-862: The CFP confirmation names the reference and the address it emailed, and each of DEC-098's three states carries the design's own primary label

VERIFIED. src/routes/public/submit-views.tsx:269-311 renders a confirmation that states neither the submission's reference nor the address the confirmation went to: the flag reads a bare 'Submitted' and the body says 'to the address you provided'. Both omissions cost the submitter something real. The reference is the identifier every other surface in the product addresses a submission by (portal rows, organizer detail, review queue, exports) and the only string a speaker can quote in an email — src/routes/public/submit.tsx:436 already holds `submission.seq`, so formatRef('SES', seq) is one call away and is currently made nowhere on this path, including in the confirmation EMAIL (submit.tsx:498-511). Echoing the typed address is the cheapest possible catch for the single most common CFP failure — a mistyped email, after which the speaker waits forever for a claim link that went somewhere else; it leaks nothing, because it is the submitter's own input echoed back one second later. Decision: ConfirmationPage takes `ref` and `submittedEmail`; the eyebrow reads `SUBMITTED · <ref>`, the heading 'That's in. Check your email.', the body names the title and the echoed address, and the SAME formatRef value is threaded into the confirmation email body so page and email can never print two different references. The three states take the design's own primary labels (docs/design/README.md:321-325): fresh → 'Create a password' on the claim path; pending-existing-contact → no claim URL anywhere in the HTML plus the emailed-link sentence; has-account → 'Log in to track it'. A separate 'Already have a password? Log in ›' block sits below the primary in the two no-account states. DEC-098's security rule is untouched and is the constraint, not a suggestion: test/claim-onscreen-scope.test.ts must stay green byte-for-byte in its assertion that pending-existing-contact emits zero '/claim/', and the fresh case must keep an `<a href={claimPath}>` element for the walkthrough/scale scrape.
