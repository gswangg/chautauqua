# DEC-305: Wave-11 section->lane map, mandatory -c3- log infix, disjoint ports, and product-read-only lanes

Two collisions would silently destroy evidence, both verified by glob rather than assumed. First, campaign 1 already committed docs/verification-log/task-w11-a-build-test.md, task-w11-b-walkthrough.md, task-w11-c-perf-smoke.md, task-w11-d-render-sweep.md, task-w11-e-spec-audit.md and task-w11-f-triage-closure.md — six of the eight wave-11 lane letters have a homonym on disk, and a lane writing the bare name would OVERWRITE a first-campaign log rather than add to it (DEC-129's homonym guard protects the grep, not the filename). Every wave-11 log is therefore docs/verification-log/task-w11-<x>-c3-<scope>.md, the '-c3-' infix mandatory exactly as DEC-286 required '-c2-' for wave 8, headed `# task-w11-<x> - <scope> @ <short-sha>`. Second, four sections need a live `wrangler dev` at once and DEC-257 requires the fresh-clone lane to run README-verbatim on the DEFAULT port with no --var override, so ports are assigned by the planner rather than chosen by lanes: b=8791, c=8792, d=8793, f=8794 (only if it needs a server), g=8787 default and exclusively; a, e and h take no port. Lanes other than g may pass `--var PUBLIC_BASE_URL:http://localhost:<their port>` and must record it if they do; DEC-296 makes it unnecessary but harmless, and g must never pass it. Section->letter is fixed here so two lanes never claim the same scope: a build+test, b walkthrough, c perf-smoke, d render-sweep, e spec-audit, f triage-closure, g fresh-clone, h rubric-coverage. Every lane commits exactly ONE new file on its own branch and touches nothing under src/, app/, test/, scripts/, migrations/, wrangler.jsonc, decisions/, field-guide/ or docs/eval-findings.md, and never edits another lane's log. A defect a lane finds is LOGGED as an OPEN ITEM, never fixed — any product commit voids the freeze (DEC-069, DEC-256 para 4).
