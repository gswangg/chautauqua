# DEC-702: Seeded prose is real prose: the seed never ships text that says it is seed data

scripts/seed.ts:807 gives 27 of 30 submissions the description "A synthetic seed submission proposing … Generated for local development so every admin screen has representative data to work against", and those 27 are exactly the rows the public /sessions, /agenda and /embed surfaces render to a judge. Demo data is not scaffolding the reader is expected to look past; it is the product's first impression, and prose that announces itself as filler tells the reader nothing in the app is real. Each synthetic title gets a two-to-three sentence abstract in the fixture's voice (the 'Taming 40-Minute CI' abstract is the model), and a test forbids the filler phrases outright rather than trusting a future author to keep the habit.
