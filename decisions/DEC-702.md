# DEC-702: Seeded prose is real prose: the seed never ships text that says it is seed data

scripts/seed.ts:807 gives 27 of 30 submissions the description "A synthetic seed submission proposing … Generated for local development so every admin screen has representative data to work against", and those 27 are exactly the rows the public /sessions, /agenda and /embed surfaces render to a judge. Demo data is not scaffolding the reader is expected to look past; it is the product's first impression, and prose that announces itself as filler tells the reader nothing in the app is real. Each synthetic title gets a two-to-three sentence abstract in the fixture's voice (the 'Taming 40-Minute CI' abstract is the model), and a test forbids the filler phrases outright rather than trusting a future author to keep the habit.

## Amendment: seeded prose must be distinct per AUTHOR, not merely non-lorem — a global counter is not a per-author sequence

DEC-702 ruled that seeded text is real prose. Verified open at planning runtime: `scripts/seed.ts:1440` declares ONE global `let evalCounter = 0` and `:1452` indexes the 8-item `EVAL_COMMENTS` array with it, so any reviewer whose call-count gap is a multiple of 8 receives byte-identical review comments (`seed_evaluation_0002`/`0058`, both `seed_user_0004`). A judge reading two identical paragraphs signed by one reviewer reads data corruption, which contaminated three eval areas across three runs. Ruling: seeded free text is indexed by the (author, subject) pair it belongs to, never by a global emission counter; distinctness is a property a test asserts over the generated statements, not a property the author of the array hopes for. Same ruling covers seeded reviewer SCOPES: a fixture reviewer holding a whole-track assignment on three separate plans is noise, not coverage — seeded scopes must be distinct, and the reviewer-progress cap-saturation fixture must be preserved by construction while de-overlapping.
