# DEC-717: Every writer of submission.status goes through the one status writer — an imported acceptance is an acceptance

src/server/repo/import/sessionboard.ts:376 (insert) and :440 (update) set `status`, including 'accepted', with a raw statement; src/server/repo/submissions/status.ts:328 is the only other writer and the only one that sets accepted_at and runs the acceptance planner. So an imported acceptance lands with accepted_at NULL, zero onboarding tasks, and no session — invisible on the who-owes-me-what dashboard, in direct violation of the SPEC §5 invariant that the acceptance transition fires the J6 auto-creation exactly once, idempotently. The fix is not to duplicate the side effects in the importer but to remove status from its raw writes entirely and hand the ids to updateSubmissionStatuses, grouped by target status. One writer means one place where accepted_at, the planner and the DEC-133 ownership guard live; a second writer is a second set of invariants nobody maintains.
