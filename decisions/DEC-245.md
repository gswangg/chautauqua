# DEC-245: SSR action-confirmation conventions (closes remaining Section D feedback findings)

Prod eval flagged invisible outcomes on server-rendered actions. Convention: every SSR mutation that re-renders or redirects must surface an action-specific role="status" confirmation. Applied now in three places: (a) POST /submit/:slug/save-draft redirects to /submit/:slug?draft=saved and the GET renders a distinct 'Draft saved — you can return later to finish and submit.' banner (the existing 'Resuming your saved draft' copy remains for later resumes); (b) POST /portal/profile/headshot success renders 'Headshot uploaded.' (distinct from the details form's 'Profile saved.') next to the preview; (c) the login pending-state onsubmit handler (src/routes/auth.tsx:70, disables button + 'Signing in…') is ratified as the standing mitigation for the slow-auth double-click finding and gets a regression test pinning the #chq-login-submit handler markup.
