# DEC-812: The cron tick is configured in the repo, and its two jobs cannot take each other down

VERIFIED resolveBaseUrlForCron (src/server/origin.ts:129) throws when PUBLIC_BASE_URL is unset — correctly, since a scheduled job has no request to sniff — but PUBLIC_BASE_URL appears only in the gitignored .dev.vars and is ABSENT from wrangler.jsonc:50-55 `vars`. So on any deploy, runDueReminders (src/routes/tasks.ts:507) throws before its per-event try/catch, and src/server/scheduled.ts:17's bare `await` means runAirtableSync never runs either: two features die on every tick, dev-green. Two halves, both in the repo (no provisioning): (1) PUBLIC_BASE_URL becomes a real `vars` entry beside MAIL_FROM_*, pinned by test/wrangler-config.test.ts so a deploy cannot lose it again; (2) handleScheduled runs each job in its own try/catch, logs it, and rethrows an aggregate at the end — the tick still fails loudly and visibly, but one job's failure can never cancel the other. Nothing is defaulted and nothing is invented; only the coupling is removed.
