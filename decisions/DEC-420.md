# DEC-420: Reminder emails render each outstanding task's OWN due date, in the owning event's timezone

src/server/repo/tasks/reminders.ts:79 formats the due date as `new Date(ms).toISOString().slice(0,10)` — a UTC calendar date, the exact defect DEC-408 closed on the public surfaces and DEC-413 closed on the portal, still open on the one surface that reaches the speaker's inbox (a task due 2027-03-01 23:30 America/Los_Angeles is emailed as '2027-03-02'). A second, larger defect sits beside it: :110-120 interpolates ONE aggregate `{due_date}` (the earliest date in the group) next to a comma-joined `{task_list}`, so a speaker owing three tasks is told all three are due on the earliest one's date — SPEC J7's 'always knows what's owed and when' is actively contradicted by the product's own reminder. Settled here so no lane re-litigates the shape: reminders.ts formats through formatEventDate(ms, timeZone) from src/lib/event-time.ts (already on main since DEC-413), taking the zone from schema.event.timezone selected in listOutstandingForEvent's EXISTING joined query (the event row is already joined — no extra query, no N+1), and the body carries one line per outstanding task with that task's own due date, 'No due date' preserved for null. event.timezone is NOT NULL and formatEventDate throws on empty/invalid (DEC-408's no-UTC-fallback contract), so no fallback is added and none is permitted.
