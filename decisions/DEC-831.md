# DEC-831: A scoped queue is headed by the plan it is scoped to and counts what is LEFT; a nav link's href is the route it must highlight

VERIFIED ReviewerQueue.tsx:162-182 heads the plan-scoped route with the plan's name alone (DEC-819's half) and its rows read 'N ratings so far' — the one number a reviewer does not need — while docs/design/README.md:303-305 asks for 'Review · <plan>' over the count, '<scope> · closes in N days' beneath, and a scorecard back link that names the plan. Separately VERIFIED App.tsx:134 builds every nav href as path.replace(/\*$/,''), which turns '/review/*' into '/review/' — a trailing slash that never equals the '/review' pathname, so the ONE tab a reviewer has never highlights. Decision: the scoped queue states its own arithmetic — eyebrow 'REVIEW · <plan>', h1 '<N> left to score' where N is the unscored items, subtitle naming the scope (the plan's own track filter, or 'All tracks') and the close date in the EVENT's timezone, a progress bar of scored/total, and rows that read SCORED <x.x> or NOT SCORED with a 'Score this' action. The score a row prints is the reviewer's OWN weighted score, computed server-side by the same computeWeightedScore the results table blends with (a number a page prints must share the query's arithmetic), returned as `myScore: number | null` on the queue item. And a nav destination's href is the pathname it must match: the star is stripped with its slash, proved by a test that ENUMERATES NAV_SECTIONS and asserts each entry is active at its own route.


## Amendment (wave 40)

**A countdown is a formatter, not an expression: one days-until reader, ceiling at the owning event's day end**

Verified on main: three surfaces count down to the same CFP close date with two different formulas. app/src/pages/overview/rows.ts:74 does Math.round((value - now) / 86_400_000) on the raw day-label instant; app/src/pages/settings/CallForPapersPanel.tsx:67 and app/src/pages/review/ReviewerQueue.tsx:23 do Math.ceil((dayLabelEndInstant(closeMs, tz) - now) / MS_PER_DAY). For one seeded deadline the fleet read 'IN 19 DAYS' in Settings and '17 days' on Overview when the true answer was 18 — a rounding function and a timezone expansion, disagreeing twice in the same direction. A day-label date without the owning event's timezone is not yet an instant (DEC-522), and rounding a countdown down at noon is how 'closes today' becomes 'closes yesterday'. RULING: one exported daysUntil(dayLabelMs, timezone, now) in app/src/lib/dates.ts — ceiling through dayLabelEndInstant, floored at zero — and all three surfaces read it, keeping only their own copy grammar ('IN N DAYS' / 'closes in N days' / 'N days'). Where a pure helper lacks the timezone, it takes the parameter rather than guessing.
