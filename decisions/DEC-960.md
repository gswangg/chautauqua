# DEC-960: The scrim IS the dialog, and exactly one module in the SPA is allowed to draw it

DEC-651 made ModalFrame the ONE dialog frame and said in as many words that callers must not re-invent the chrome. Three modules still do, and each pays for it in the gate report. app/src/pages/contacts/ImportWizard.tsx:180, PipelineBoard.tsx:549 (PipelineCardModal) and ContactDrawer.tsx:325 each hand-build `<div className="chq-scrim" role="dialog" aria-modal="true" onClick={handleScrimClick}>` plus their own useEscapeKey and their own close affordance. ImportWizard's close is a bare '×' on a page-specific .chq-contacts-import-close (the report's '506px centred button'); PipelineCardModal has NO close control at all — Escape or a scrim click only — and titles itself with an <h3 className="chq-page-title chq-modal-title">, a third heading vocabulary; ContactDrawer hand-builds a head whose name+meta+Close is exactly what ModalFrame's title/subtitle/Close already models. DEC-950 landed a scan that only sees ModalFrame CONSUMERS, so it cannot see a module that never imports ModalFrame at all — the vocabulary rule needs a scan aimed at the chrome, not at the fields. Rule: all three adopt ModalFrame (ContactDrawer keeps its geometry through modalClassName='chq-drawer chq-contacts-drawer'; PipelineCardModal and ImportWizard get real .chq-modal-actions with the primary first per DEC-651), and a scan enumerates every app/src/**/*.tsx and fails any file where a SINGLE JSX opening tag carries both the `chq-scrim` class and `role="dialog"`, excluding components/ModalFrame.tsx. The pair is the ban, not the class: the two PHONE shells (App.tsx's 'More' sheet, PhoneAgenda's bottom sheet) put role="dialog" on the inner element, and mobile chrome stays out of scope until the mobile round.
