# DEC-769: Occupancy excludes the session being placed, and while armed a cell owns its own click

VERIFIED in app/src/pages/agenda/DayGrid.tsx: occupancyCount (line 175) counts every placed session in the cell INCLUDING the armed one, so a session's own current slot advertises "will clash with 1 session" and clicking it re-places the session where it already is -- a visible no-op. And the placed SessionCards render after the cell buttons in the same grid areas (line 309ff), so a click aimed at a specific 15-minute row inside an occupied column is intercepted by the card and lands at the CARD's startMin instead of the row clicked. J9 is explicit that conflicts are surfaced, never blocking; a control that announces the consequence and then does nothing has broken both halves of that promise. Decision: occupancyCount never counts armed.submissionId, and while a session is armed the cell buttons sit above placed cards so handleCellPlace runs with the minutes the organiser actually aimed at. Unarmed behaviour (click a card to arm it) is unchanged.
