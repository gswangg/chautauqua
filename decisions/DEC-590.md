# DEC-590: The speaker portal leads with a worklist whose headline count equals the rows it renders, and sign-out becomes page furniture rather than chrome

The portal opens on a progress bar, then a table of submissions, then tasks, then sessions, then a link to resources — five equal-weight sections in which the two things a speaker was emailed about are third and fourth. The design's answer is the same worklist pattern Overview uses: an h1 naming the count and the actionable rows directly beneath it. The count must be derivable by reading the page — it is exactly the rows rendered in the worklist (pending task assignments plus pending co-presenter invitations) and nothing else; no aggregate the page does not show, and per copy rule 2 no time estimate. Meanwhile Sign out is currently a filled secondary BUTTON in the masthead of every portal page — the most prominent control on a surface whose purpose is to get work done — and moves to a quiet tertiary link in a page footer. The two active field-overlap bugs on the hotel-stay and edit-session forms are in this task's lane because they are the same stylesheet: a speaker form field stack is a single-column flow with an explicit row gap, and no label or control is positioned outside normal flow.
