# DEC-765: A contact pushed to an event is linked by its own id, and the dialog's role reaches participant.role

TWO verified holes in one path. (1) src/routes/api/contacts/crud.ts:359 drops the `role` the modal posts, and src/server/repo/submissions/create.ts:106 hardcodes role:"speaker" -- the moderator/panelist options are decoration (field guide w22: a selector the POST body drops is decoration). (2) pushContactToEvent takes only {email, firstName, lastName} and re-resolves the contact through findOrCreateContact's email match, even though the route already holds the owned contact row. Email round-tripping mints a duplicate contact whenever the CRM address differs from the submission address -- and src/server/repo/agenda.ts keys speaker double-booking on participant.contactId, so a duplicate makes an invited placeholder INVISIBLE to conflict detection, which is exactly what the agenda probe saw (auto-schedule stacked "Invited: Priya Raman" over Priya's real session with zero flags). Decision: pushContactToEvent/pushContactsToEvent take the contact's id and a role, link that exact contact, and never call findOrCreateContact; createSubmission accepts an optional participant role defaulting to 'speaker'. Identity is an id you already hold, never a string you re-derive.
