# DEC-991: A clip exception names the ELEMENT's nature; a branch-ownership note or a deferred visual pass is not a reason, and the map holds exactly one entry

DEC-620's clip probe is the guard against silently truncated content, and scripts/render-sweep.ts's KNOWN_CLIP_EXCEPTIONS now silences it on 34 route/selector pairs. Eight of those read "owned by another in-flight branch" — a task-scheduling note from a wave long merged, hardened into a permanent exemption, which is precisely the failure DEC-985 named for HTML_ROUTE_EXCLUDED. Another twenty read "line-height tighter than font metrics; needs a visual pass", which is a defect with a comment: .chq-portal-hero declares line-height:1.05 and .chq-pub-header-title line-height:1 against a display face whose rendered line box is taller, so the glyphs overflow their box on the portal, every public surface, the CFP form and the account card. The only reason that may stand in this map is one about the element itself — the agenda card's deliberate -webkit-line-clamp truncation, where the card scrolls and nothing is lost. Every other entry is deleted, and the headings it covered declare a line-height at or above the font's line box; a scan over the reasons is what stops the list growing back. The same file's dead .chq-pub-gallery-tile/.chq-pub-gallery-name rules — defined by the stylesheet, used by no markup — go with them: scan BOTH directions.
