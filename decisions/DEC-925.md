# DEC-925: One count-phrase helper: a printed number and its noun always agree

The product prints counts in two incompatible ways and gets one of them wrong. app/src/lib/dates.ts:207 pluralizes correctly while :178 emits '1 days ago'; app/src/pages/contacts/pipeline-age.ts:34-46 emits 'Confirmed 1 days ago' on five card states; ContactsApp.tsx:219 emits '1 events - 1 speakers - 1 possible duplicates' in the CRM KPI strip (a defect the graders filed by hand). Everywhere it IS right, it is right by a hand-copied ternary -- `${n} recipient${n === 1 ? '' : 's'}` appears in RemindPreviewModal, CommentThread (twice), BulkEmailModal (three times) and RecentSends -- six copies of one rule, which is how the seventh copy gets it wrong. This is the same shape as the nine date complaints that turned out to be one formatter. Rule: app/src/lib/plural.ts owns countOf(n, singular, pluralForm?) returning '1 session' / '3 sessions' and plural(n, singular, pluralForm?) returning the noun alone; every count phrase in app/src reads it; and a scan test bans the bare suffix idiom `? '' : 's'` anywhere under app/src so the seventh copy cannot be written. Irregular nouns pass their plural explicitly (person/people), never by appending an 's' to a guess.

## Amendment (wave 52)

ONE spelled-count helper, and the register rule — prose spells small counts, chrome keeps numerals.

Three private spelled-number tables exist for one rule: src/routes/root.tsx:258-265 (SPELLED_COUNTS, spells 1-9, lowercase), app/src/components/ErrorSummary.tsx:7-31 (ONES, spells 0-10, Capitalised), app/src/pages/overview/rows.ts:31 (spellSmallNumber, spells 0-10 lowercase). They disagree at 0 and 10. Worse, the SAME error-summary sentence is built twice with two grammars: src/routes/public/submit-views.tsx:342 renders `countOf(n,'thing')` -> '3 things need fixing' (numeral) while ErrorSummary.countHeading renders 'Three things need fixing' (spelled) — one V9 error standard, two voices. The design handoff's own note (fleet ruling d) says numbers-as-words in prose. RULING: `spellCount(n)` joins plural/countOf in src/domain/count-copy.ts (pure core, DEC-957 crossing via app/src/lib/plural.ts), spelling 0-10 in LOWERCASE ('zero'..'ten') and returning numerals from 11; callers capitalise at sentence start. REGISTER: a SENTENCE addressed to a person (an h1 headline, an error-summary heading, a hub hero clause, a consequence line) spells the count; CHROME (pagers, table cells, micro-labels, meta lines, counters) keeps countOf's numeral. No module may declare its own spelled-number word list; a scan pins that, with exactly two allowlist entries — count-copy.ts (the home) and app/src/pages/overview/rows.ts (in flight as w51-e, to be removed next wave).
