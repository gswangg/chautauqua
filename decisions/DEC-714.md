# DEC-714: A role control offers the app's OWN role vocabulary, imported -- never another system's role names

The add-to-event modal hardcodes the participant role, and the mandate asks for a Speaker/Reviewer/Guest control. But 'reviewer' is an ACCOUNT role granted in Review (it creates a login, not a participant) and 'guest' has no representation in the data model, so offering those three would be a control that lies about two of its options. The app already owns PARTICIPANT_ROLE_OPTIONS (DEC-604, single-sourced). The control offers exactly that vocabulary, imported, and writes participant.role; the capability the mock is reaching for (choose who this person is on the event) is delivered honestly.
