The ledger graded SPEC §7's read budget 'VERIFIED (mechanism)' by citing the perf harness and asserting the two prior open items were closed from the presence of DEC-439's code -- exactly the inference the measurement refuted. Two rules follow. (i) A SPEC row whose bar is a measured number may only be graded from a perf-smoke run whose sha is an ancestor-or-equal of the ledger's own S; code presence proves a fix was attempted, never that a budget is met. (ii) A ledger must enumerate every stage-1 evidence log that landed since the previous ledger's sha and reconcile each one's RESULT and OPEN ITEMS against its own S; a log it cannot see is a reason to move its cut, not a reason to omit the row. DEC-447's ordering therefore extends: a closing wave's ledger is cut behind that wave's evidence lanes, not only behind its source lane.
