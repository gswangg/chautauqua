# DEC-451: "Every Drizzle read carries a WHERE" becomes a scanning test with an empty, deliberate allowlist

The Airtable defect survived sixteen waves of audits, two spec-audit ledgers and an external review lens because nothing mechanically enforced the invariant that every read is scoped -- reviewers verified endpoints, not queries. The codebase already carries scanning conformance tests (test/d1-bind-safety.test.ts is the model), so the enforcement mechanism is proven and costs one file. The allowlist ships empty and is the point of the design: a future genuinely-global read is not forbidden, it is made deliberate and reviewable at the moment it is written, rather than discovered by a perf lane two campaigns later. The scanner must exclude subquery builders (exists(db.select()...), .as()) and any chain that does contain .where(), and must document each exclusion, because a conformance test that cries wolf gets deleted by the next worker who trips it.
