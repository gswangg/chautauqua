# DEC-752: The bulk bar carries three moves and a Clear, and the primary is the move that applies to what you are looking at

docs/design/Chautauqua Submissions.dc.html:91-99 gives the bar four controls — "Move to accept queue" (primary), "Decline queue", "Waitlist", "Clear" — while main renders one button per member of SUBMISSION_STATUSES, six controls of equal weight that turn a triage bar into a status enum. Simply adopting the mock would delete bulk "Mark accepted", which volume-first (SPEC §2.2) will not tolerate at 23 acceptances, so the three slots are contextual on the active status filter: with no single status selected (or Pending) they are the mock's three queue moves; viewing Accept queue the primary becomes "Mark accepted"; viewing Decline queue it becomes "Mark declined". The count, the "Kept across pages · sent in batches of 100" note and the DEC-193 refetch behaviour are unchanged. Related chrome from the same frame: the column picker's summary reads "Columns: <state>" and a status pill never wraps — "Decline queue" breaking across two lines is what made the column look broken.


## Amendment (wave 14): the selection band occupies its space always; selecting toggles its visibility, never its presence

USER-FILED (gate-12 era, afternoon): checking a row on contacts and on submissions makes the bulk bar appear above the table and pushes the whole table down. The user asked for the frames to settle the form factor. They do: docs/design/Chautauqua Submissions.dc.html:91-100 draws the band INLINE between the filter block and the table header -- `background:#EFEBDF; border-radius:4px; padding:11px 15px`, a 14px/600 count, a 12px meta line and a right-aligned action cluster -- not as an overlay and not as a sticky band. Per the user's own instruction, an inline frame makes reserve-the-space the frame-faithful answer. RULING on HOW to reserve, which is the part a worker must not have to guess: do NOT reserve with a min-height magic number (nothing in the frame states the band's rendered height, and a guessed pixel is the class of number this swarm has burned waves disputing). Reserve BY CONSTRUCTION: the band is always mounted with its real structure, and a shared wrapper `.chq-bulkbar-slot` carries `is-empty` when nothing is selected, setting `visibility: hidden; pointer-events: none;` -- an identical box, zero layout shift, and elements removed from both the accessibility tree and the tab order. ONE wrapper component and ONE class serve both surfaces. Existing per-surface band classes (.chq-submissions-bulkbar*, .chq-bulkbar*) are NOT unified this wave: the user asked for one implementation of the non-shifting behaviour, and re-classing a fidelity-PASS surface is a separate, riskier concern.
