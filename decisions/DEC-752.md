# DEC-752: The bulk bar carries three moves and a Clear, and the primary is the move that applies to what you are looking at

docs/design/Chautauqua Submissions.dc.html:91-99 gives the bar four controls — "Move to accept queue" (primary), "Decline queue", "Waitlist", "Clear" — while main renders one button per member of SUBMISSION_STATUSES, six controls of equal weight that turn a triage bar into a status enum. Simply adopting the mock would delete bulk "Mark accepted", which volume-first (SPEC §2.2) will not tolerate at 23 acceptances, so the three slots are contextual on the active status filter: with no single status selected (or Pending) they are the mock's three queue moves; viewing Accept queue the primary becomes "Mark accepted"; viewing Decline queue it becomes "Mark declined". The count, the "Kept across pages · sent in batches of 100" note and the DEC-193 refetch behaviour are unchanged. Related chrome from the same frame: the column picker's summary reads "Columns: <state>" and a status pill never wraps — "Decline queue" breaking across two lines is what made the column look broken.
