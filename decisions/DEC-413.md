# DEC-413: The speaker portal renders every date in the owning event's timezone, carried per row, because the portal spans events

DEC-408 fixed the CFP page and stopped there. The speaker portal still renders every date as a UTC calendar day: src/routes/portal/index.tsx:115 and :238 (submittedAt) and :143 (dueDate), src/routes/portal/tasks.tsx:162 and :280 (dueDate), all via new Date(ms).toISOString().slice(0,10). A required task due 23:59 on 1 March in America/Los_Angeles renders to its speaker as 'Due 2027-03-02' -- a day late, silently, on the one surface whose entire job is telling a speaker what is owed and when. The event row already carries a validated IANA timezone (the former src/db/schema.ts:111 (now split across src/db/schema/)) and all three portal queries -- getPortalData's submission and task selects, getMyTaskAssignments, getPortalSubmissionDetail -- ALREADY innerJoin schema.event, so this is one added select column each and no new query. The timezone rides on the ROW, not on a portal-level setting, because /portal legitimately shows submissions and tasks from several events at once and there is no single 'portal timezone' to hang it on. The formatter is a date-only sibling in DEC-408's src/lib/event-time.ts and inherits its failure mode verbatim: an empty or invalid timeZone throws, it does not quietly render UTC.
