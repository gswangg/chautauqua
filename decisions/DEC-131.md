# DEC-131: ics escapeText normalizes CR before escaping: CRLF and lone CR become \n, so no raw CR ever appears inside a content line

src/mail/ics.ts:33 escapeText handles \n but not \r; HTML form posts submit textarea content as CRLF, so multiline titles/descriptions emit a bare CR inside SUMMARY/DESCRIPTION lines — illegal per RFC 5545 §3.3.11 and enough to make strict clients (Outlook, Gmail) reject the invite, undermining the "ics good enough" requirement for schedule.ics and J5 invite attachments. Fix: escapeText first normalizes s.replace(/\r\n/g, "\n").replace(/\r/g, "\n"), then applies the existing backslash/semicolon/comma/newline escape chain in its current order. The only CR bytes in output remain the CRLF line terminators and fold separators emitted by buildIcsCalendar/foldLine.
