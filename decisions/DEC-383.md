# DEC-383: Page and surface stylesheets carry no colour literal at all; one allowlisted exception; no drop shadow anywhere

DEC-367's 'there is no red and no third accent' is only as strong as the thing that checks it, and a ten-lane re-skin is precisely where a stray `#c0392b`, a helpful grey or a soft shadow slips back in. The rule that makes it checkable is stronger than 'no red': a stylesheet that is not a token file may contain NO hex literal and no rgb()/rgba() at all — it composes `var(--chq-*)`. Scope: `app/src/pages/**/*.css` and `src/routes/**/*.css.ts`. Excluded by construction are the two token-owning files, app/src/styles.css and src/views/theme.ts, which are where colour is allowed to exist and are already pinned by the DEC-372 parity test. Exactly one exception is allowlisted, the one the handoff itself grants: `#A8A392` in app/src/pages/contacts/contacts-panels.css, used only for the merge screen's `text-decoration` strike-through (DEC-376). Additionally, no rule anywhere under app/src or src may set a non-inset box-shadow (the mocks' `0 18px 44px rgba(27,29,23,0.13)` frame shadows are canvas presentation, and the only sanctioned shadows are the two inset underlines on `.chq-nav-link.is-active` and `.chq-rail-link.is-active`), and the strings `#c0392b`, `#fdecea` and the named colours red/crimson must not appear at all.
