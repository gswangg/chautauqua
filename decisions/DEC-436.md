# DEC-436: CONTRAST_BLOCKING's flip is wave 14's, and only from wave 14's own reading

DEC-431 fixed the order: DEC-426's contrast pass has never read all-PASS, its first all-PASS reading is wave 13's (after DEC-430's two remedies land), and the flip therefore belongs to the wave after -- this one. The flip fires ONLY inside the lane that re-runs the sweep on the current tree and reads all-PASS in its own run; a flag flipped on a prior wave's transcript is the exact failure that let five non-defects be scored as defects for three waves. If wave 13's remedies have not merged when this lane runs, the lane will read FAIL, will not flip, and will have cost nothing -- the rule is self-protecting, which is why it does not need a dependsOn. Two corollaries carry over unchanged: remedies change pixels, never the instrument (no aria-hidden or decorative exemptions may be added to scripts/render-sweep-contrast.ts, DEC-430), and the lane that flips a constant also owns the test that asserts its old value, so the suite never encodes a stale flag.
