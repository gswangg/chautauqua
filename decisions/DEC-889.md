# DEC-889: The scorecard is a rating surface: a clamped abstract with one disclosure, not a second copy of the submission detail

app/src/pages/review/Scorecard.tsx renders the description AND a full definition-list dump of every session answer AND every speaker answer, so the abstract appears twice and the reviewer scrolls past the speaker's contact details to reach the ratings the screen exists for. The Review frame shows a clamped abstract over 'Read the full submission ›'. Rule: the abstract clamps to its first ~60 words, and when clamped a single disclosure expands the remainder plus the full answer lists IN PLACE — a disclosure, not a link, because a reviewer has no organizer submission-detail route to send them to and a dead link is worse than a long page. Nothing is deleted from the payload and nothing is re-fetched; server-side anonymization (anonymizeForReviewer, which already strips speakers/speakerAnswers) remains the only thing that decides what a reviewer may see. The DEC-873 rating controls and the DEC-873 weighted blend are untouched.

## Amendment (wave 72): the scorecard's reading column IS the frame's body — ABSTRACT and FORM ANSWERS are at rest, not behind a disclosure

DEC-889 clamped the abstract to 60 words and hid the remainder plus BOTH answer lists behind one "Read the full submission ›" control, on the reasoning that the scorecard shouldn't reprint the submission detail. The redrawn review pack settles that differently and with designed authority: frame 03--01's reading column is ABSTRACT eyebrow + rule + abstract, then a FORM ANSWERS block of label|value rows — and gate-4 measured the consequence of the disclosure, ~600px of dead space in a 820px column beside a full 300px rail. A reviewer scoring against criteria needs the answers in front of them, not one click away. Ruling: the word clamp and the disclosure are retired; the reading column renders the full abstract under an ABSTRACT eyebrow and the session/speaker answers as label|value rows. Server-side anonymization (anonymizeForReviewer) remains the ONLY thing that decides whether speakerAnswers exist — the component never re-derives visibility from role — and when `plan.anonymized` is true the reading column SAYS so ("The speaker's name and company are hidden while this plan is anonymised") instead of silently omitting. The rail's two actions become full-width stacked; the second action keeps the label "Save", not the frame's "Save draft", because DEC-873 is right that there is no draft state and a control must not name a state the schema does not have.

## Amendment (wave 86): the scorecard at 390 — a criterion's scale is one row of equal 44px chips, Overall is a numeral beside its arithmetic, and an unscored card says 'Not yet' rather than computing

Chautauqua Review.dc.html:280 (→351) and :934 (→EOF) are the same surface in two states, and the difference between them is the whole ruling. Scored (:280): drill head `‹ Wave 2 queue` (44px) with `7 of 18 done` pushed right, an 11px uppercase plan/track/round eyebrow, a 21px submission title, a 12px ref line; body opens with the clamped abstract and its ONE disclosure (`Read the full submission`, 44px) that DEC-889's wave-72 amendment already rules is at rest; each criterion is label + weight caption on a baseline row, a 13px hint, then the scale as a single flex row of chips; Overall is a 34px numeral beside the sentence that explains the arithmetic (`Weighted mean of the two scored criteria · Fit is recorded, not averaged`) plus the counter-example line; then comment + a 44px recuse row; dock is filled Submit + bordered Save draft at 46px. RULING: the chip row is `display:flex; gap:7px` with every chip `flex:1; min-height:44px` centred — equal shares, never a scrollable strip and never a select — and the unscored state is a rendering of existing scorecardLogic state, not a new flag.
