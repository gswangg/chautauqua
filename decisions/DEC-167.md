# DEC-167: Contact merge carries the full profile (extends DEC-026/DEC-101)

Verified: contact rows have phone/bio/headshot_url/social_links_json/notes but planMerge and mergeContacts only cover email/name/company/title/customFields, so a duplicate holding the only bio/headshot loses them permanently when its row is deleted — breaking J11 merge and the J7/J10 speaker-profile surfaces. Rule: planMerge extends fill-if-blank (primary non-blank wins, else duplicate) to phone, bio, headshotUrl; socialLinks merge per-key fill-if-blank across {twitter,linkedin,github,website}; notes are lossless — primary's notes, then when the duplicate's notes are non-blank and different, append '\n\n---\n\n' + duplicate's. mergeContacts persists all of these columns. headshotUrl carry-over is sufficient for serving because getHeadshotServeScope resolves by any contact row referencing /headshots/:fileId.
