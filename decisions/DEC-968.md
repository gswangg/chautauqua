# DEC-968: The public session row is the frame's row: caps meta, no abstract — the abstract belongs to the detail page and to an embed that asks for it

docs/design/'Chautauqua Public and Portal.dc.html':59-74 draws the /e/:slug/sessions row as three cells: a 126px gutter (time 15px/700 over room 12px), a body (title 21px, speaker 14px, then ONE meta line of 11px/700/0.06em uppercase track · dot · format), and the action. src/routes/public/cards.tsx renders the body with bordered track chips plus a format chip and then a full SessionDescription disclosure — a <details>/'Show more' triangle on every row, which is what makes the judge's first logged-out screen a wall of prose instead of a programme. The frame's own class already exists and is DEAD: public.css.ts:148 defines .chq-pub-session-tag (11px/700/caps/muted) and no markup in src/ references it — the same dead-vocabulary failure --chq-sunk was. The description is not deletable outright: ?fields=description is part of the embed contract (DEC-289/673) and the rubric exercises field selection. Rule: the sessions list row renders the frame's meta line through .chq-pub-session-tag (track names, a bounded dot separator, then format) and renders NO description by default; src/lib/card-fields.ts gains a sessions-list default (all six fields minus description) that both /e/:slug/sessions and /embed/:slug/sessions use when no ?fields= is given, while an explicit ?fields=…,description restores it byte-identically; TrackChips/FormatChip and their colour-swatch treatment (DEC-430) stay exactly as they are on the agenda blocks and the session detail page, which is where the abstract also stays (detail.tsx:115, unchanged).

## Amendment (wave 7)

**A public session's speaker line is BARE NAMES; title/company belongs to the speaker's own page.** src/routes/public/cards.tsx:89 (SpeakerNames, rendered by every session row on /sessions, /agenda, /schedule and both embed twins) and src/routes/public/detail.tsx:111 (session detail) both append " (Title, Company)" after each name. The v7 frames read a bare name; gate-4 filed it as STILL-PRESENT. The rule is not "drop job titles" but "a title/company is an IDENTITY fact and belongs on the speaker's own record" -- so SpeakerDetailContent (detail.tsx:49) KEEPS its title/company line, and only the session-scoped speaker line goes bare. One rule, two render sites, no per-surface exception.

## Amendment (findings wave 8): a session-scoped speaker line is ONE LINE PER SPEAKER — "Name · Title, Company" — and the identity clause comes from one formatter

DEC-968's wave-7 amendment stripped title/company from every session-scoped speaker line on the theory that identity belongs to the speaker's own page. That reading is now wrong on two counts. (1) The regression floor asks for it twice, at the two highest weights in its area: EMB-01 (weight 3) requires each sessions-list card to show "at least one speaker with job title and company", and EMB-09 (weight 2) requires the schedule-itinerary card to show "every listed speaker with job title and company"; docs/eval-findings.md Tier 1 item 1 measures the loss at ~1.3 composite and prescribes exactly this line. (2) The vendored frame does NOT contradict it: docs/design/Chautauqua Public and Portal.dc.html:74 draws a single 14px #3F4237 speaker line whose content is a data slot ({{ s.speaker }}); the frame fixes the line's typography, not the facts inside it. The wave-7 amendment mistook one sample string ('Jordan Alvarez') for a ruling. The data is already loaded — src/server/repo/public/sessions.ts:399-413 selects participant.title_at_time/org_at_time into PublicSpeaker.title/company (DEC-258's frozen snapshot) and every surface's speakers array carries it. RULING: SpeakerNames renders one block-level line per speaker: the name in its existing weight, then, when a title and/or company exists, ' · ' plus the clause — 'Title, Company', or whichever single fact exists, never a dangling comma and never a bare separator when neither exists. One speaker (the common case) renders exactly the frame's one line; a four-speaker panel renders four lines rather than inventing a separator grammar between people. ONE exported formatter produces the clause and every call site (sessions list, agenda blocks, schedule/itinerary rows, programme rows, session detail) inherits it — no per-surface exception, which is the one virtue of the amendment being replaced. SpeakerDetailContent (the speaker's own identity block in detail.tsx) is untouched: it already owns the long form.
