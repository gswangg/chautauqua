# DEC-981: The one active-participant predicate reaches the data that LEAVES the product, and every participant reader is enumerated by a scan rather than spot-checked

src/sync/airtable.ts:168-184 builds each submission's "Speakers" cell from every participant row of the org with no invite-status filter and no ORDER BY. Two defects in one query: (a) a co-presenter who DECLINED is published into the customer's Airtable base as a speaker — precisely the defect DEC-974 just closed on the admin agenda, surviving in the single reader whose output leaves the product and disagrees with both the public programme (visibleParticipantConditions) and admin; (b) the joined string's order is whatever SQLite returns, so an otherwise unchanged submission can be upserted with a permuted Speakers value on the next sync, firing the customer's automations on a non-change — the opposite of J12's promise that their automations fire on real rows. The sync composes ACTIVE_INVITE_STATUSES (src/domain/acceptance.ts:114) and orders by participant.order then contact.id. Because this class of miss is invisible to review — one forgotten WHERE clause among a dozen correct ones — it is institutionalised: a scan enumerates every `.from(schema.participant)` query under src/ and requires each either to compose one of the shared predicates or to appear on an allow-list carrying a per-site reason (the admin submissions list shows every author of a row; merge/import address rows by id).
