EMB-05/08/13 need drill-down detail with bio + per-speaker sessions and full session detail. The public surface is server-rendered JSX with no client framework, so separate SSR pages with a Back link (?from=surface) are the smallest correct implementation and keep the no-login, edge-cacheable property. Name search as a plain GET form (?q=) keeps the pages JS-free and satisfies the EMB-05/EMB-12 search requirement.

## Amendment (wave 59): a public drill-in carries the visitor's narrowing out and brings it back

VERIFIED on main: detail links already carry `?from=<surface>` so Back returns to the right SURFACE (shell.tsx:141-163), and DEC-489's wave-54 `carry` fragment already preserves embed knobs across a drill-in — but `carry` is documented as 'never supplied outside /embed', so on the full-chrome pages a visitor who is on day 2 of the agenda, or inside a track filter, clicks a session and comes back to day 1 with no filter. Honouring a knob on arrival and emitting it on departure are two halves of one contract (the field guide's DEC-489/DEC-851 doctrine); this is the third surface where only one half was built. RULING: `carry` is the visitor's active surface state whatever its origin — embed knobs and narrowing params alike — and is supplied on BOTH bases. It is built with the existing `embedKnobQuery(surface, values)` encoder (src/lib/embed-knobs.ts:95), never a second encoder, so the surface's own EMBED_KNOB_TABLE decides which params may travel and an unhonoured param is dropped rather than emitted. Session and speaker card links pass the surface's active values; the detail route reads the params it was handed and gives them to BackLink, which appends them to the surface path it already builds.
