# DEC-654: Scale is a GATE, not a paragraph: the scale-mandate functional bars are timed, named checks that exit non-zero

docs/mandates/scale-mandate.md's deliverable is "a stress module in the walkthrough (or a gate:scale script) that seeds the aie profile and asserts the functional bars". The aie seed profile exists (perf:seed:aie, DEC-619) and the timing end is being wired to it, but the FUNCTIONAL bars — bulk status over 500 chunked at 100 with no visual rollback, auto-schedule over ~320 reporting a reason for every unplaced item, reminders reporting {sent, skipped, remaining} honestly under the 100 cap, Overview staying at ROW_CAP while totals exceed it — have no executable form, so "holds at AI Engineer scale" is currently a claim. Each bar becomes a pure evaluator with its own unit test plus a runner that names the first failure; AUDIT.md then records a measurement instead of an assertion.
