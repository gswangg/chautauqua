# DEC-493: The walkthrough harness is product evidence: resolve the seeded event by slug, and fill a locked field by the product's own rule

`npm run walkthrough` is the J1-J12 acceptance instrument SPEC.md:374-377 calls "the actual final exam", and at main it reports FAIL on 2 of 6 areas for reasons that are entirely its own. (1) scripts/walkthrough/data.ts:155 resolves "the seeded event" as `eventsBody.items[0]` of a list ordered `desc(startDate)` (src/server/repo/events.ts:71/101); the producer module, which runs immediately before it, creates throwaway events dated 2027-09-01, so `items[0]` is a throwaway event and every J12 export probe reads an empty event. scripts/walkthrough/review.ts already does the right thing (`EVENT_SLUG = "devflow-conf-2027"`, `items.find(e => e.slug === EVENT_SLUG)`). (2) scripts/walkthrough/speaker.ts:657 fills every required non-dropdown/checkbox/number field with the literal string `Walkthrough answer for field ${f.id}`, including the locked `email` field, and src/forms/validate.ts correctly rejects it -- the product is right and the harness is wrong, so the fix is in the harness and must never weaken DEC-454/455's one-email-rule. Decision: the seeded event is resolved by SLUG in every walkthrough module, through one shared loud-failing helper in scripts/walkthrough-lib.ts, and locked fields are filled by their locked short name (src/forms/types.ts `lockedFieldName`), never by field kind alone. A harness that fails for its own reasons is indistinguishable, to the next reader of the log, from a product that fails.
