# DEC-790: The public call-for-papers page offers an account before it needs one

CFP-05 was judged partial solely on the absence of a visible "Create an account" affordance on the public CFP surface. VERIFIED src/routes/public/submit-views.tsx:300 — the claim link ("Create a password to track your submission") exists only on the CONFIRMATION page, and only in the DEC-098 "fresh" state, so a visitor who has not yet submitted sees no route to an account at all, and a returning speaker sees no way back in. Decision: the submit page itself carries one quiet identity line — sign in to the speaker portal, and, for a visitor with no account, the same claim path the confirmation page already computes — with no new token semantics: DEC-098's rule that a claim URL is never rendered for a pre-existing contact is untouched, so the on-page affordance links to /login and the claim URL stays email-only. The mock's track checkboxes are inline with their labels, and the CFP stylesheet is corrected to match rather than centring the box above the text.
