# DEC-961: A terminal CFP page carries the way forward, and the confirmation says how long editing stays open

src/routes/public/submit-views.tsx's ClosedPage (:53) and NotYetOpenPage (:73) render a card with a heading, one sentence and ZERO links. A stranger who follows a shared CFP link one day after the deadline — the single most common way this page is reached — has no route to the event's programme, no route to the hub, and nothing to click at all. The field guide already records that a link is the route it lands on; a page with no links is worse than one with the wrong link. ConfirmationPage (:273) is the other half: SPEC J2 and §5's close-date invariant are that editing stays open until the close date (and accepted speakers keep editing after that), which is the single most useful fact the submitter could be told and the one the server has already computed one branch away — and the page never states it. That branch also prints two consecutive paragraphs (:306-312) that both offer /login, so the pending-existing-contact state says the same thing twice. And the submit form itself offers a returning speaker no route to their account. Rule: ClosedPage and NotYetOpenPage each end with one .chq-cfp-links row — 'Browse the programme ›' at /e/<slug>/sessions and 'All events ›' at /; ConfirmationPage states the edit window in the event's own timezone ('You can edit this until <date>.', or '…until the call for papers closes.' when the form has no close date) and offers 'Submit another talk ›' and 'Browse the programme ›'; the pending-existing-contact branch keeps exactly ONE 'Already have a password? Log in ›' block, the same one the fresh branch renders; and SubmitPage carries one quiet 'Already have an account? Log in ›'. The optionality grammar is unchanged (DEC-917/951: no asterisks) and the new CSS is one value-free rule in cfp.css.ts (DEC-374).
