# DEC-479: mergeContacts owes DEC-456's cascade too: after a merge, user.email equals the surviving contact.email

DEC-456 made patchContact conflict-check and then cascade a new address onto the linked user row, with an explicit stated invariant at src/server/repo/contacts/crud.ts:133-141 that 'login identity never drifts out of sync with the CRM's record of the contact's address'. src/server/repo/contacts/merge.ts:203 repoints user.contact_id from the merged contact to the kept one and never touches user.email, so the identical desync survives by the other door: keep A (a@x, no account) + merge B (b@x, with account) leaves contact A reading a@x and its own user row reading b@x. findAccountUserId (DEC-456) still resolves by contactId so no duplicate account is minted, but the organizer-facing record now shows an address that cannot log in, and every 'use this email' instruction from the CRM is wrong. An invariant stated in one function and violated in its sibling is not an invariant. The same pre-write conflict check patchContact performs applies here, and the cascade runs on the surviving row.
