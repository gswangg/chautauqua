# DEC-822: A saved embed saves the recipe, not just the name — and turning one off is an intentional blank, not a broken page

VERIFIED DEC-785 landed the skeleton and stopped one step short of the point: SavedEmbedsPanel.tsx:49 posts `{name, surface, format: 'iframe', options: {}}` and PATCH /embeds/:id (src/routes/api/embeds.ts:88-109) accepts only `name` and `enabled`, so no saved embed can ever carry a track, day, limit, field set or accent — every knob in EmbedsPanel remains stateless and is lost on reload. A saved embed whose filters cannot be saved is a bookmark with a name on it. docs/design/README.md:276-285 states the whole reason for saving: editing the filters later updates every page the snippet is pasted on. Decision: the builder is the EDITOR of one saved embed — it carries a Name field, heads itself 'Editing · <name>' when opened at ?embed=<id>, and its primary action writes the FULL current parameter set (surface, format, and every knob) through POST/PATCH, with Copy demoted to secondary; the list row states the recipe it stores ('Sessions · iframe · AI Engineering · 6 fields') beside its On/Off pill, under the caption 'Turning one off breaks it wherever it is pasted'. DESIGN OVERRIDES DEC-785 on the disabled case: /embed/e/:id for a DISABLED embed returns an empty 200 (an intentional blank inside someone else's iframe), while an unknown id keeps the designed 404 — a page the organiser switched off must not shout 'not found' on a customer's website.
