# DEC-900: The agenda's page actions live on its title row; a control offered when its action is not needed is clutter

app/src/pages/Agenda.tsx:239-253 stacks a .chq-toolbar band under the title carrying the summary, Auto-schedule, Publish schedule and an 'Add a room or track' link — roughly 170px of chrome the 06-agenda frame does not have, and a direct violation of the standing grammar (primaries never float in their own band; they belong to the title row or a form footer). Rule: the summary and both actions move onto the title row, right-aligned, exactly as every other page's head reads; the toolbar element is deleted, not restyled. The summary states '<N> unplaced · <M> conflicts · <P>% placed' with only the conflict count bolded, since bolding everything ranks nothing. 'Add a room or track' does not survive as a permanent link: rooms and tracks are Settings' job and the nav already reaches it, so the link renders ONLY in the grid's empty state (an event with no rooms cannot place anything, which is the one moment the organizer needs it) — conditional-and-quiet, not a fourth control competing with Publish.
