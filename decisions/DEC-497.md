# DEC-497: Phone and render evidence predating the embed rework is not evidence about it

The most recent phone-width pass in this round (docs/verification-log/task-w21-d-walkthrough-stage1.md STEP 4, seven surfaces at 390x844) was cut at sha bf56ba7. Since then DEC-489/490 (task-w24-b/c, merged mid-plan) changed what every /embed/* surface accepts and what the producer-side embed generator offers -- i.e. exactly the surfaces the phone pass covers and the admin panel that emits them. SPEC.md:374-377 makes the phone pass part of the acceptance bar, and SPEC.md:162-169 makes embeds a first-class product surface. Decision: the render/phone evidence for the closing ledger is re-cut at a sha that contains the embed knob rework, using the existing `npm run gate:render-sweep` gate (route manifest + 390x844 overflow + tap-target + font-floor + contrast) rather than a hand pass, and any public/embed surface absent from app/src/routeManifest.ts is added there by that same lane -- a manifest that omits a surface makes the gate's green a claim about a smaller product than the one being shipped.
