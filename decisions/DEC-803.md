# DEC-803: A pipeline card states how long it has sat there, and a decline states why

VERIFIED app/src/pages/contacts/PipelineBoard.tsx contains no age, date or reason text at all, and src/server/repo/pipeline.ts's list projection returns only (id, contact, stage, updatedAt); moveEntry (:255) hard-codes `body: null` on every move activity even though pipeline_activity already HAS a body column. docs/design/README.md is explicit on both points: every card shows its age derived from the newest move ('Added 6 days ago', 'No reply · N days' on contacted, bold caps past 30 days) because silence is the most common outcome of outreach and nobody sets it as a stage; and `declined` carries a reason because the stage conflates 'they declined, no capacity' with 'we passed, off-topic' — same column, opposite follow-up next year. Decision: entry.updatedAt IS the moment the card entered its current stage (only moveEntry writes it; addNote does not), so it is returned as `stageSince` rather than inventing a second timestamp; the decline reason is the move activity's own body, so declining requires a reason (loud field error, never a silent null) and no schema change is needed; and the caption text is a pure per-stage function in the app so the wording and the past-30-days emphasis are testable without a DOM. Emphasis is weight and wording, never colour (there is no red in this product).

## Amendment (wave 55): enrolling a contact straight into `declined` carries a reason, exactly as moving into it does

src/routes/api/pipeline.ts:219-224 refuses a PATCH move into 'declined' with a blank reason because the stage conflates "they declined" with "we passed" — the handoff's own words. Its sibling POST /pipeline at :112-153 validates the stage against the closed set and nothing else, so an enroll at stage:'declined' mints a reasonless declined entry, and :147 hard-codes declineReason:null into the 201 body regardless. The enroll dialog offers all five stage chips, so this is the ordinary path, not an API-only edge. A guard on the move route and none on the create route is the same predicate-family gap the swarm has closed five times: the create path must carry the move path's rule, and enrollContact must write the reason onto the enrol move activity so declineReasonForEntry finds it.
