# DEC-706: A section's action is a link on its rule; a filled primary lives on the title row or a form footer; a row is chosen by clicking the row, never by a radio

The Review landing shows three symptoms of one missing rule: a lone New-plan band floating above the list, "Remind laggards (N)" as a filled primary in its own toolbar, and radio circles bolted onto plan rows to express selection. The design pack never draws a filled button in its own band -- primaries sit on the title row beside the h1 or in a form's footer, and a section's own action is a right-aligned tertiary LINK on that section's 2px rule. Selection of a list row is expressed by the row itself (quiet active state, aria-current) because a radio imports a form control's grammar into a navigation surface and doubles the click target. Stating this once, as a rule, stops each page from re-inventing button placement -- the same class of fix as DEC-651 (one dialog) and the button-tier vocabulary.
