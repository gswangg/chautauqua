# DEC-983: A visibility predicate is applied WHOLE or not at all, and an ALLOWED reason may never park a known defect

DEC-981's scan landed with two entries in its ALLOWED map whose reason is literally "pre-existing gap outside this task's scope, flagged for follow-up" — a defect with a comment, which is the same failure mode as a decision with no code. Both are real: (a) src/server/repo/files-library.ts:414-416 picks the Content library row's LEAD SPEAKER with role='speaker' and no invite filter, so a co-presenter who DECLINED and holds order 0 is printed as the session's speaker on the admin surface that names who owns a deliverable — precisely the defect DEC-974 closed on the agenda and DEC-981 closed on the Airtable sync, surviving one file further along; (b) src/server/repo/review/submissions.ts's listSpeakerNamesForSubmissions declares itself a "visible-participant" read in its own doc comment and then applies only HALF of DEC-274's visibleParticipantConditions() — participant.visible=1 with no invite_status clause — so the review results table and its CSV export name a speaker the public programme omits. A half-applied predicate is worse than none: it looks filtered. Rule: a reader that filters participants at all composes one of the shared predicates entire (acceptedSpeakerConditions / visibleParticipantConditions), never a hand-picked column from one; a reader that intends every author filters nothing and says so. The two follow-up allowances are DELETED rather than reworded, and the scan's stale-allowance assertion is what makes that stick.
