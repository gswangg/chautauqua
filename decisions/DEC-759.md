# DEC-759: A placed agenda card sizes to its content and overlapping sessions split the slot into lanes — no card is an inner scroll region

DEC-620 fixed clipped cards by making .chq-day-grid-placed-card and .chq-session-card scroll internally (agenda.css:229, 251). That trades an invisible clip for an invisible scroll: a 30-minute conflicted card hides its clash caption behind a scrollbar the organiser has no reason to expect, which is why the conflict cell reads as broken. The schedule's whole job is to surface conflicts without blocking the write (J9), so the reveal has to be readable at rest. The row, not the card, absorbs the overflow: time rows become minmax(slot-height, auto) so a row grows to its tallest card, and two sessions in the same room+slot render as side-by-side lanes at 1/N width rather than one card stacked invisibly over another. The conflict caption stays type on the card (DEC-367: never colour, never red). A scan test forbids overflow-y:auto on any placed-card rule so the remedy cannot come back the next time a card is tight — a control the organiser has to discover by scrolling inside it is not a control.
