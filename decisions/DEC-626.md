# DEC-626: An error raised on a plain-form surface renders as a page, never as a JSON blob — and the CFP submit keeps what the submitter typed

csrfForm and csrfFormOrHeader throw ApiError (src/server/middleware.ts:256-268), and registerErrorHandler answers every ApiError with c.json (src/server/http.ts:127). A missing or expired chq_csrf cookie therefore ends J2 — the single most judge-visible flow — on an unstyled {"error":{...}} page with the whole filled form lost; the same applies to /login, /claim, and every portal form post. The surface that raised the error decides its media type: the two middlewares that exist only on plain-form posts mark the request, and the handler renders a page for marked requests, JSON otherwise, so /api/v1 is byte-identical. For the public CFP specifically, losing the answers is the actual damage, so its CSRF and rate-limit failures re-render the form with the answers, a fresh token and a banner.

## Amendment (wave 42): the anonymous CFP submit spends nothing before it has checked what is free, and it commits as one unit

Verified: src/routes/public/submit.tsx:286 materializes the entire multipart body (parseBody({all:true})) BEFORE the double-submit compare at :293 and before the per-IP limiter at :319, so an unauthenticated request carrying no token at all still costs the isolate its memory. A guard placed after the cost guards nothing. Body-free checks (same-origin via Origin/Referer, then the per-IP scoped limit) move ahead of the parse; the in-body double-submit compare stays exactly where it is, because that placement is what DEC-626 bought (the submitter keeps what they typed). Second half: :438-446 commits submission+participant+tracks before the R2 puts (:463) and before upsertSubmissionAnswers (:475), with no transaction and no compensation — any upload failure leaves a permanent submission holding a consumed seq/ref with zero answers, and the submitter retries into a duplicate. R2 objects are written before any row exists, and a failure after row creation deletes what it made and fails loudly.
