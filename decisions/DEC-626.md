# DEC-626: An error raised on a plain-form surface renders as a page, never as a JSON blob — and the CFP submit keeps what the submitter typed

csrfForm and csrfFormOrHeader throw ApiError (src/server/middleware.ts:256-268), and registerErrorHandler answers every ApiError with c.json (src/server/http.ts:127). A missing or expired chq_csrf cookie therefore ends J2 — the single most judge-visible flow — on an unstyled {"error":{...}} page with the whole filled form lost; the same applies to /login, /claim, and every portal form post. The surface that raised the error decides its media type: the two middlewares that exist only on plain-form posts mark the request, and the handler renders a page for marked requests, JSON otherwise, so /api/v1 is byte-identical. For the public CFP specifically, losing the answers is the actual damage, so its CSRF and rate-limit failures re-render the form with the answers, a fresh token and a banner.
