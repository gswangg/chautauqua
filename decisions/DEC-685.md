# DEC-685: A dialog's form is one shared FormRow inside ModalFrame — labels above, inputs at the modal's full measure

Mandate item 25 (user QA, orchestrator-verified with a screenshot): the bulk-email modal renders labels BESIDE inputs at drifting baselines ("Body" floating at its textarea's bottom-left), inputs at arbitrary non-matching widths, half the modal dead space. Verified: no FormRow exists anywhere in app/src — every modal hand-rolls its own label/input arrangement, which is precisely how the frame drifted before DEC-651 made ModalFrame the ONE dialog container. The fix is the same shape as DEC-651: one exported FormRow (label above, help/caption slot, error slot, control at full measure) living beside ModalFrame, and the dialogs migrated onto it, so a new dialog inherits the form skeleton instead of inventing one. Honesty captions already present stay exactly as they are — this decision governs layout, never copy.
