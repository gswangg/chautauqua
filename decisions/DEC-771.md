# DEC-771: The seed is the grader package: nothing collides on the identity a human matches by, and every screen shows its own shape

SPEC §8 makes the seed the grader package -- "every screen a judge opens is filled". Three graders reported the opposite: two accepted sessions share the title "Taming 40-Minute CI" so later graders matching by title hit both; a duplicate "Confirm participation" task; Priya carries two contact records; the Content worklist is 28/30 "No files yet"; Comms has no multi-recipient batch and too few templates. Decision: the seed guarantees (a) no two submissions in an event share a title, (b) no two contacts in an org share a normalized email or a full name, (c) no two tasks in an event share a title, and (d) enough density that each worklist shows its non-empty shape -- roughly a third of accepted sessions carry files with at least one multi-version chain and one comment thread, at least one ~20-recipient comms batch, and five templates. A seed test ASSERTS these by enumeration over the seeded rows, never by sampling (field guide: universal rows graded from ENUMERATION never sample).
