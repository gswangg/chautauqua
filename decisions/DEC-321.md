# DEC-321: The default CFP form collects job title, company and bio as OPTIONAL locked speaker fields backed by contact columns; blank-only fill on an existing contact, and the frozen participant snapshot is taken from the resolved values

Verified gap: createDefaultForm (src/server/repo/forms.ts:99) emits exactly five locked fields — title, description, first_name, last_name, email — and nothing else. So a real public-CFP submitter never supplies a job title, company or bio; contact.title/company/bio stay NULL, participant.title_at_time/org_at_time are frozen NULL at src/routes/public/submit.tsx:537-538, and SPEC.md:164's public speakers list ('headshot/title/company') plus the speakers CSV render blank for everyone. docs/fixtures seed those values directly, which is precisely why no test or walkthrough has ever caught it, and docs/sessionboard-reference/01-call-for-papers.md:19 lists speaker bio among the default form's fields. DEC-299's backfillNullAttribution only repairs this AFTER a speaker signs into the portal and saves a profile — which for most submitters never happens, and cannot happen before acceptance. Fix at the source: extend LOCKED_SPEAKER_FIELDS (src/forms/types.ts:42) with job_title, company and bio, backed by the real contact columns per DEC-016 ('locked = real cols') exactly as first_name/last_name/email already are, NOT as submission_answer blobs. Two deliberate calls. (a) required: false for all three — the reference doc makes bio required, but flipping any existing CFP field to required would break every test, fixture and walkthrough that posts the form today, and the producer can tick `required` in the builder in one click; shipping the fields is what unblocks the surface, and 'settings get defaults, not wizards' (SPEC 2.1, DEC-301's precedent). (b) On an EXISTING contact the submitted values fill only blank columns and never overwrite a non-empty stored value: the speaker's own saved profile outranks whatever they retyped into a CFP form months later, and this keeps the write idempotent across repeat submissions. participant.title_at_time/org_at_time are then frozen from the RESOLVED (post-fill) contact values, so the DEC-258 snapshot is real from minute one.
