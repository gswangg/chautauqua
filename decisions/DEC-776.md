# DEC-776: Overdue is ONE predicate, over the roster the grid can actually show

VERIFIED: src/server/repo/tasks/grid.ts:245-253 counts overdue assignments across the whole event (join task only, no roster condition) while its ROWS are gated by acceptedSpeakerExistsForContact (DEC-754); src/server/repo/overview.ts:117 runs the same event-wide count for the nav badge, and :151 builds the Overview detail rows with `status = 'pending'` while the count beside them uses `status <> 'complete'`. So an assignment belonging to a contact who is not on the accepted roster (exactly what duplicate contacts and removed participants produce) is counted in three places and displayable in none -- the probe's "9 on Speakers vs 12 in nav". Decision: ONE exported condition builder in src/server/repo/tasks/crud.ts -- not complete AND due date in the past AND the contact satisfies acceptedSpeakerExistsForContact -- composed by grid.ts's counts, overview.ts's aggregate AND overview.ts's detail rows, so the badge, the page stat and the rows are the same set. A count is a promise that a list of that length exists.

## Amendment (wave 61): every J6 "outstanding" number -- and the speaker's own task list -- composes the chase predicate, not just `overdue`

grid.ts renders three numbers side by side: "N accepted · M tasks open · K overdue" (OnboardingGrid.tsx:503-504). `speakers` composes rosterParticipantExistsForContact; `overdue` composes overdueAssignmentConditions -> acceptedSpeakerExistsForContact; but the countsRow query at grid.ts:377-384 has NO predicate beyond `task.event_id = ?`. So a contact who declined their invite, or whose submission left 'accepted', keeps inflating "M tasks open" forever while both neighbours correctly exclude them -- DEC-776's own rationale ("a task_assignment for a contact who is no longer an accepted speaker must not inflate this count") applied to one of three siblings. The same population error runs the other way in the portal: getMyTaskAssignments (portal/tasks.ts:44-68) filters only by contactId+orgId, so a declined speaker still sees "Hotel stay requirement form -- REQUIRED, overdue" on their own portal page even though wave 59 (DEC-829) already stopped emailing them about it. RULING: the chase predicate -- an accepted submission with an ACTIVE invite -- is the single definition of "owes something", and it governs outstandingRequired, outstandingContacts and the portal task list. `speakers` stays the wider roster count (it heads the rows, which include invited/declined). A contact who becomes chaseable again gets the same rows back; nothing is deleted.
