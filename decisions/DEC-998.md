# DEC-998: The Content detail links straight into the submission's edit form, its history and its speakers — which means those two panels become URL state

GATE-2 recorded CNT at 73.9 (was 88.6) as PURE TURN BUDGET: the scenario died before reaching session-edit, revision history and speaker bio/headshot, all of which the product HAS — SubmissionDetailPage.tsx holds a title/abstract editor (startEditing/saveEdit, PATCH /submissions/:id) and a history timeline (loadHistory, GET /submissions/:id/history) behind local useState, and ContactsApp already deep-links a contact drawer through ?openContact=<id> (ContactsApp.tsx:157). Nothing links to any of it from /admin/content/:submissionId, whose header renders a title and a plain-text speaker subtitle and no action at all. Click depth is worth more than several features here, and the cheapest depth to remove is the depth that exists only because a panel's open/closed state was never addressable. So the two panels move to URL state (?edit=1, ?history=1 — the DEC-710/DEC-728 precedent that made settings drills and comms tabs bookmarkable), and the Content detail grows one quiet action row that lands directly ON the open editor, ON the open history, and ON the speaker's own record. A link is the route it LANDS on: linking to the page and hoping the reader finds the disclosure is what cost the turns.
