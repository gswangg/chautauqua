# DEC-668: The seed carries three evaluation plans in distinct lifecycle states, because a plans list of one cannot demonstrate the state vocabulary it renders

scripts/seed.ts creates exactly one evaluation_plan (:826), open across SEED_NOW. The Review landing's plans list, its progress bars and its closed/open/upcoming vocabulary therefore cannot be compared against the mock or exercised by a judge -- both the fidelity agent and the user's manual QA blocked on this. Three plans (one closed and fully evaluated, one open mid-wave, one not yet open) is the smallest set that makes every state on that page real. Plan 1 keeps its existing id and its existing reviewers/evaluations so nothing that references seed_evaluation_plan_0001 moves; the new plans take fresh seedId indices. Every new instant is a SEED_NOW offset (DEC-591's one clock).
