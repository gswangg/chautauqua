# DEC-429: Stage-1 dependency-advisory policy: devDependency advisories are recorded, not chased; a runtime-dependency advisory is a lane

docs/verification-log/task-w10-f-build-test-redesign.md:249-256 records "npm audit reports 4 vulnerabilities (2 moderate, 2 high)" as OPEN ITEM 1 and correctly notes that no decision in decisions/ covers dependency-audit policy, so every future build/test gate lane will re-raise the same count. Deciding it here rather than spending a lane: package.json:19-22 declares exactly two runtime dependencies, hono and drizzle-orm; everything else (vite, wrangler, playwright, vitest, tsx, esbuild and their transitive trees) is a devDependency that never reaches the Worker bundle, so an advisory against it cannot affect the deployed artifact, while force-upgrading that chain risks the build for zero product benefit. Policy: a gate lane CLASSIFIES each advisory by whether its package can reach the Worker bundle and records the classification; a devDependency-only advisory is recorded and closed in the same breath, not carried forward as an open item; an advisory against hono, drizzle-orm, or anything that ends up in the shipped bundle is a stage-1 fix and gets its own lane. No lane this wave.
