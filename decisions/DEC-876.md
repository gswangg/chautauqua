# DEC-876: A send's body is a detail read, not a widened list: comms history opens one message and shows what was actually sent

DEC-543 deliberately keeps LIST_COLUMNS narrow (src/server/repo/email.ts:103-115) — the email-log list selects id/contact/toEmail/subject/status/sentAt and drops bodyText — and that ruling stands: a list endpoint must not ship a column none of its rows render. But the consequence today is that the Comms history shows no body ANYWHERE, so a 23-recipient fan-out whose per-recipient rendering genuinely differs is unevidenced and a failed send is unauditable in substance (SPEC J5 wants the history to be the record of what was sent). The detail read already exists and is already org-scoped: GET /api/v1/events/:eventId/email-log/:emailId (src/routes/comms.ts:713) returning SELECTED_COLUMNS including subject/bodyText. Decision: the expanded batch view in app/src/pages/comms/HistoryTab.tsx gains a per-row Open control that fetches THAT message and renders its own rendered subject and body text — one message at a time, no list widening, no client-side re-render of merge fields (the stored text is the truth of what was sent, tokens already resolved). A failed row opens the same way, since the point of the audit trail is that an attempted send is still a record.
