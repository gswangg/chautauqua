# DEC-101: Contact merge repoints all six contact-FK columns — adding file.uploaded_by_contact_id and file_comment.author_contact_id — and dedupes same-submission participants before the participant repoint (supersedes DEC-026's four-table list)

mergeContacts (src/server/repo/contacts.ts:489-502) repoints participant/task_assignment/email_log/user then deletes the merged contact, leaving file.uploaded_by_contact_id (schema.ts:495) and file_comment.author_contact_id (schema.ts:510) dangling — J8 version history and comment threads silently reference a deleted contact (no FK enforcement), violating fail-loudly. Ruling: buildMergeRepointOps returns six ops ("file" and "file_comment" added to the union) and mergeContacts executes all six. Additionally, when both contacts are participants on the same submission, a blind repoint produces duplicate participant rows (no unique index guards participant(submission_id, contact_id)), double-listing the speaker publicly; so BEFORE the participant repoint, the merged contact's participant rows on submissions where the kept contact already participates are deleted (the kept contact's row wins). Order is load-bearing: dedupe-delete, then repoint, then delete the contact row.
