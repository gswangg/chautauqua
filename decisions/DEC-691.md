# DEC-691: Settings is a read-only SUMMARY over the mock's seven sections; each section is a summary plus a drill-in, and Public pages is first-class with the embed builder reachable FROM it

The user names Settings the most divergent surface in the app (mandate item 26) and item 39 gives the target. Verified: app/src/pages/Settings.tsx:31-42 lists TEN rail sections (Event, Call for papers, Portal, Tracks and rooms, Resources, People and roles, API tokens, Exports, Embeds, Import from Sessionboard) and every one of them lands the organizer directly in an edit-everything form. The mock (docs/design/Chautauqua Settings.dc.html:61-215) shows SEVEN read-only sections — Event · Call for papers · Tracks and rooms · Public pages and embeds · Speaker portal · People and roles · Your data — each a label/value grid with a single drill-in link in its header ("Edit the form", "Add", "Open as a speaker", "Invite someone", "Replace", "Embed code"). Two of the mock's sections are compositions of panels this build already has (Speaker portal = Portal + Resources; Your data = Exports + API tokens) and one, Public pages, does not exist at all: the Embeds builder replaced it, which is backwards — a list of the live public pages is what an organizer looks for, and the embed snippet is a thing you reach FROM a page, not instead of it. Sessionboard import stays as an honestly-labelled extra section rather than being smuggled into one of the seven.
