# DEC-910: A role count counts the role — 'everyone who is not an organiser' is not a reviewer

app/src/pages/settings/PeopleRolesPanel.tsx:135 computes `reviewerCount = users.filter(u => u.role !== 'organizer').length`. The user vocabulary has three roles (organizer, reviewer, speaker — App.tsx:164, src/routes/api middleware), so every speaker with an account is counted and printed as a reviewer. The panel then hangs a hint off that number about per-track review scoping, which makes the lie load-bearing: an organiser reads 'Reviewers 6' and staffs a review wave against it. This is the same defect the field guide already names twice — the printed number must be its own query's arithmetic, and a negation over an open set silently absorbs every member nobody thought about. Rule: counts are derived by ENUMERATING the role vocabulary and grouping, never by two hand-written complementary predicates; a role's count is the count of users holding that role, and a role with no members either renders zero or does not render, but is never folded into its neighbour.
