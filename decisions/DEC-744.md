# DEC-744: One page measure: `--chq-measure` (820px) is the only desktop clamp a single-column subscreen may use; a page with an aside fills the frame

Verified on main: app/src/styles.css has `.chq-measure { max-width: 820px }` but no token, and page CSS invents its own clamps — forms.css:54/234 (720px) and review.css:542/763 (660px) — so the form builder and plan editor render a narrow left-hugging column inside a 1372px frame while the design's own subscreen frames are 900px wide (docs/design/Chautauqua Submissions.dc.html:358, Chautauqua Review.dc.html:251), i.e. ~836px of content. One token (`--chq-measure: 820px`) declared in :root and consumed by `.chq-measure` makes the measure a fact instead of four opinions, and a source-scan test in the established app/src/shell-geometry.test.ts style keeps a future page from re-inventing a literal. Prose measures in `ch` and modal widths are NOT page measures and stay as they are. Two-column detail pages (submission detail, session detail) are out of this decision's reach — they fill the frame with a grid, and they are being rebuilt in the in-flight wave.
