# DEC-799: A ratchet counts only the promises actually made

VERIFIED src/routes/review/plans.ts:210-218 refuses to turn anonymity off when countSubmittedEvaluationsForPlan (src/server/repo/review/plans.ts:238) is non-zero — but that counts EVERY submitted evaluation on the plan, including ones submitted before anonymity was ever switched on. So a plan that collected reviews openly and then had anonymity enabled for a moment is locked into anonymity forever, on the strength of a promise never made to those reviewers. The guard needs a fact the schema does not yet carry. Decision: evaluation_plan records anonymized_at, set when anonymity transitions off->on and cleared when it is legitimately switched off; the ratchet counts only evaluations submitted at or after that instant. Anonymity remains a one-way door for everyone who reviewed under it, and no one else. Same surface, same wave: the results column that already computes a weighted number is labelled "Weighted score", because a column heading is the only place the reader learns what the number means.
