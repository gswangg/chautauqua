# DEC-259: Wave-3 lanes are fix-authorized inside an owned file list, log as task-w3-<x>-c2-<scope>.md, and are evidence rather than battery sections

Two problems to settle centrally. (1) Naming: campaign-1 already committed docs/verification-log/task-w3-{a,b,c,d,e,f}-*.md (wave2-closeout, build-test, walkthrough, perf-smoke, interim-gate, spec-audit, triage-closure). Reusing task-w3-<x>-<scope>.md would collide on disk and defeat DEC-129's homonym guard. Every campaign-2 wave-3 log therefore carries a mandatory '-c2-' infix: docs/verification-log/task-w3-<x>-c2-<scope>.md, headed '# task-w3-<x> - <scope> @ <sha>' with the base sha, and ending with the two greppable lines 'OPEN ITEMS: n' and 'RESULT: PASS|FAIL'.

(2) Authority: DEC-256 made wave-2 battery lanes strictly read-only and said "a lane that finds a defect LOGS it; fixing it is next wave's work". Wave 3 IS that next wave, so its lanes MUST fix what they find — but only inside the owned file list named in their own task, and every fix must land with a vitest regression test that fails before and passes after. A defect outside the owned list is recorded as an OPEN ITEM with file:line and left for the wave-4 planner; no lane edits another lane's files, decisions/, field-guide/, docs/eval-findings.md, or another lane's log. Wave-3 logs are evidence about a J-area, NOT DEC-256 battery sections: they never claim a FROZEN SHA and they can never by themselves satisfy the DEC-069/DEC-256 exit predicate.
