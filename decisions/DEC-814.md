# DEC-814: An unverified stranger may name a contact, never edit one

The public CFP is anonymous by design and matching a repeat submitter by lower(email) is right — one person must not become two contacts. But DEC-321(b) went one step further: src/routes/public/submit.tsx:405-434 calls fillContactProfileIfBlank (src/server/repo/submit.ts:170-197) so an anonymous submission WRITES title/company/bio into a matched, real CRM contact whenever those columns are blank, with no verification anywhere on the path. Anyone who guesses a known speaker's address can author that speaker's CRM profile. Decision: the fill-if-blank write survives only for a contact this submission just created; for a pre-existing contact the submitted job title and company are snapshotted onto the PARTICIPANT row (titleAtTime/orgAtTime — already the per-submission snapshot, DEC-258) and the bio stays a submission answer. The contact row is untouched by strangers. DEC-321's actual benefit — a real profile for a genuinely new submitter — is unchanged, the organiser still reads the submitted values on the submission, and fillContactProfileIfBlank is deleted rather than left as a shim.
