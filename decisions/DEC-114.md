# DEC-114: Mechanical code-bearing test for DEC-069/091 sha derivation: first-parent diff outside the bookkeeping set; empty re-merges are non-code-bearing

The merge train rewound and re-merged branches this cycle (reflog shows duplicate "merge task-w9-a/b" and "merge task-w10-a" entries), and re-merging an already-incorporated branch produces a merge commit with no tree change. Without a mechanical rule such a commit could be read as voiding every gate section and force an endless re-verification loop. Extending DEC-076/077/090: a commit is code-bearing iff `git diff --name-only <sha>^ <sha>` (first parent for merges) lists at least one path outside docs/verification-log.md, docs/verification-log/**, docs/eval-findings.md, field-guide/**, decisions/**, and pure string-constant appends to src/decisions.ts. A merge commit whose first-parent diff is empty, or empty after excluding that set, is NOT code-bearing and does not void gate sections. Gate workers apply this rule when re-deriving the newest code-bearing sha per DEC-091 and when auditing post-sha commits in triage-closure.
