# DEC-838: A knob bound to nothing is a lie; the accent binds only where the default already renders that colour

VERIFIED the per-event accent is offered everywhere and consumed almost nowhere. --chq-brandable-accent is SET by the public shell (src/routes/public/shell.tsx:143), the embed shell (:180), the portal (portal/shared.tsx:74) and the CFP (submit-views.tsx:44), and the embed builder offers an Accent color field for every surface (DEC-817 just taught its parser to accept the '#' its own placeholder shows) — but the property is READ by exactly two rules in the whole codebase: src/routes/public/public.css.ts:183 (one border-left) and cfp.css.ts:25 (.chq-cfp-header border-top). So an organiser who sets an accent and embeds Sessions, Speakers, Gallery, Agenda or Schedule sees no difference at all, which is the EMB grader's 'accent color no-op'. Decision: the accent must reach at least one visible decoration on EVERY surface in the isSurface vocabulary and on its /embed twin — and it may be bound ONLY to decorations whose currently-rendered colour is already the brand olive, because src/views/theme.ts:49 defaults --chq-brandable-accent to #4E5C31; that constraint keeps every v5 mock byte-identical at the default while making the custom value the only visible change, so this cannot regress fidelity. It is never bound to body text or to a filled control (contrast is not a knob an organiser may break), and adoption is proved by ENUMERATION: a test renders each surface with a non-default accent and asserts the emitted HTML carries an element from the accent-bound class set.
