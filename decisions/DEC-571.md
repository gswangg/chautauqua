# DEC-571: Agenda cards clip to their slot; track identity is carried by type, not by a coloured border

Twelve of twelve placed cards bleed 29–190px into the next row because .chq-day-grid-placed-card sets height:100% on a 24px grid row with no overflow rule, and the card is a flex column that grows past its box. One CSS fix: overflow:hidden + min-height:0 on .chq-session-card and the placed-card modifier, with the title clamped. Separately, SessionCard currently paints the track's seeded hex (#2563eb blue, #16a34a green, #d97706 amber) as a left border, which reintroduces exactly the multi-accent palette DEC-367 removed ("There is no red, and no third accent") and makes track the one meaning carried by colour alone. Accent is always olive (--chq-brand), ink when conflicted; the track is named in text on the card. Track.color stays in the data model and on the surfaces whose mocks use it — this decision governs the admin agenda grid only.

## Amendment (wave 72): a session card is ref · duration / title / speaker — the duration comes inside the card and the track line leaves it

Frame 06's tray anatomy, three findings verified on main. (a) `· 30 min` renders as a dangling sibling paragraph OUTSIDE the card (UnscheduledTray.tsx:103) when the frame prints it inline on the ref line, `DFC-033 · 10 min` — a fact about the card floating beside the card. (b) The card carries a `.chq-session-card-tracks` line (SessionCard.tsx:91-93) that the frame's cards do not have, in either the tray or the grid; track identity already reaches the organiser through the filter bar and the detail page, and DEC-367/571 already forbid a colour swatch for it. (c) The count reads `Unscheduled (6)` where the frame prints a bare right-aligned `6`, and the tray hint is permanently clipped to "…click Uns…" by `white-space: nowrap` + ellipsis in a fixed 268px tray (agenda.css:697-705) while the frame's copy simply wraps. Ruling: SessionCard takes an optional `durationMin?: number` and renders `{ref} · {n} min` on the head line when present; the tracks line and the now-unused `tracks` prop are removed from the card and its three call sites; the tray count is bare; the hint wraps. Nothing about drag/drop, arming, accessible names or ConflictChip changes.
