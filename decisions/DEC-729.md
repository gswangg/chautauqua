# DEC-729: The portal shows every submission the speaker owns, with its status — pending and declined included

Verified on main: /portal renders 'Your session' from getMySessions (accepted only) and there is no `/portal/submissions` list route, while the detail page links back to a 'My Submissions' page that does not exist. A speaker therefore cannot track a pending submission and never learns of a decline — the CFP grader's P1 and a direct J2/J7 failure ('always knows what's owed'). The portal must list every submission where the speaker holds a participant row, newest first, with the DEC-016 public status label (never accept_queue/decline_queue), each linked to its detail page; the 'N things to do' worklist stays on top.
