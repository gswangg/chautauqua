# DEC-432: Event scope belongs in the WHERE: the two surviving JS post-filters over unbounded row sets move into SQL

Two survivors of the DEC-333/DEC-418 sweep, both on hot reads carrying SPEC.md:331's p95 budgets. src/server/repo/portal.ts:686-701 (getMyResources) SELECTs every resource row in the ORG — all events, every year — and then keeps only the speaker's with rows.filter((r) => eventIds.includes(r.eventId)); the scope belongs in the WHERE as inArray(resource.eventId, eventIds) batched through the repo's existing chunkIds convention, with the position ordering in orderBy. src/server/repo/contacts/stats.ts:30-40 (getContactStats) returns one row per participating contact for the whole org and computes returningSpeakers as .filter(...).length; it becomes a single count(*) over a GROUP BY contact_id HAVING count(distinct event_id) > 1 subquery. Both are the case SPEC.md:193-195 forbids ("a feature that demos well with 10 rows and dies at 2,000 is not built") at the org scale target of 200-800 speakers across multiple events, and both are inconsistent rather than intentional — the admin list at src/server/repo/submissions/list.ts and the public lists after DEC-418 already do this correctly.
