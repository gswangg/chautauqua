# DEC-955: A merge chip whose value depends on an attachment toggle reveals that toggle where the chip is

{feedback} is a member of COMPOSE_MERGE_FIELDS (src/mail/render.ts:33), so the composer offers it as an insertable chip on the template step. But buildMergeVars (src/domain/compose.ts:141) OMITS the `feedback` key entirely unless feedbackComments is non-null, which requires includeFeedback AND feedbackPlanId — both of which live on the NEXT step (ComposeWizard.tsx:607-633, the preview step's Attachments panel). An organiser who takes the chip the product offered gets a 'missing merge fields: feedback' rejection when they press Next, from a control they have not been shown yet. That is a circular gate, and DEC-682's security posture (never invent or leak another plan's feedback) is not what is wrong — the placement is. This extends the house rule already recorded in the field guide: a toggle needing a companion choice REVEALS it and WAITS. Rule: on the template step, when the body text contains {feedback} and includeFeedback is off, the plan picker is revealed inline beneath the merge-chip row captioned '{feedback} needs a plan'; choosing a plan sets includeFeedback and feedbackPlanId, so the preview call carries them and cannot be rejected for that field. If the event has zero evaluation plans the {feedback} chip is not offered at all — a chip that no configuration can satisfy is a trap. The Attachments panel on the preview step keeps both controls (the mock puts them there) and stays the single source of that state.
