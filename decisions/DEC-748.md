# DEC-748: The merge screen shows EVERY differing field, including value-versus-empty, and the duplicate rule is stated where the pairs are listed

Verified in src/domain/contacts.ts:353 — `if (dupVal === "" || dupVal === beforeVal) continue;` drops a field entirely when the DUPLICATE side is empty, so a pair whose primary carries AcmeCo/Engineer against a blank duplicate renders only the Email row and the organiser commits an irreversible merge without seeing what is on the table. The design's merge column is explicit that the discarded value is the evidence for the decision (docs/design/README.md: the struck value exists precisely because merge cannot be undone), so an empty side renders as a struck em dash rather than vanishing; the `fill` case (primary empty, duplicate has a value) already produces a row and keeps it. The screen also gains the Labels row that DEC-738's server-side formatter exists to serve — combined, not chosen, per the pack — a "1 of N pairs" counter, and a keep column headed by the record's own name instead of a generic DISCARD radio. Finally, DEC-143's matching rule (same email, OR same name at the same company, with a blank company matching any) is affirmed rather than widened — pairs at different named companies are usually different people — but it must be SAID on the Duplicates tab, because a detection rule the organiser cannot read looks like a bug.
