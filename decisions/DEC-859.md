# DEC-859: The add-to-the-pipeline dialog is the frame's dialog: ModalFrame, chips, and the consequence stated before the write

VERIFIED against docs/design/README.md 'Contacts — pipeline stages' (v5, authoritative): 'The enroll dialog sets stage (the five chips), fit (1–5, optional), and a one-line "Why them" shown on the card, and states the two things that surprise people: adding writes a move to the activity feed, and no email is sent'; and 'Every card therefore shows Fit 5 (olive, same family as the review scorecard's score pills) or a dashed Unrated'. On main, PipelineBoard.tsx:379-441 hand-rolls .chq-scrim/.chq-modal instead of ModalFrame (which this very file already imports for its decline-reason dialog), titles itself 'Enroll a contact', renders stage and fit as <select> elements, and states neither consequence — while the card's rated pill (PipelineBoard.tsx:308, contacts-panels.css:145) takes the neutral .chq-pill default rather than the olive family. Decision: one dialog vocabulary (ModalFrame), one label vocabulary — the trigger and the dialog title both read 'Add to the pipeline', because a button and the dialog it opens naming two different things is the maze the product exists to beat; stage and fit render as chip rows reusing the .chq-segmented radio-group markup the submission-detail status control already uses, so no third chip vocabulary is invented; the footer states both surprises verbatim ('Adding writes a move to the activity feed · No email is sent'), before the primary, because a write whose side effects surprise people says so at the moment of choice (DEC-802/DEC-829/DEC-844 family); and the rated Fit pill takes the SAME filled-olive treatment as DEC-730's complete state in speakers.css, referencing that rule's existing custom property, never a new hex literal (DEC-383 palette closure).
