# DEC-793: A field the composer will reject is never advertised, and a rejected recipient is named

VERIFIED ComposeWizard.tsx:417-425 the Body step offers no merge-field affordance at all while the panel at :469 is titled "Attachments and merge fields" and contains only attachment toggles; VERIFIED :154-165 and :200-206 render only err.message for a failed preflight, discarding the API's per-entry payload (src/routes/comms.ts:532 missingToFields keys each culprit as contactId:submissionId), so the organiser reads "One or more recipients are missing merge fields" with no way to learn who or what. Same class in BulkEmailModal, whose placeholder advertises {first_name} that BULK_EMAIL_MERGE_FIELDS rejects. Decision: the allowed vocabulary is rendered where the body is written — quiet chips under the Body field, imported from app/src/lib/merge-fields (so it grows automatically with DEC-792), click inserts at the cursor, one line of hint that tokens fill per recipient; the panel is retitled "Attachments"; and a preflight rejection lists one line per culprit naming the person (resolved through the loaded submission's participants — a person is named by CONTACT) and the missing field. No surface may advertise a token its own validator refuses.
