# DEC-793: A field the composer will reject is never advertised, and a rejected recipient is named

VERIFIED ComposeWizard.tsx:417-425 the Body step offers no merge-field affordance at all while the panel at :469 is titled "Attachments and merge fields" and contains only attachment toggles; VERIFIED :154-165 and :200-206 render only err.message for a failed preflight, discarding the API's per-entry payload (src/routes/comms/compose-core.ts missingToFields keys each culprit as contactId:submissionId), so the organiser reads "One or more recipients are missing merge fields" with no way to learn who or what. Same class in BulkEmailModal, whose placeholder advertises {first_name} that BULK_EMAIL_MERGE_FIELDS rejects. Decision: the allowed vocabulary is rendered where the body is written — quiet chips under the Body field, imported from app/src/lib/merge-fields (so it grows automatically with DEC-792), click inserts at the cursor, one line of hint that tokens fill per recipient; the panel is retitled "Attachments"; and a preflight rejection lists one line per culprit naming the person (resolved through the loaded submission's participants — a person is named by CONTACT) and the missing field. No surface may advertise a token its own validator refuses.


## Amendment (wave 28): the pre-flight on an irreversible send offers the partial action and a way out, and says why it interrupted

ComposeWizard already names each blocked recipient (a DEC-793 win) at :738 and :822 and hard-disables Send, but it offers only two exits — go schedule everything, or drop the calendar attachment for everyone — which is the all-or-nothing block rule 10 exists to forbid. Ruling: the blocked banner gains (a) a partial primary, 'Send to the N who have a slot', which proceeds with the scheduled subset and reports the excluded ones in the step-4 report, (b) a per-recipient way out, a 'Place on the agenda ›' link, and (c) the caption 'This is the last point at which it is cheap to fix.' The existing hard block stays as the default; the partial action is an additional path, never a silent narrowing of the recipient set — the step-4 report must state the reduced count.
