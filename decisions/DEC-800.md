# DEC-800: Two records with one name are a candidate even when the companies differ — with the reason named

VERIFIED src/domain/contacts.ts:76-131 groups by email, then by normalized name SUB-GROUPED BY COMPANY (DEC-143), so the single most common speaker-CRM duplicate — the same person who changed employers between events — is silently never offered for merge, which is the mandate's open "drops pairs when companies differ" item and explicitly asks for a decided rule. Widening recall without saying WHY a pair was surfaced would just move the problem to the organiser's judgement. Decision: a name match across two different non-empty companies IS a duplicate candidate, and every group carries the reason it was grouped — 'email' (strongest), 'name_and_company', 'name' — which the duplicates surface renders as a plain caption on the pair. Detection widens; the merge itself stays a deliberate, reviewed act, and the persisted "Not a duplicate" dismissal (DEC-770) suppresses the weaker pairs an organiser has already judged.
