# DEC-602: /agenda is the time grid and /schedule is the chronological itinerary list; a grid slot has a fixed height and holds nothing interactive

EMB-3 (a 70px gutter column that renders nothing) and EMB-4 (12/12 blocks bleed 29-190px, and on /schedule the 'Add to itinerary' checkbox is clipped out of view for 3 of 4 blocks) are one geometry defect: the grid derives block spans from dayStart/gridMin but never uses that same row math to label the gutter, and it puts a control inside a box whose height is decided by a session's duration. The gutter gets hour labels from the one computed row map the blocks already use, blocks clip to their slot, and the itinerary control leaves the grid entirely — because /schedule was never an agenda. EMB-09 asks the itinerary for chronological cards carrying track, title, description, full date/time, room and every speaker with title and company, which is the phone list at every width, not a room-column wall. That also gives EMB-10 somewhere honest to live: a picks-only filter over a list, not over absolutely-positioned blocks.
