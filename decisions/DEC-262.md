Wave 4 is not that wave: it lands the battery-FAIL fixes DEC-261 explicitly permits ('the only product commits permitted from wave 4 onward are fixes for defects that make a battery section FAIL'), and wave-3 lanes were still in flight at planning time (task-w3-b/c cut with no commits, task-w3-a landed mid-read). The battery therefore runs in wave 5, unchanged, on a quiet tree.
