# DEC-852: A deadline the product silently moved is visible before it bites, and a date outside this year names its year

VERIFIED: DEC-801 made lateness judge against effectiveAssignmentDueDate (raw due date shifted by a 7-day grace from assignment), and all four surfaces share that one rule — but app/src/pages/speakers/OnboardingGrid.tsx:508-524 only RENDERS the effective date once the cell is already overdue (`overdueTitleText` is gated on `overdue`). Until then the cell says 'Pending' and nothing else, so the organiser cannot see the date the product moved their deadline to, and cannot tell a chase is premature. A rule that quietly changes when someone is late owes the new date at the moment it applies, not at the moment it fires. Separately, taskDueLabel (OnboardingGrid.tsx:42-48) prints `Due 15 Jan` with no year, so a 2028 deadline is indistinguishable from a fortnight away. Decision: every non-complete cell with a non-null effective due date states that date in its title and accessible name (visible cell text stays Pending/OVERDUE/Complete — DEC-730/DEC-789 shapes are unchanged), and the column caption appends the 4-digit year whenever the due date's UTC year differs from now's.
