# DEC-923: The mailer is the ONLY author of an email_log row, and a failed attempt has exactly one name

DEC-766 put the failed-attempt row in a route-level helper (src/mail/log-failed.ts, DELETED by this decision's ruling below) that every fan-out must remember to call -- and three of five have forgotten: src/server/repo/tasks/reminders.ts:206-210 (both the J6 cron pass and the bulk 'remind everyone outstanding' button), src/routes/review/plans-progress.ts:241-243 and src/routes/content-notes.ts:139-142 swallow a mailer.send rejection with no row, so a wholly-failed batch reads as '0 total' in Comms history -- exactly the gap DEC-766 exists to prevent. Worse, where the helper IS called it now double-writes: EmailBindingMailer (src/mail/email-binding.ts:60-83) already logs status 'error' and rethrows, and the route then logs a second row with status 'failed', so one failed send prints '3 error, 3 failed' through RecentSends.tsx's statusTally. Two writers producing two vocabularies for one event is the drift a shared reader always exposes. Rule: every Mailer implementation writes EXACTLY ONE email_log row per attempt, status 'sent' or 'failed' -- no third word; logFailedSend and all three of its call sites are DELETED (no shim, no re-export, per the house rule); and a scan test holds the invariant that insert(schema.emailLog) appears in exactly one module, src/server/context.ts's d1EmailLogWriter. Every fan-out in the product then inherits the audit row without any of them remembering anything.
