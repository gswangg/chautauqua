# DEC-427: A failed public-cache version bump must not turn a committed mutation into a 500 -- DEC-399's purge scope stays closed and untouched

Two independent review lenses raised src/server/pubcache.ts. DEC-201/333/348/399 settled ONE of the two questions -- whether the bump should be narrowed from '*' to a publish-affecting-writes allowlist -- and closed it three times; that disposition is unchanged here and remains not-an-open-item. The other question has never been decided: bumpPublicVersionMiddleware (src/server/pubcache.ts:138-142) awaits kv.put AFTER await next(), i.e. after the handler's write has already committed, with no try/catch. Cloudflare rate-limits writes to a single KV key to roughly one per second, and chq:pubver is one key for the whole app, so at SPEC.md:73-76 concurrency a throw there converts a succeeded mutation into a 500 the client will retry -- the response would be lying about the outcome, which is the opposite of fail-loudly, not an application of it. CLAUDE.md's own carve-out is exact: catch at network/IO boundaries, trust invariants internally. Rule: a kv.put failure inside the bump is caught, logged loudly with console.error naming PUBVER_KEY and the error, and the handler's own status is preserved; a missing KV *binding* stays a throw, because that is a configuration bug rather than an IO failure and the distinction is the whole point. Regression bar: a fake KV whose put rejects must leave a 200/302 response's status untouched and must still surface the failure on the console.
