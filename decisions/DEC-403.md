# DEC-403: Whatever the mobile pass visits, the desktop pass visits: routeManifest is the union of SPA routes and every no-login surface

scripts/render-sweep.ts's MOBILE_ROUTE_MANIFEST visits eight surfaces app/src/routeManifest.ts does not — /e/:slug/sessions/:id, /e/:slug/speakers/:id, the three /embed/* surfaces, /login, /docs/api and /dev/mailbox — so those pages are graded for geometry at 390px but never for HTTP status, non-empty render or console/page errors at 1240px. That is exactly the check that caught the /admin/overview crash, and the public detail and embed routes are the ones swyx's technical teammate persona (SPEC §1) actually consumes. The manifest is already documented as 'the exhaustive list of app routes the browser render-sweep visits', so widening it is closing a stated gap, not inventing a bar. app/src/routeManifest.test.ts only asserts SPA <Route> patterns are covered, so non-SPA entries are additive and safe.
