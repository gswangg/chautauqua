# DEC-376: Contacts splits across two lanes by view with a fixed file boundary; the merge screen is the one place an off-palette colour is allowed

Contacts is five views plus two modals at two widths — too much for one lane and too entangled for two lanes to negotiate at merge time, so the boundary is fixed here rather than discovered. Lane 1 owns ContactsApp.tsx (page chrome, tab pill strip, toolbar, bulk bar, pagination), ContactsTable.tsx, StatsStrip.tsx, ContactDrawer.tsx, BulkEmailModal.tsx, AddToEventModal.tsx and app/src/pages/contacts/contacts.css. Lane 2 owns PipelineBoard.tsx, DuplicatesView.tsx, SegmentsPanel.tsx, FilterRulesPanel.tsx, ImportWizard.tsx and app/src/pages/contacts/contacts-panels.css, and does not edit ContactsApp.tsx — its panels are already mounted there and their props do not change. Per the handoff, the merge screen is the single exception to the no-off-palette rule: the discarded value renders in --chq-muted with `text-decoration: line-through` in #A8A392, because merge is irreversible and the discarded value is the evidence for the decision. That hex is permitted only in contacts-panels.css and only for that strike-through; DEC-367's no-red, no-third-accent rule is otherwise absolute.
