# DEC-833: A send is auditable to its words: the organiser can read exactly what a recipient received

VERIFIED the product records every send and shows the organiser none of it. EmailBatchRow (src/server/repo/email.ts:198) carries subject/recipientCount/statusCounts only, DEC-543 deliberately strips bodyText from list projections for payload size, and the ONLY surface that renders a body is the dev mailbox (src/routes/dev/mailbox.tsx). So Comms history answers 'we mailed 23 people' and cannot answer 'what did we say to them', which is the question a complaint starts with — and per-recipient rendering means the answer differs per row. Decision: DEC-543's list rule stands and the body is fetched one row at a time — a new organiser route GET /api/v1/events/:eventId/email-log/:emailId returns the full stored row (getEmailLogById, scoped to org AND asserted to belong to that event), and each recipient in the expanded batch carries a quiet 'Show what was sent' disclosure that renders the stored subject and body verbatim, never a re-render. The stored row is the audit record: it is shown as sent, including for a failed attempt.
