# DEC-732: A dialog's form is one measure with labels above; a send names the constraint each recipient carries

Two verified honesty/craft defects share one fix point. ModalFrame has no form primitive, so dialogs invent their own layout (bulk email renders labels beside inputs at drifting baselines, Comms' Body textarea is 187px against Subject's 571px). One exported FormRow inside ModalFrame — label above, control at the modal's full measure — makes every dialog inherit the pattern, and mounting ModalFrame through a root portal stops an ancestor's stacking context from deciding where a modal lands. Separately, a preview that hides a recipient's blocking condition lies by omission: per-recipient SCHEDULED / NO SLOT tags plus an 'N have no slot' advisory keep the preview equal to what the send will actually do.
