# DEC-993: One 'Insert a field' control per composer, and the merge-field sample values live beside the vocabulary they describe

Six bordered chips under a body field read as six competing buttons, and the compose wizard and the template editor each render their own row of them (ComposeWizard.tsx:584, TemplatesTab.tsx:216) from COMPOSE_MERGE_FIELDS. v6 replaces both with a single "Insert a field ▾" control whose open state lists each token beside a sample value, so a writer can tell {task_list} from {feedback} without sending a test email. The sample values are part of the merge-field vocabulary, not of either screen, so they live in src/mail/render.ts next to COMPOSE_MERGE_FIELDS/BLOCK_MERGE_FIELDS and reach the SPA through app/src/lib/merge-fields.ts — the ONE module DEC-660 allows to cross the app/→src/ boundary for this vocabulary. Typing the samples as a Record over MergeField makes a missing sample a compile error, so the vocabulary and its examples can never drift. The existing constraints are unchanged and still enforced by the control: DEC-847 keeps task_list/feedback out of the subject picker, and the feedback token stays conditional on the event having plans.
