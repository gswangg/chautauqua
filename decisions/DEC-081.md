# DEC-081: Review surface is set-based: one plan-filtered load per request, pure assignment resolution, and targeted per-submission scope/cap checks on the rating PUT

The review dashboard does ~reviewer-count full-event scans per view and every rating PUT pays O(event) — violating §7's one-round-trip and p95<50ms budgets exactly where "keyboard-fast review" matters, and crashing outright at 2k submissions via the unchunked submissionTrackIds inArray. Binding contracts: (1) listPlanFilteredSubmissions(db, plan) keeps its signature but becomes two joined, id-list-free queries — matched submissions (selected columns only: id, seq, title, description) with SQL-side plan-filter narrowing via submission_track innerJoin + inArray on plan.filters.trackIds (bounded ≤~25), and full trackIds hydration via a submission_track⋈submission query filtered by eventId, grouped in JS. (2) New pure resolveAssignments(all: SubmissionSummary[], reviewerRows: PlanReviewerRecord[]): Map<userId, SubmissionSummary[]> in src/domain/evaluation.ts; progress and remind load the plan data ONCE and fan out in memory; resolveReviewerSubmissions(db, plan, userId) is reimplemented on top for single-reviewer paths. (3) The rating PUT and reviewer submission GET replace full scans with isSubmissionInReviewerScope(db, plan, userId, submissionId): Promise<boolean> (reviewer's own scope rows + small bounded membership queries, honoring plan.filters.trackIds) and countEvaluationsForSubmission(db, planId, submissionId, round): Promise<number> (SQL count(*)) for the cap. The round parameter exists from day one so DEC-082 threads current_round through without changing signatures.
