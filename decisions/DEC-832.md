# DEC-832: The composer sends the text it shows — a template is a starting point, not a mode

VERIFIED both halves silently discard the organiser's typing: ComposeWizard.tsx:143-148 posts templateId INSTEAD of subject/bodyText whenever a template is selected, and src/routes/comms.ts:230-236 then renders from the stored template, ignoring the body — while the textarea stays fully editable on screen, chip row and all. A field you can type in that the send throws away is worse than a locked one, because nothing tells you. Decision: selecting a template COPIES its subject and body into the composer's own fields and clears the selection; the composer thereafter always posts subject+bodyText, which the same merge-field validation and preflight already cover. The route stops accepting the ambiguous combination: templateId together with subject/bodyText is a loud 400 naming the conflict rather than a silent precedence rule. No back-compat alias, no read-only textarea, one writer for what gets sent.
