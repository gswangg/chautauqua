# DEC-846: A composer sends the text on screen; a template is a starting point, not a hidden source of truth — and history owes the WORDS

## Amendment (wave 116): a ?template= landing is provenance, and a LATE templates response must never overwrite composer text the organizer has already edited

app/src/pages/comms/ComposeWizard.tsx:293-305 runs an effect on [templates] that, the first time templates arrive, unconditionally calls setSubject(found.subject) and setBodyText(found.bodyText). Nothing checks whether the organizer has typed since mount. Under load the /templates read resolves after the first keystrokes and the effect silently replaces the words the organizer wrote — on a screen whose next control is Send. This is not a hypothetical: it is main's ONLY red across the whole suite (1415 files / 17515 tests at a03569c9), app/src/pages/comms/ComposeWizard.templateParam.render.test.tsx failing with `expected 'Hi {speaker_name}, still waitlisted.' to be '...Edited.'` in the full run and passing in isolation on the identical commit. A gate that renders two verdicts on one tree is how a merge train learns to discount reds (wave-115's lesson), and here the flake is the bug wearing a disguise. RULED: the landing applies only while the composer's own text is still pristine. templateId provenance may still be recorded from a late response (it is provenance, not authority — the send posts the composer's words either way), but subject and bodyText are the composer's the moment the organizer touches them. The test stops racing: it awaits the prefill before editing, and gains a regression case where the templates response lands AFTER the edit.
