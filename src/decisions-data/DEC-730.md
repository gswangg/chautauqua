## Amendment (wave 90): the v12 status inversion is ONE vocabulary in every state including write-failure — Speakers frame :549's filled Complete pill is a stale pre-inversion cut and does not bind

The Tier-0 speakers audit (docs/design/audit/speakers-v12.md, finding 1) has sat unresolved since wave 1 because it is a design question no worker may answer. Chautauqua Speakers.dc.html:549 ('Speakers · a write failed') draws Complete as a FILLED olive pill at weight 700, Overdue as an ink-OUTLINED cell with a 3px left border, and Pending as outlined — while three other frames in the same file (:28/:129 the 1600 grid, :598 'One task, every speaker', :665 'One task · still waiting') draw the inverted v12 constants: complete is muted 600 text with no fill/border/radius, pending is bare bold ink, overdue is the filled-ink chip. DESIGN-RULINGS.md's B8 states the intent in words the drawing cannot override — 'weight goes to the exception, never to the resting state', 'overdue is filled ink, pending is outlined, complete recedes', and the legend it mandates reads 'Overdue is filled · pending outlined · complete recedes'. Honouring :549 literally would mean the grid renders one status vocabulary normally and a second, older one only while a rollback banner is up: a status field that changes its meaning-to-appearance mapping depending on an unrelated write outcome, which is the exact 'same field never changes vocabulary between screens' failure B8's density paragraph was written to prevent. RULED: frame :549 was not re-cut when the pack's inversion landed; its status cells are STALE and do not bind. The app's `.chq-speakers-status-*` axis-1 rules and TaskCell.tsx's statusCellClass are correct as shipped, and this is recorded as a blessed deviation with a receipt rather than left as an open audit finding a future wave re-litigates. Everything else in :549 — the banner copy, 'Reload the grid', 'Try again', the 'Review these reminders' / 'Send these N' strings w1-b already fixed — continues to bind.

## Amendment (wave 101): Under B8's density pairing, `margin-inline` / `margin-block` are GEOMETRY, not colour or weight — a dense chip may carry a negative inline margin its roomy twin does not

`app/src/status-density-pairing.scan.test.ts` went red (1 divergence, DIVERGENCE_CEILING 0) because commit 0abe77cf gave `.chq-speakers-status-dense` `margin-inline: -6px` (breathing room for B8's hover ring, zero glyph movement) and the scan classified `margin-inline` as a NON-geometry property, so it read a legitimate density difference as a vocabulary divergence. B8's own words are the test: "Colour and weight are identical in both; only geometry differs." A negative inline margin is pure geometry — it moves nothing about the chip's ink, hue or weight, and its whole purpose is to let a 20px matrix cell absorb a ring the 44px roomy variant has room for already. RULING: the scan's geometry-property set is amended to include `margin-inline`, `margin-inline-start/-end`, `margin-block`, `margin-block-start/-end` and `margin`, with the rationale written at the set's definition. This is a fix to the instrument's own classification, not a licence: the ceiling stays 0 and colour/weight divergence remains a failure. The paired ratchet must keep failing when the true count drops below the ceiling, so the ceiling can never go stale in the other direction.
