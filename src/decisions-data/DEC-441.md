# DEC-441: Amendment (wave 135): The reminder email is built to its frame: greeting, nudge, 'Still to do', one CTA, signature -- and one builder still serves both preview and send

## Amendment (wave 110): the reminder review dialog shows what each person owes and what they will read -- the drafts carry the tasks, never a re-parse of the body

docs/design/Chautauqua Speakers.dc.html:492-541 draws, per recipient, a 1fr/auto row: name over that person's outstanding TASK NAMES, with an overdue/due-soon flag right-flushed; then a `What <name> will read` eyebrow over a rendered email card; then the caption `Each speaker's list is their own · the body is built from their outstanding tasks, not typed`; then `Logged in Comms history` beside Send/Cancel. Live renders a bare `<ul>` of `name · email` and one `<pre>`. The point of an assisted-chasing review step is that the organizer can see WHO owes WHAT before an irreversible send; a list of addresses cannot support that judgement. RULED: previewRemindNow's draft shape gains the per-recipient task list built from the SAME `group.assignments` buildReminderMessage consumes -- never re-derived, never scraped back out of `text` (a second derivation of one fact is DEC-613's trap). The overdue/due-soon vocabulary is the existing one in app/src/pages/speakers/overdue.ts, not a third phrasing.

## Amendment (wave 135): The reminder email is built to its frame: greeting, nudge, 'Still to do', one CTA, signature -- and one builder still serves both preview and send

Chautauqua Speakers.dc.html:515-535 draws the reminder email in full, and the tree ships a different message: buildReminderMessage emits 'You have outstanding tasks for {event}:' + task lines + a bare portal URL, and sendReminderEmails calls renderEmailHtml with no `cta` at all. The frame's CTA label 'Open your tasks' is the single remaining offender in the whole non-390 control-label ledger (DESKTOP_CONTROL_LABEL_CEILING=1), and the mail shell ALREADY renders both the button and its 'Or paste this into your browser: <url>' fallback -- but only when a cta is passed, which nothing does. So the last desktop control-label gap is closed by using a shell affordance that has been sitting unused, not by inventing markup. The frame's greeting, its count-composed nudge sentence, its 'Still to do' section label and its organiser signature come with it, because a frame is adopted whole or not at all. DEC-441's wave-110 invariant is preserved unchanged: buildReminderMessage stays the ONE builder so previewRemindNow and the real send remain byte-identical, and the plain-text part keeps the portal URL in its own body (the shell's paste-line is HTML-only, and a text-only client must still be able to reach the portal).
