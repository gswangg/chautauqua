# DEC-441: Amendment (wave 135): The reminder email is built to its frame: greeting, nudge, 'Still to do', one CTA, signature -- and one builder still serves both preview and send

## Amendment (wave 110): the reminder review dialog shows what each person owes and what they will read -- the drafts carry the tasks, never a re-parse of the body

docs/design/Chautauqua Speakers.dc.html:492-541 draws, per recipient, a 1fr/auto row: name over that person's outstanding TASK NAMES, with an overdue/due-soon flag right-flushed; then a `What <name> will read` eyebrow over a rendered email card; then the caption `Each speaker's list is their own · the body is built from their outstanding tasks, not typed`; then `Logged in Comms history` beside Send/Cancel. Live renders a bare `<ul>` of `name · email` and one `<pre>`. The point of an assisted-chasing review step is that the organizer can see WHO owes WHAT before an irreversible send; a list of addresses cannot support that judgement. RULED: previewRemindNow's draft shape gains the per-recipient task list built from the SAME `group.assignments` buildReminderMessage consumes -- never re-derived, never scraped back out of `text` (a second derivation of one fact is DEC-613's trap). The overdue/due-soon vocabulary is the existing one in app/src/pages/speakers/overdue.ts, not a third phrasing.

## Amendment (wave 135): The reminder email is built to its frame: greeting, nudge, 'Still to do', one CTA, signature -- and one builder still serves both preview and send

Chautauqua Speakers.dc.html:515-535 draws the reminder email in full, and the tree ships a different message: buildReminderMessage emits 'You have outstanding tasks for {event}:' + task lines + a bare portal URL, and sendReminderEmails calls renderEmailHtml with no `cta` at all. The frame's CTA label 'Open your tasks' is the single remaining offender in the whole non-390 control-label ledger (DESKTOP_CONTROL_LABEL_CEILING=1), and the mail shell ALREADY renders both the button and its 'Or paste this into your browser: <url>' fallback -- but only when a cta is passed, which nothing does. So the last desktop control-label gap is closed by using a shell affordance that has been sitting unused, not by inventing markup. The frame's greeting, its count-composed nudge sentence, its 'Still to do' section label and its organiser signature come with it, because a frame is adopted whole or not at all. DEC-441's wave-110 invariant is preserved unchanged: buildReminderMessage stays the ONE builder so previewRemindNow and the real send remain byte-identical, and the plain-text part keeps the portal URL in its own body (the shell's paste-line is HTML-only, and a text-only client must still be able to reach the portal).

## Amendment (wave 5/36): A modal subtitle names the event its frame names -- the reminder preview counts what has NOT been sent, and the response modal dates the SEND

Two Speakers modal subtitles say something the v12 frames do not. Chautauqua Speakers.dc.html:232 draws 'Priya Raman - sent 2 Apr' under a form-kind task's answered view; app/src/pages/speakers/ResponseModal.tsx:29 renders 'Completed {formatDate(detail.completedAt)}'. The frame dates the moment the organizer ACTED (when the task went out), which is the fact that makes a stale response legible; the completion date is already implied by the answers being present. Chautauqua Speakers.dc.html:499 draws '3 speakers - nothing has been sent yet' where the app names only a recipient count. RULED: ResponseModal's subtitle names the sent date per the frame; RemindPreviewModal's subtitle carries the frame's not-yet-sent clause alongside the server's own count (never a recomputed number -- DEC-829's rule that this dialog prints the server's figures verbatim stands). The frame's rendered-email card at :516-533 is a SEPARATE and larger gap (RemindPreviewModal.tsx still ships `<pre>{first.text}</pre>`) that needs a structured server contract and is deliberately NOT in this wave's scope.

## Amendment (wave 6): ResponseModal's frame subtitle names the assignment's own createdAt as the SENT date, surfaced on the DEC-291 wire as `assignedAt`

Chautauqua Speakers.dc.html:235 draws `Priya Raman · sent 2 Apr` under the answered form-kind task modal; ResponseModal.tsx renders `Completed {formatDate(detail.completedAt)}`. DEC-441 already ruled the frame right — the date that makes a stale response legible is when the organiser ACTED, and completion is already implied by the answers being present — but never named WHICH stored fact is 'sent'. `task_assignment` carries no sent column; it carries `createdAt` (the moment the task went out to this contact), `completedAt`, and `lastRemindedAt`. Ruled: `createdAt` of the assignment row IS the sent date. `getAssignmentResponseDetail` selects `schema.taskAssignment.createdAt` and returns it as `assignedAt: number` on `AssignmentResponseDetail`; the modal renders `{contactName} · sent {formatDate(detail.assignedAt)}`. `lastRemindedAt` is explicitly NOT it — a reminder is not the send — and no new column is minted for a fact the table already holds (DEC-851/DEC-988: never a declared knob with no reader, and never a second source for an existing one).

## Amendment (wave 7): The reminder preview renders a structured card built from the send's own parts — and its header is the SUBJECT, never a fabricated letterhead

Speakers.dc.html:491 draws a rendered email card; RemindPreviewModal.tsx ships `<pre>{first.text}</pre>`. `buildReminderMessage` (src/server/repo/tasks/reminders.ts:391) ALREADY computes every part the frame draws as a local — greeting (:422), nudge (:409), taskLines (:415), portalLine (:418), signature (:419) — and throws them away into a joined string. RULED: widen the return to carry `parts` alongside a byte-identical `text`, so the real send is unchanged and the preview stops re-parsing prose. The frame's letterhead block (`DevFlow Conf 2027` / `12–14 May 2027 · Moscone West`) is NOT built: the send produces no such block, and DEC-441's whole premise is that the organiser previews exactly what is sent. The card's header band instead carries the `subject` `buildReminderMessage` already returns — which today's `<pre>` drops entirely, so this makes the preview strictly MORE honest, not less.
