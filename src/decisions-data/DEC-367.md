## Amendment (wave 30): the overflow instrument's two escape hatches (scroller-held and KNOWN_CLIP_EXCEPTIONS) are premises about the pack, and an ungrounded escape hatch hides overflow rather than curing it

RULED: (a) every `overflow-x: auto|scroll` declaration in the product sheets is enumerated by directory walk (never a hand list) and joined to the pack — a scroller whose selector's surface has no frame drawing a scrolling region is recorded as UNGROUNDED-SCROLLER; (b) KNOWN_CLIP_EXCEPTIONS is re-derived from the tree and any member that no longer matches a real element, or that is not a visually-hidden affordance under CLIP_VISUALLY_HIDDEN_MAX_PX, is a stale allowlist row (DEC-725); (c) both populations get two-sided ceilings seeded by measurement, and the roster names each row so a later wave can drain rather than re-derive. ## Amendment (wave 47): a transition that names a --chq-motion-* duration must name its paired easing token — a ban-list of forbidden easings cannot see a MISSING one
