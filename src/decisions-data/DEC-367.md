# DEC-367: The disabled token's two legal uses become executable at phone widths

DESIGN-RULINGS.md gives the token exactly two legal uses -- an inert control and a drag handle -- and states why the boundary is not cosmetic: "#8E8A7A on #DDD8C8... fails the 4.5 floor at 3.06:1 on paper, so anything carrying meaning in it is unreadable by someone who needs the contrast", and records that the token escaped into three separate designs in consecutive rounds, each time as "the quiet version of something". test/disabled-token-uses.scan.test.ts exists but the mandate's Tier-2 list still carries "disabled-token compliance (two legal uses) at phone sizes" as unbuilt, and phone blocks are precisely where a de-emphasis reflex reappears (a phone card has no column to recede into, so the temptation is to grey the text). RULED: the existing scan's population is extended, in its own idiom, to every rule inside a max-width <= 700px block across app/src/**/*.css and src/**/*.css.ts; a use there is legal only when its selector resolves to an inert control (:disabled / [disabled] / [aria-disabled]) or to the codebase's own drag-handle token (derived from the source, not guessed); everything else fails carrying the ruling's own sentence and the offending file and selector. De-emphasis stays weight, not lightness: 800 -> 600 at #565A4B. Exemptions structural and two-sided, so a stale one fails with delete-this-line.
