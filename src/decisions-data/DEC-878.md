## Amendment (wave 129): The 390 decision dock's third verb is the frame's short form; the shared vocabulary is not edited and the accessible name never shortens

Chautauqua Submissions.dc.html draws `Waitlist` at :97 and :314 and `Wait` only at :391, the 390 decision dock where three controls share 390px. The pack is therefore using its own shared vocabulary everywhere and shortening it exactly once, under width pressure — that is not the vocabulary fork DEC-613 forbids, it is the same width-conditioned copy DEC-919's wave-128 Overview ruling already endorsed and that this very file already implements for its back link (.chq-detail-back-full/.chq-detail-back-short). So DECISION_ACTION_LABELS stays untouched as the one vocabulary and the short form is a sibling span, exactly one visible per width. The clause worth stating out loud: a shorter TAP label must not become a shorter ANNOUNCED name — the long form remains the button's accessible name, because the reason for the shortening is pixels and screen readers do not have that constraint.
