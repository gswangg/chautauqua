# DEC-878: The per-submission decision rail is a triage ACTION, not a status picker: Accept is the primary, Decline|Waitlist the secondary pair, and the caption says deciding sends nothing

## Amendment (wave 129): The 390 decision dock's third verb is the frame's short form; the shared vocabulary is not edited and the accessible name never shortens

Chautauqua Submissions.dc.html draws `Waitlist` at :97 and :314 and `Wait` only at :391, the 390 decision dock where three controls share 390px. The pack is therefore using its own shared vocabulary everywhere and shortening it exactly once, under width pressure — that is not the vocabulary fork DEC-613 forbids, it is the same width-conditioned copy DEC-919's wave-128 Overview ruling already endorsed and that this very file already implements for its back link (.chq-detail-back-full/.chq-detail-back-short). So DECISION_ACTION_LABELS stays untouched as the one vocabulary and the short form is a sibling span, exactly one visible per width. The clause worth stating out loud: a shorter TAP label must not become a shorter ANNOUNCED name — the long form remains the button's accessible name, because the reason for the shortening is pixels and screen readers do not have that constraint.

## Amendment (wave 131): A desktop-only label span hidden at phone is the width-conditioned-copy twin and owes a receipt naming that idiom, not a bare display:none

`app/src/phone-capability-removal.scan.test.ts` fails on main with exactly one offender: `app/src/pages/comms/comms.css :: .chq-comms-batch-toggle-label-desktop`, an unreceipted interactive phone `display:none` against a ceiling of 0. It arrived with v12m-w4-q, the lane that gave the comms History mount the frame's `See the recipients` copy. DEC-919's rule has never been 'no hiding' — it is that a hidden control must be receipted with the ruling that licenses it, because an unreceipted hide is indistinguishable from a capability silently removed at 390. This one is the DEC-878 family: two sibling spans, exactly one visible per width, the long form remaining the accessible name — the same shape `Wait`/`Waitlist` (DEC-878), `See the recipients`/`Open` (DEC-833) and `.chq-detail-back-full`/`.chq-detail-back-short` already use. The remedy is therefore a receipt comment at the rule that names the idiom and its sibling class by content (never `file:line` — citations drift and this campaign has re-pointed dozens), plus proof that the phone twin actually renders and carries the same accessible name. What must NOT happen is raising the ceiling: it reads 0, the tree regressed against it, and this is the one cause.

## Amendment (wave 16): the 390 submission detail draws no Abstract or Form-answers heading — the headings go screen-reader-only, never deleted

docs/design/Chautauqua Submissions.dc.html:355's scroll body opens directly on the abstract `<p>` (:367) and then the `sc-for` answer rows (label/value pairs), with no section heading above either — while the SAME frame DOES draw a heading for the third block (`Reviews · 3 of 3 in`, :375). So the frame is not headingless out of laziness; it distinguishes the two blocks that need no heading from the one that does. app/src/pages/submissions/SubmissionDetailPage.tsx renders `<h2 class="chq-detail-section-title">Abstract</h2>` and `…>Form answers</h2>` at every width. RULED: at 390 only, those two headings take the house `chq-visually-hidden` treatment rather than `display:none`. A heading is document structure, not a control: hiding it outright would strip the phone document's landmark outline from every screen-reader user to satisfy a drawing, whereas a visually-hidden heading matches the frame pixel-for-pixel while keeping the outline intact — and DEC-919's capability-removal receipt is satisfied by construction, since nothing is removed. The Reviews heading and every other `.chq-detail-section-title` on the page are untouched.
