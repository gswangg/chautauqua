## Amendment (wave 147): the CFP confirmation card's meta reads through the one session-display vocabulary, in the frame's own order

Three v12 frames draw the opposite order and a third clause: `Talk, 30 min · Platform & Infra · intermediate` (Public and Portal.dc.html:1080), `Lightning talk, 10 min · Developer Experience` (:1130), `Panel, 45 min · AI Engineering` (:1153). `src/lib/session-vocabulary.ts` already exports exactly the two transforms the frames use — `sessionFormatLabel` ('Talk (30 min)' -> 'Talk, 30 min') and `audienceLevelLabel` ('Advanced' -> 'advanced') — and submit-post never calls either: a second, poorer display derivation sitting beside the shared one (DEC-613). The `audience_level` field role exists (`src/forms/types.ts:28` FORM_FIELD_ROLES, `src/server/repo/forms.ts:276`), so the third clause needs no new data. RULED: format-then-track-then-audience-level, joined ' · ', each clause through the shared vocabulary, at the frame's 13px muted register; the audience clause is omitted (not blanked) when the form defines no such field. `test/cfp-confirmation-email-honesty.test.ts`'s three assertions on the old order move — the app did not match the frame, so the product moves and the pin moves with it (the inverse of DEC-582's pin-moves rule).
