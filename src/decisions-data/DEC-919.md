# DEC-919: Every public surface narrows a list with ONE idiom: the same search box and the same pill bar

## Amendment (wave 7): a 390 head is reached by RE-LINING, never by removing a control

The Submissions 390 frame (docs/design/Chautauqua Submissions.dc.html:137) draws a two-band head — wordmark row, then title + count on one baseline — and no action buttons at all, while the app's .chq-submissions-head-actions carries Forms / Export CSV / New submission with no phone rule, so three buttons wrap raggedly into a head the frame draws clean. The tempting fix is display:none, and this codebase has already reached for it: submissions.css:808-822 hides the sort select, the track select, the divider, the column picker and every status pill but two. That is the wrong instrument in general — a 390 frame is the same product at a different width, and an organiser triaging on a phone who cannot sort has lost a capability the frame never asked anyone to remove. RULING: a head that the frame draws as two bands is reached by RE-LINING — the action cluster wraps to its own full-width row below the title, each control at the roomy 44px floor, ink where the frame puts it. display:none at phone width is legal in exactly one case: the control governs something that is not on the screen at all (the column picker over a table that has stacked into cards — which is why THAT hide stands and is recorded in DEVIATIONS.md). Everything else re-lines. Losing a control is a divergence from the frame, not a way of matching it.
