# DEC-180: Every field label the pack draws is a population, and until this wave nothing graded it

The three divergences DEC-613 rules on were found by hand, not by any instrument — which is the finding. Derived on 32436e11b across all 13 *.dc.html files: 155 desktop and 127 phone drawn labels sit in the pack's field register (an 11px/12px label span immediately preceding an input-shaped `border:1px solid #BAB6A6` box), and no scan in test/ or app/src/ grades any of them. The two saturated frame ledgers measure CITATION; the two control-label ledgers measure control-shaped populations; the field-width ledger uses labels only as a join key and files a miss as UNRESOLVED. A universal needs a population (DEC-180), and this one was never enumerated. RULED: a new instrument derives that population from the frames themselves — never a hand list — joins each drawn label to a real `FormRow label=` / `SettingsField label=` / `<label>` text / `aria-label` call site in comment-stripped non-test source, and grades MATCHES / DIVERGES / UNRESOLVED with a two-sided ratchet per side, seeded by forcing each to -1 and reading the printed rows on the measuring branch. It MEASURES AND FILES ONLY — it moves no product byte and repairs no row, because a lane that both widens a population and drains it can always drain by narrowing. Its audit doc must name every in-flight lane repairing a row it counts, so the merge train's re-measure is expected rather than surprising.

## Amendment (wave 29): the pack's COUNT-COMPOSED muted prose is a population — a digit is a discriminator for the caption ledger, never a licence to leave 145 rows ungraded

test/frame-caption-ledger.scan.test.ts's own header states its digit filter drops "count-composed" rows because they are "graded by a different instrument were one to exist" — and none exists. I measured the pack directly at main b2157a6e2: applying the caption ledger's exact grammar (a <span|div|p> whose style carries color:#565A4B / #3F4237 / #8E8A7A, no text-transform:uppercase, no '<'/'>' in TEXT, no '{{', >=20 chars, no '@') and INVERTING only its digit filter yields 145 rows across 13 files, against 233 in the ledger's own live population. These are real product consequence captions — 'Chautauqua Comms.dc.html':440 '3 have no slot yet — they can still be emailed, but not sent a calendar invite'; ':531 'All 21 are in History, each with the address it went to'; 'Chautauqua Contacts.dc.html':388 '3 submissions and 1 task move to the kept record. The discarded record is deleted.' RULED: (a) the digit-bearing rows are their own graded population with their own two-sided ceilings, not an exclusion; (b) the join is a DIGIT-COLLAPSED SKELETON (every run of digits replaced by a single sentinel, then whitespace collapsed and the pack's entity table decoded on BOTH sides) — a literal join is structurally wrong for copy the app composes; (c) the join has two tiers, because I measured 57 of the 145 skeletons present in non-test app/src+src source and the remainder composed at render time: tier 1 SOURCE (skeleton present in product source) and tier 2 INSTRUMENT (skeleton asserted by a *.render.test.tsx markup instrument against rendered DOM). A row in neither tier is UNRESOLVED or DIVERGES, never silently dropped; (d) fixture identities and pack-annotation prose leave via written FIXTURE/EXPLAINER exclusion tables whose reasons are PRINCIPLES and whose citations are resolution-guarded, exactly as the sibling caption ledger does.
