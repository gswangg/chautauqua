# DEC-829: The Speakers roster lists every participant of an accepted submission; ACTIVE is the predicate for expansion and access, never for LISTING

## Amendment (wave 110): the speaker-detail rail has its own register -- a rail row is two lines, and a rail empty state is one line

docs/design/Chautauqua Speakers.dc.html:411-425 draws `Across your events` as 10px-padded rows separated by hairlines, each a 14px/600 event name over a 13px muted participation line (`Spoke · Build Caches in Anger`, `Submitted, declined`). Live renders `<li>{e.name}</li>` -- the participation half of the row, which is the only reason the section exists, is missing -- and its empty case reaches for the page-scale EmptyState (24px headline, 44px of top room) inside a 320px rail. RULED: `otherEvents` carries the participation sentence from the server rows that already produce eventName/startDate (no new query); the rail's empty case is one muted 14px line, achieved by scoping the existing component under `.chq-speaker-detail-rail` rather than minting a second empty-state component. This is a desktop change and it is legal: the v12 desktop frame demands it.

## Amendment (wave 112): the speaker rail's section labels are aggregates the frame spells, not raw lengths

Chautauqua Speakers.dc.html:319 ("Speaker detail") spells four section labels literally. Three disagree with the app: the frame draws `Sessions · 1 accepted` where app/src/pages/speakers/SpeakerDetailPage.tsx`sessions.length` prints a raw length (drift-proof citation, DEC-967 wave-115: this construct's line number has moved twice since wave 112, so it is pinned on its text); `Tasks · 2 outstanding` where :577-583 prints a three-part `Tasks · N · N outstanding · N overdue`; and `Across your events` with NO count where :686 appends `· {otherEventsCount}`. `Files · 3` already matches, which proves the divergence is not a house idiom but three separate drifts. RULED: each label renders the frame's aggregate. The accepted count derives from `detail.sessions[].status`, already in the DEC-930 payload -- no new query and no new wire field. `otherEventsCount` keeps its reader as the list cap (a declared value with no reader is a lie, DEC-851/988); it simply stops appearing in the label. This is a desktop-affecting change and is legal precisely because a v12 desktop frame demands it.

## Amendment (wave 128): recipients are not submissions: the bulk-send wizard states the relationship once, where it changes

`ComposeWizard.tsx` counts SUBMISSIONS at step 1 (`${countOf(selection.selectedIds.size, 'submission')} selected`, :724/:962) and RECIPIENTS downstream (`{idsKeptCount} of {idsArrivedCount} kept` :1146, `{noSlotCount} of {preview.length}` :1378/:1501, `{sendResult.sent} of {sentTotal} speakers were emailed` :1592). The two numbers genuinely differ — a submission can carry a lead and co-presenters — so neither count is wrong, and '17 submissions selected vs 19 recipients' is the app being correct in two vocabularies without ever saying they are two. RULED: do NOT rename either count — a submission count that silently became a people count would be the lie DEC-829 exists to forbid (a roster is not a projection of its tasks; a recipient list is not a projection of its submissions). Instead the wizard states the relationship ONCE, at the step where the population changes — the step that first shows recipients names how many people the selected submissions resolve to — built from the count-copy helpers already imported (`countOf`), never a third hand-typed grammar. Every existing count keeps its own noun.
