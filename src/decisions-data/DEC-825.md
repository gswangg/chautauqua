## Amendment (wave 116): a decision row's secondary verb needs a verified phone home before its phone hide earns a receipt

Two of the six unreceipted DEC-919 rules are a decision row's third verb dropped at 390 with nothing standing in for it: the Overview waitlist button at app/src/pages/overview/overview.css`.chq-overview-btn-waitlist {` (a drift-proof citation, DEC-967 wave-115, for the same reason as its sibling below), and the phone Select-mode docked bar's secondary action, app/src/pages/content/content.css`.chq-content-worklist-selecting .chq-bulkbar-actions .chq-btn-tertiary` (a drift-proof citation, DEC-967 wave-115: the receipt names the construct, so the pointer survives the selector moving in its sheet). The receipt grammar the scan enforces is narrow and correct — a `/* phone-hidden: <reason> */` comment is legal only for a navigation control with nothing to reach, or a control re-lined elsewhere on the SAME phone surface, cited by file:line — and neither row qualifies today. RULED: each row closes by finding or building the phone home, never by receipting on assumption. Where the capability is genuinely reachable one tap away on the same phone surface (a detail screen the card already links to, a docked bar the mode already mounts), that is a re-lining and earns a receipt citing the control by file:line. Where it is not, the smallest affordance the frame supports is built. app/src/pages/overview/Overview.phone.render.test.tsx:221-226 pins the current phone rule and is re-pointed in the same commit — a pin on an offender is a lock on its repair.
