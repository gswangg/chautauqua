# DEC-727: A test that touches `window` is jsdom/SLOW — composeDraft.test.ts leaves the fast tier

app/src/pages/comms/composeDraft.test.ts is 12 of the fast tier's 41 failures, all `ReferenceError: window is not defined` at composeDraft.ts:57, and test/test-tiers.test.ts independently reports it as the one file the fast config wrongly selects. That is one defect reported twice, not two. The tier predicate is DERIVED, never hand-listed, so the repair is to make the derivation true: pin the file to jsdom in vitest.config.ts's environmentMatchGlobs and add the matching exclude line in vitest.fast.config.ts, exactly as the six existing entries there do. Fixing composeDraft.ts to guard `typeof window` would be the wrong repair — it is a localStorage contract module and the house rule is to fail loudly, not to defend against an environment the tier system is supposed to supply.


## Amendment (wave 115): a gate whose measured runtime sits at its undeclared timeout is a coin flip, and it teaches the train to discount reds

`test/public-cacheability-enumeration.test.ts` FAILED in the fast tier ("Test timed out in 5000ms", measured 5316ms) and PASSED in the full suite on the identical tree — the same commit, two verdicts. That is worse than a red: a gate that reports a defect that is not there is how a merge train learns to land batches over failing ratchets, which is precisely what happened to the desktop ledger this wave. Cause: `buildApp()` re-parses `src/index.ts`'s mounts and rebuilds the Hono app from scratch, is called three times in the file, and no `it` in it declares a timeout. RULED: a fixture that costs seconds is built once and shared, and any test still measuring above half the default timeout declares its own explicitly. Amortising the fixture is preferred over raising the limit — raising a timeout hides the cost, sharing it removes it — and the file's "never silently narrow" guard on the mount list must stay reachable exactly once rather than being dropped along with the repetition.
