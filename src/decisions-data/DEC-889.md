# DEC-889: The scorecard is a rating surface: a clamped abstract with one disclosure, not a second copy of the submission detail

## Amendment (wave 129): REVERSES wave-72 at phone only: the scorecard's 390 panel discloses the answers behind the frame's control

Chautauqua Review.dc.html's 1600 scorecard prints ABSTRACT and `Form answers` at rest with no disclosure; its 390 panel (:293-295) prints the abstract and then `<a min-height:44px>Read the full submission</a>` and NO answers section at all. DEC-889's wave-72 amendment retired "the 60-word clamp and the 'Read the full submission' disclosure ... entirely" — but it reasoned from the desktop reading column, and the app now prints the answers at every width and renders no disclosure anywhere, so at 390 the app diverges from the frame on both counts at once. The frames are not inconsistent with each other: at 1600 a reviewer has the room to read everything at rest, and at 390 the body cannot afford it, so the pack substitutes one 44px control. That is a width-conditioned reading, not a contradiction, and the retirement stands where it was ruled. Per DEC-099 an amendment that reverses says so: this one reverses the wave-72 clause at <=700px only and leaves desktop byte-identical.


## Amendment (wave 145): The scorecard's Overall value carries the 390 frame's own unscored word ("Not yet"), and the phone recusal label is the phone frame's

docs/design/Chautauqua Review.dc.html:968-969 (frame "Scorecard · a criterion unscored", 390) draws the Overall row's value as `Not yet` at `font-size:14px; font-weight:600; color:#565A4B`; :336 draws the recusal checkbox label as `Recuse me — conflict of interest`, where the DESKTOP frame at :259 draws `Recuse me from this one`. The app renders `formatScore(overallScore)` — an em dash — at every width, and `Recuse me from this one` at every width; grep for "Not yet" across app/src/pages/review returns nothing. RULED: the frame's per-width copy is BUILT, not blessed away — the precedent is already the campaign's (waves 129/130: `Wait`/`Waitlist`, `Place the other six`/`6 more unplaced` were built as width-conditioned copy). The mechanism is the established exactly-one-copy-visible idiom (both copies rendered, CSS reveals one, stated verbatim in src/routes/portal/edit.tsx's editDock comment) — never a `matchMedia` read, which is how cfp-steps-script.tsx breached the desktop freeze. An em dash is the results table's vocabulary for a RECUSED reviewer's cell (Review.dc.html:1113), so reusing it for "nobody has scored yet" collides two distinct facts on one glyph; the frame separates them and so does the app.
