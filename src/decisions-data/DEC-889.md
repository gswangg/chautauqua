# DEC-889: The scorecard is a rating surface: a clamped abstract with one disclosure, not a second copy of the submission detail

## Amendment (wave 129): REVERSES wave-72 at phone only: the scorecard's 390 panel discloses the answers behind the frame's control

Chautauqua Review.dc.html's 1600 scorecard prints ABSTRACT and `Form answers` at rest with no disclosure; its 390 panel (:293-295) prints the abstract and then `<a min-height:44px>Read the full submission</a>` and NO answers section at all. That is a width-conditioned reading, not a contradiction, and the retirement stands where it was ruled. Per DEC-099 an amendment that reverses says so: this one reverses the wave-72 clause at <=700px only and leaves desktop byte-identical.

RULED: the frame's per-width copy is BUILT, not blessed away — the precedent is already the campaign's (waves 129/130: `Wait`/`Waitlist`, `Place the other six`/`6 more unplaced` were built as width-conditioned copy).
