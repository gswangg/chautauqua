# DEC-991: A display-face line-height <= 1 is legal exactly where a v12 frame declares it

test/display-heading-line-height.scan.test.ts flags `.chq-phone-agenda-wordmark` and `.chq-phone-agenda-h1` in agenda.css for `line-height: 1`, but docs/design/Chautauqua Agenda.dc.html:129 and :133 both literally draw `line-height:1` on those two elements. The ruling exists to stop clipped descenders from a careless `line-height:0.9`, not to override the geometry authority. RULED: add a frame-cited exemption mechanism to the scan — a selector is exempt only when its rule carries a `docs/design/...:NNN` citation whose quoted literal itself contains `line-height:1`, so the exemption cannot be claimed without a receipt the frame-citation scan independently verifies. agenda.css's values are unchanged — moving the app off-frame to satisfy a lint would be the wrong repair.

## Amendment (wave 119): a new `overflow-exempt:` comment may be authored only in the commit that makes its rule flagged; otherwise file a finding

The overflow scan carries a staleness half that fails when an `overflow-exempt:` comment sits over a rule the scan does not flag. Under today's blind predicate 31 rules are unflagged, so a drain lane that reached for an exemption would turn main red for the opposite reason — the licence and its subject would come apart exactly as DEC-989's wave-95 finding describes. RULED: a drain lane neutralises (a real declaration in the terminal phone block) and never exempts. A lane that concludes a flagged rule is genuinely correct as drawn records it as a row in docs/design/audit/overflow-390-v12.md with the frame line that justifies it, and leaves the rule alone; the exemption is authored later, in the same commit as the predicate change that makes it non-stale. This keeps the exemption population one that the scan itself re-checks (DEC-991's own shape) instead of an allowlist that can only be verified by hand.
