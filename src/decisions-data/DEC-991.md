# DEC-991: A display-face line-height <= 1 is legal exactly where a v12 frame declares it

test/display-heading-line-height.scan.test.ts flags `.chq-phone-agenda-wordmark` and `.chq-phone-agenda-h1` in agenda.css for `line-height: 1`, but docs/design/Chautauqua Agenda.dc.html:129 and :133 both literally draw `line-height:1` on those two elements. The ruling exists to stop clipped descenders from a careless `line-height:0.9`, not to override the geometry authority. RULED: add a frame-cited exemption mechanism to the scan — a selector is exempt only when its rule carries a `docs/design/...:NNN` citation whose quoted literal itself contains `line-height:1`, so the exemption cannot be claimed without a receipt the frame-citation scan independently verifies. agenda.css's values are unchanged — moving the app off-frame to satisfy a lint would be the wrong repair.
