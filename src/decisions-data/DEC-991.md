# DEC-991: A display-face line-height <= 1 is legal exactly where a v12 frame declares it

test/display-heading-line-height.scan.test.ts flags `.chq-phone-agenda-wordmark` and `.chq-phone-agenda-h1` in agenda.css for `line-height: 1`, but docs/design/Chautauqua Agenda.dc.html:129 and :133 both literally draw `line-height:1` on those two elements. The ruling exists to stop clipped descenders from a careless `line-height:0.9`, not to override the geometry authority. RULED: add a frame-cited exemption mechanism to the scan — a selector is exempt only when its rule carries a `docs/design/...:NNN` citation whose quoted literal itself contains `line-height:1`, so the exemption cannot be claimed without a receipt the frame-citation scan independently verifies. agenda.css's values are unchanged — moving the app off-frame to satisfy a lint would be the wrong repair.

## Amendment (wave 119): a new `overflow-exempt:` comment may be authored only in the commit that makes its rule flagged; otherwise file a finding

The overflow scan carries a staleness half that fails when an `overflow-exempt:` comment sits over a rule the scan does not flag. Under today's blind predicate 31 rules are unflagged, so a drain lane that reached for an exemption would turn main red for the opposite reason — the licence and its subject would come apart exactly as DEC-989's wave-95 finding describes. RULED: a drain lane neutralises (a real declaration in the terminal phone block) and never exempts. A lane that concludes a flagged rule is genuinely correct as drawn records it as a row in docs/design/audit/overflow-390-v12.md with the frame line that justifies it, and leaves the rule alone; the exemption is authored later, in the same commit as the predicate change that makes it non-stale. This keeps the exemption population one that the scan itself re-checks (DEC-991's own shape) instead of an allowlist that can only be verified by hand.

## Amendment (wave 149): the 390 probe grades every media block that APPLIES at 390, not the subset whose condition reads max-width <= 700px

`app/src/phone-overflow-scan/rules.ts`'s `isPhoneCondition` returns true only for `max-width: <=700px`, and `cascade.ts`'s `isCandidateRule` admits a rule only when it is `isPhoneScoped || isTopLevel`. A rule inside `@media (max-width: 900px)` is NEITHER — nested, so not top-level; above 700, so not phone-scoped — and is dropped from the population entirely even though that block genuinely applies at 390. Measured against the population on main: TEN such blocks exist. The same predicate breaks the cascade in the other direction: `app/src/pages/agenda/agenda.css` collapses `.chq-agenda-layout` from `1fr 268px` to `1fr` inside a 900px block, and because that override is invisible the merge keeps the desktop tracks and manufactures a phantom offender. One narrow predicate is thus simultaneously a false-negative hole (a whole class of declarations ungraded) and a false-positive source (a real collapse unseen) — DEC-967's "an instrument's dead branch looks exactly like a clean pass", in both directions at once. RULED: candidacy and cascade merging use a new exported `appliesAt390(condition)` — true when the condition declares `max-width: X` with X >= PHONE_FRAME_WIDTH_PX and no `min-width: Y` with Y > PHONE_FRAME_WIDTH_PX. `isPhoneCondition` STAYS, unchanged and separately named, because a different contract owns it: DEC-385's one-terminal-phone-block rule is about where a phone override may be AUTHORED in a sheet, not about what a browser applies at a width. The two questions are not the same question and must not share one predicate. Measured: the widening surfaces zero new (A)/(B)/(C) offenders, so `OVERFLOW_OFFENDERS_CEILING` stays 0 and is asserted, never raised.

## Amendment (wave 156): supersession verdict on the colliding DEC-991s — the display-face line-height ban governs, and a frame's `line-height:1` declares BAND geometry, not a glyph metric

`src/decisions-data/DEC-991.md` and `decisions/DEC-991.md` carry entirely different subjects under one id and contradict each other on live phone typography. The former rules a display-face `line-height <= 1` LEGAL exactly where a v12 frame declares it, prescribes a frame-cited exemption mechanism for `test/display-heading-line-height.scan.test.ts`, and asserts "agenda.css's values are unchanged". The latter's wave-27 amendment rules `line-height: 1` on the display face BANNED OUTRIGHT and prescribes DELETION, citing a measured clip receipt (`docs/verification-log.md:3591`, `div.chq-forms-header-titles`/`h1` clip=3px). The tree implements the BAN: `test/display-heading-line-height.scan.test.ts`'s header opens "DEC-991 wave-27 amendment: LINE-HEIGHT 1 ON A DISPLAY FACE IS A CLIP", the exemption mechanism was never built, and `app/src/pages/agenda/agenda.css`'s `.chq-phone-agenda-wordmark`/`.chq-phone-agenda-h1` rules OMIT `line-height` while `docs/design/Chautauqua Agenda.dc.html:129`/`:133` literally draw `line-height:1`. So the app is deliberately off-frame on two declarations, justified by one DEC-991 and forbidden by the other, and no document records which one wins. RULED: the BAN governs, because it is the later ruling and the only one with a measured receipt behind it, and because a clipped descender is a fidelity defect the frame cannot have intended. The reconciliation is not a compromise value: a frame drawing `line-height:1` on a 26-27px display-face `<h1>` inside a fixed 390×844 card is declaring the HEAD BAND's tightness — geometry the app already satisfies through the band's own drawn `padding:14px 16px` and `gap:8px` — not a glyph metric, and `normal` is by definition the font's own line box and therefore cannot clip. `src/decisions-data/DEC-991.md`'s frame-cited exemption mechanism is retired as unbuilt and is not to be built. The two omitted declarations are recorded as a blessed, frame-cited deviation in `docs/design/DEVIATIONS.md` §3 so an audit reads a verdict instead of re-flagging a divergence.
