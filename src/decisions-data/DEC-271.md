# DEC-271: Amendment (wave 34): the recusal anti-join is a POPULATION of evaluation reads, not the two the last lens happened to name

## Amendment (wave 110): a recusal RETRACTS the score -- every downstream read anti-joins review_recusal, not just the queue

DESIGN-RULINGS.md:71 (row 18) fixes the caption the Attachments panel must print: `Only submitted, non-recused reviews are merged`, and app/src/pages/comms/ComposeWizard.tsx:1324 prints it. The resolver at src/server/repo/comms.ts filters on planId/round/submittedEvaluationCondition() and nothing else, and src/routes/review/recusals.ts places no evaluation check on recusing -- so a reviewer who scores, then declares a conflict, has their comment merged into an irreversible outbound email to the speaker and their score ranked into the plan's mean. RULED: recusal is a retraction, not merely a queue filter. The comms feedback merge and the ranked-results aggregate both anti-join review_recusal on (planId, submissionId, userId). One artefact may not say `non-recused` while another counts the same score -- a caption that the code contradicts is a lie with a send button attached.

## Amendment (wave 34): the recusal anti-join is a POPULATION of evaluation reads, not the two the last lens happened to name

DEC-271's wave-110 amendment ruled that "every downstream read anti-joins review_recusal, not just the queue" and only two reads obey it (src/server/repo/comms.ts:301-314; listEvaluationScoresForPlan, src/server/repo/review/evaluations.ts:88-105). Verified on main @ fe9742c0: there are 20 `.from(schema.evaluation)` sites across 7 files, and the ruling was never turned into a population — the classic "a ruling's second clause is the one that never lands" (DEC-613 wave-32). Two sites are misclassified today: src/server/repo/exports/evaluations.ts:222 (a recused reviewer's per-criterion scores and weightedScore still ship in the organiser CSV while the same score is excluded from the plan mean — DEC-873, an export IS a read side) and src/server/repo/review/evaluations.ts:418 listEvaluationsForSubmission (its own sibling on the SAME response, countAssignedReviewersForSubmission at src/server/repo/review/reviewers.ts:292-308, deletes recused pairs, so GET /api/v1/submissions/:id/evaluations can return items.length > assigned). RULED: every `.from(schema.evaluation)` read in src/server/repo/** is classified exactly once — SCORING READ (must anti-join), LIFECYCLE/FREEZE read (must NOT: a recusal may never un-freeze criteria or un-lock a plan), or DELETE/HISTORY read (must NOT: a retraction is not an erasure) — and the classification is pinned by a scan whose exemptions each carry a reason and whose failure prints the offender list, never a count (DEC-808). A hand-listed pair is not a population (DEC-180).
