## Amendment (wave 56): A dead exit predicate is a suite failure, not a planner discovery — the live registries get a real-tree self-test

The exit predicate has now thrown before printing a single campaign row in FOUR consecutive waves for four different reasons (w50 unregistered ratchet, w53 orphans/one-sided, w54 PACK_PROSE promise unpaid, w56 PACK_PADDING_UNMATCHED_CEILING registered in OUT_OF_CAMPAIGN_SCOPE with an 'is promoted once ...' sentence and no PARKED_FIDELITY_PROMISES row). Every time, a planner found it by hand. The reason is structural: test/exit-predicate-campaign.test.ts grades only synthetic fixtures (baseFixture/allGreenFixture) — nothing in the suite ever runs the REAL registries against the REAL tree, even though campaign.ts's own loadCampaignFiles header says 'anything (including tests) that needs the real corpus must call this function'. RULED: the campaign registries get a live-tree self-test that calls loadCampaignFiles(REPO_ROOT) and runs findCampaignCeilingCandidates -> assertCampaignPopulationTotal, assertEveryGradedCeilingIsGated and assertParkedPromisesAreHonestlyParked against the real registries, then buildCampaignReport, so minting a ratchet without registering it, or draining a parked ceiling without paying its promotion, reddens the scan tier in the same run rather than surviving until a planner types the command. The fixture test stays as-is (it grades the functions); the new test grades the REGISTRY.
