## Amendment (wave 132): A ceiling the exit predicate PRINTS is a declared constant, and the campaign is never DONE on declarations alone

The campaign's most expensive illusion, measured twice now: on 967ca667 `scripts/exit-predicate.ts` printed `UNRESOLVED_CITATION_CEILING … CURRENT 0` while the tree measured 3, and printed every other Tier-2 ceiling at 0 while seven fast-tier files were RED. `buildCampaignReport`/`ceilingRow` parse the ceiling CONSTANTS out of source and `campaignDone` is computed from nothing else, so a merge batch that lands without the suite leaves a GREEN predicate over a broken tree -- and the column header `CURRENT` actively invites the misreading, because CURRENT is what a measurement is called. RULED two ways, because neither alone is enough. The column is renamed DECLARED with a legend saying a declared ceiling is what the constant SAYS and never what the tree measures. And the verdict gains a suite-evidence input: `campaignDone` is false unless a suite-verified sha is supplied AND the product sha is an ancestor of it -- the same ancestry test `gradePredicate` already applies to the five DEC-069 slots, reused rather than re-invented. Absent evidence the report prints `tier-2: DECLARED ONLY -- NOT MEASURED` and exits non-zero. The exported functions stay pure; the evidence arrives as an argument, never as a shelled-out test run inside a grader.
