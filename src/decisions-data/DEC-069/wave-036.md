## Amendment (wave 36): the promotion rule for fidelity ceilings parked in OUT_OF_CAMPAIGN_SCOPE

Ten entries in `scripts/exit-predicate/campaign-scope.ts`'s `OUT_OF_CAMPAIGN_SCOPE` describe themselves as real v12 fidelity facts and each carries a promotion promise ("it measures fidelity now and is promoted once its measurement technique is proven complete enough to gate on"). Not one has ever been promoted, so the exit predicate can print a green campaign over a red frame-fidelity ledger — the same illusion DEC-069 was written to forbid. RULED: a parked fidelity ceiling becomes graded (moves into BOTH `CAMPAIGN_CONSTANT_NAMES` and `TIER2_CEILING_NAMES`) when (a) its own MEASURED count is 0 and (b) its instrument-completeness sibling is either 0 or has every row individually adjudicated in its audit doc. Clause (b) is what keeps promotion honest: `DESKTOP_FLOOR_DIVERGENCE_CEILING` reads 0 over a population where 131 of 247 rows are unresolved in buckets, and promoting that 0 would grade a 53%-blind instrument as proof of desktop conformance. A ceiling that fails a clause keeps its entry plus one principle-only sentence naming which clause failed.
