## Amendment (wave 101): the control-font register is a PER-RULE vocabulary contract: the global `button, input, select, textarea` reset does not discharge it, and a control-class rule that sets a type register declares `font-family: inherit` beside it

`app/src/control-font.scan.test.ts` is red at 36 offenders against a ceiling of 34; two of the three new offenders (`.chq-settings-drillrow-add`, `.chq-settings-drillrow-export-download`) arrived with commit f6b54547 in this train. It is worth stating plainly why the fix is not "the reset already covers it": `app/src/styles.css:208-213` does declare "button, input, select, textarea, optgroup { font-family: inherit }" (the selector list opens at :208), so most of these controls render correctly today. The contract exists anyway because the register is set LOCALLY — a rule that says `font-size: 13px; font-weight: 600` on a control class and says nothing about family is one refactor (a `<div role="button">`, a span-based chip, an SSR sheet with no reset) away from silently falling to the UA font, and the reader of that rule cannot tell which. RULING: every control-class rule that declares `font-size` or `font-weight` declares `font-family: inherit` in the same body, matching the idiom already used at styles.css:213/469/541/560/593/627/871 — not "var(--chq-font-ui)", which would pin the wrong family inside display-font contexts. The ceiling is a ratchet: a lane that fixes offenders lowers CEILING to the number its own branch measures, never to a round number and never upward.

## Amendment (wave 109): The cancelled-padding idiom is for borderless controls only; a bordered box needs a real gap

The user's 2026-08-19 ruling recorded in DEVIATIONS.md gives quiet-action families (`.chq-link-button`, `.chq-btn-tertiary`, the dense/roomy status chips) `padding-inline` cancelled by an equal-and-opposite negative `margin-inline`, so the focus ring and hit box gain room while no glyph moves. That ruling is scoped to controls that paint no box, and it states its own rider: wherever such a family renders as ADJACENT SIBLINGS, the rule owning their separation must add the cancelled padding back or the rendered gap closes. comms.css's terminal phone block applies the idiom to BORDERED subjects -- `.chq-comms-head-actions > .chq-pill` and `.chq-btn` under `-editor-actions`, `-history-head-actions`, `-preview-actions`, `-refusal-actions`, `-select-actions`, `-send-report-footer-actions`, `-template-actions` -- and settings.css does the same on the Public-pages rows and the Saved-embeds button group. On a bordered control the negative margin is not layout-neutral: each visible box overlaps its neighbour by 20px and bleeds 10px past the container. RULING: the idiom's subject must be borderless. A bordered or filled control keeps `min-height:44px; display:flex; align-items:center` (and its `flex`) and drops the padding/margin pair; its own border already gives the ring and the hit box their room.

## Amendment (wave 112): borderlessness is declared by the rule, not by membership in a hand-kept class list

comms-cancelled-padding.w5n.test.ts's isBorderlessSubject is four hard-coded class names, one of which (.chq-comms-send-report-all-history) was already admitted by hand after someone verified its declarations. .chq-comms-phone-recent-head-link declares `background:none; border:none` in its own rule and carries the cancelled-padding pair, so it is safe by the very criterion the ruling states -- and the scan fails it, because the list did not grow. A hand-listed population is not a population (DEC-180). RULED: the subject is borderless if it matches a named quiet-action family OR its own rule body declares `border: none` and `background: none` and no top-level rule for the same selector reintroduces a non-none border/background. The eight bordered-offender pins stay; the change ships with a synthetic positive and negative control so the derived predicate cannot pass vacuously.
