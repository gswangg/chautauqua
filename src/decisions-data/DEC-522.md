# DEC-522: A date-only field is a DAY LABEL, not an instant: displayed as its calendar day (read in UTC), expanded to an event-local instant at every HARD gate, compared raw only for soft signals

## Amendment (wave 7): a day label is EXPANDED before it is formatted, on every surface without exception, and its unit test is fed the inputs production actually mints

DEC-522 already rules that a date-only field is a day label (UTC midnight, minted not measured, enforced by isDayLabelMs at src/routes/api/validators.ts:82) that must be expanded to an event-local instant at every hard gate. The DISPLAY side was left implicit, and one renderer got it wrong: src/lib/event-time.ts:251 formatEventCloseDateLabel passes the raw UTC-midnight closeDate through toLocaleDateString({timeZone}) without dayLabelEndInstant, so the anonymous home hub (src/routes/root.tsx:131) advertises the CFP deadline ONE DAY EARLY for every event west of UTC — Date.UTC(2027,2,1) prints 'Sun 28 Feb' in America/Los_Angeles, the seed's own zone — while daysUntilCalendarDay on the very same line (:132) expands correctly and counts to the right day. Every other close-date renderer expands first (submit-views.tsx:519,728; sessions.tsx:107). RULING: expansion is part of the display contract, not only the gate contract — a formatter that takes a day label plus an IANA zone expands it before formatting. And the reason this survived: test/event-time.test.ts:243-249 PINS the wrong behaviour ('shifts the calendar day across a timezone boundary') using Date.UTC(2027,4,13,2,0,0), a sub-day instant isDayLabelMs would reject, while test/root.test.ts:549 uses a 20:00Z close that masks the shift. A day-label function's tests take day labels.
