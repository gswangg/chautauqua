# DEC-575: Amendment (wave 8): A file-level import refusal owns the dialog's identity; the denominator in its heading stays

Chautauqua Contacts.dc.html:770-800 draws the "the file will not do" state as its own dialog: H1 `Nothing was imported` (:777), a `<file> · N rows read` subtitle (:778) and its own Close (:780). ImportWizard.tsx keeps `stepTitle` at 'Match the columns' (step resolves to 2 whenever a header parsed and no plan/result exists — exactly this refusal's condition), so the refusal has no title of its own. Ruled: the dialog title reads `Nothing was imported` whenever a file-level refusal band is showing (`showSizeRefusal || showFileWarning`), which is the frame's own state; the existing subtitle and ModalFrame's Close already satisfy the rest. Ruled AGAINST the frame on one point: the heading keeps `N of M rows have no email address` — the frame's bare count (:785) drops the denominator DEC-575's wave-28 amendment deliberately added, and a receipt that cannot be checked against a total is worse copy, not better.
