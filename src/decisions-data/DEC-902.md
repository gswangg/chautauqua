## Amendment (wave 8): An additive control is not a divergence to fix; a control the frame draws and the app omits is

Four carried audit rows are all of one shape — the tree draws MORE than the frame: Content's status band adds a `Request changes` secondary and its title row adds `Edit title and abstract ›`/`Revision history ›` (content-agenda-desktop-v12.md :111); Overview keeps `Open` beside the two decisions; Content Files names each `LIBRARY_KIND` where the frame compresses to `Other · N`. Removing any of them is capability removal — and in the Files case the pack contradicts itself outright (desktop :308 draws the residual chip, the PHONE frame :344-346 draws none over the same 31-file library, the Speakers :57/:469 precedent). An extra control that adds real capability is blessed and its row is retired with a caption; a control the frame draws that the app cannot fire is the gap, and that is the only shape that buys a lane. RULED: there is no third disposition for a wording-only row. 'Measurement only, desktop is frozen' is not an adjudication and does not close a row. Scope guard: this ruling reaches WORDING and nothing else; a geometry, track-set or control-count residual keeps its existing disposition under DEC-902 waves 8/9 and DEC-902 wave-12m.
