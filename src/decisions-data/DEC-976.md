# DEC-976: The claim receipt becomes executable over test files: a citation inside a frame's extent must carry the literal it cites and an assertion beneath it

DEC-967's wave-86 amendment ruled that a claim is three things in one place -- a strict citation landing inside the frame's computed extent, the cited line's literal quoted verbatim beside it, and an assertion beneath pinning what that literal declares -- and then left the rule enforced by nothing but each worker's care. DEC-976's wave-23 amendment already makes the literal-quoting half executable for NON-test files; the test-file half is the half that matters now, because twenty-five-plus claims are about to land from ten lanes and the ledger counts a citation whether or not anything is asserted under it. The repo has already paid for exactly this gap twice: DEC-967's wave-57 amendment banned the tautological test, and its wave-62 amendment banned the line-number pin. RULED: frame-citation.scan.test.ts gains the test-file direction, reusing its OWN existing STRICT_RE and flatten (never a second copy of either -- a second copy of a vocabulary is a trap with a delay fuse); the frame extent is COMPUTED the way the ledger computes it, from the width:390px line up to the next font-size:19px label line, never listed; and the violations are held by a two-sided ratchet ceiling that may only be lowered. The scan does not edit other lanes' tests to make itself green -- the ceiling exists so the measurement can land honest.

## Amendment (wave 91): a frame citation in PRODUCTION source carries the same receipt as one in a test file — the backtick-quoted literal, verbatim at the cited line

frame-citation.scan.test.ts fails three ways on main. Eighteen citations in production source (forms.css, review.css ×6, scorecard.css, settings.css ×3, submissions.css ×3, portal/edit.tsx ×2, portal.css.ts, portal/tasks/views.tsx) carry no quoted literal at all, so nothing checks that the rule beneath them still matches the frame line they name. Separately, 87 test-file citations are unreceipted against a ceiling of 14 — which is the real state of the campaign: test/phone-frame-ledger.scan.test.ts reads 53 of 53 CLAIMED on main, but a claim is a citation, and the receipt scan says most of those citations assert nothing. The ledger is therefore not the exit predicate the campaign can rest on; the receipt scan is. This wave closes all eighteen production citations and the SSR test files' share; the remainder is named as the wave-10 backlog rather than papered over by raising the ceiling.
