# DEC-909: A form field states its own optionality and its own budget — one renderer, one grammar, no asterisks

## Amendment (wave 111): the live character counter is a LONG-TEXT affordance; maxlength is validation, the counter is presentation

`src/views/form-render.tsx:146` gates the counter on `field.kind === "text" || field.kind === "long_text"`, so a one-line Title, a name, a company all render `12 / 200` beside their label. The pack draws exactly THREE counters in its entire 158-frame extent, and all three are the same string on the same field: `412 / 1,200` on Abstract, at `docs/design/Chautauqua Public and Portal.dc.html:614` (Portal · Edit your session · 390), `:1037` (CFP 390) and `:1399` (Portal edit · desktop). No frame puts a counter on a short text field, a date or an email. Ruling: the counter renders for `long_text` only. `resolvedTextCap`'s `maxlength` stays on both kinds — that is the validation contract and it is untouched; this is the grammar half of DEC-909, and DEC-909's own words already say "a long-text field with a maximum carries a live counter". The inline sync script keeps its single code path.
